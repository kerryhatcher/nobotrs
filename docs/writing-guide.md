# Writing guide

Write for the reader's purpose. Use concrete nouns and direct verbs, and retain the writer's voice.

Treat lint findings as editing prompts. A phrase in the dictionary can be appropriate in context. Avoid replacing every match mechanically.

Preserve factual claims, names, numbers, quotations, URLs, citations, and code. Use a period, comma, or parentheses where an em dash distracts from the sentence. Vary sentence length when it improves clarity, without introducing errors or invented experience.

Heuristic detection does not establish authorship. Review the text itself and evaluate an edit by its meaning and usefulness.
