> Hyperspectral unmixing is a challenging problem in remote sensing.
> The goal is to estimate the abundances of the different materials
> present in a scene from a hyperspectral image. The problem is
> ill-posed and the solution is highly non-unique. In this paper, we
> propose a new method for hyperspectral unmixing based on sparse
> regression. The proposed method is based on a new sparse regression
> algorithm that uses spatially correlated supports. The proposed
> algorithm is shown to be more efficient than the state-of-the-art
> sparse regression algorithms. The proposed method is applied to
> hyperspectral unmixing and is shown to outperform the state-of-the-art
> methods.
