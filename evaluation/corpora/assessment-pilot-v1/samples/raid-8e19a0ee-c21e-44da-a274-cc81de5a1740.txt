In 2015, Maroney et al. introduced local quantum uncertainty (LQU) as an information theoretic complexity measure for two-qubit pure states and its generalization to pure multiqubit states in Fang et al. We show that the LQU condition $\mathcal{G}(\rho)\geq \frac{\lambda+\gamma}{\sqrt{\eta^2+(\delta+1)^2}}$ for separable qubit pairs remains valid under partial trace even if $\det\rho=0$ or $tr (\rho)=0,$ where $\xi, \gamma, \eta$. and $\sigma$ are parameters in Kraus representations $\sum_\alpha {\xi}_{\alpha}^{\mu}

3 \beta + 3 \epsilon + 4 x^2 } {3})$ holds for any $\tau< 1.$ In this case, we only need some conditions on parameters $(\epsilon, x,\theta)$ with fixed $\alpha> \left(\frac{\gamma-\kappa}{\beta}\right)_{+}.$ Our results shed light onto how to construct families of multipartite entangled states whose degree of global entanglement can be efficiently bounded by employing LQU. We also discuss possible application of our results to QKD systems.
