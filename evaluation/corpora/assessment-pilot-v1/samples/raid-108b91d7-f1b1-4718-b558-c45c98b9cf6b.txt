> We consider the problem of simultaneous inference for multivariate functional data with misaligned time series. The data are assumed to be generated by a multivariate functional linear model with a common time-varying mean function and a common time-varying covariance function. The time series are assumed to be misaligned in the sense that the time points of the observations are not necessarily the same for all the time series. We propose a new method for simultaneous inference for the mean and covariance functions. The method is based on a new estimator of the covariance function that is robust to misalignment. The method is also robust to outliers and missing data. We establish the asymptotic properties of the proposed estimators and the corresponding test statistics. We also provide a simulation study to illustrate the finite sample performance of the proposed method.
