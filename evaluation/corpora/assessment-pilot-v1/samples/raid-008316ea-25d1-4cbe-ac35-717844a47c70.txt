{\sl Abstract}: We investigate the third group $C^3 \subset H^3(X_\xi ; Z)$ of nonramified relative cocycles over a surface morphism $f^0 : X_\xi \rightarrow S,$ that appears in the description of the Galois group of special fibers of $\bar{B}^3(\Omega)$---the moduli space with geometric connection. We consider a surface $S = P_1P_2P'_2P'_1P$ such that any four divisors among five are linearly independent. For each subtorus $\Gamma$, we describe explicitly all 1-cocycles which restrict to a trivial class on $(\Sigma)^+\cap\Gamma$. This provides us with precise information about possible structures of elements from $\bar{H}(X_\xi)$, where $\bar{H}(X_\xi)$ means the image, under restriction, of points on the boundary components from $\partial B^3 (\Omega).$ On the other hand, for sufficiently general $P$ and $\tau$ with small enough $\mu_{\mathrm{max}}$, there exist exactly three torsion classes---each coming one form above described families.
