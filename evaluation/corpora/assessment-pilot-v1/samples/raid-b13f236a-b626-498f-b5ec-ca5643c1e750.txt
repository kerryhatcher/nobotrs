> Sparse models in high dimensions are often described by penalized optimization, where support selection and parameter estimation happen at once. This formulation requires solving a combinatorial, non-convex optimization problem involving large $p$-dimensional vectors (where p stands for number of variables), whose complexity explodes as soon as dimensionality increases. Recent approaches tackle these two problems separately in order to reduce the computational burden. Spatially dependent support models provide natural methods to select similar sets of coefficients through neighborhood systems while maintaining the interpretability of conventional sparse models. However, this kind of approach has not yet been explored systematically under more complex scenarios requiring collaborative processing across multiple models with different parameters. In particular, we study here how such models can be extended to regularize vector valued signals by selecting groups of elements supported on predefined subspaces instead for individual coordinates, leading thereby to what we call cross-subspace groupwise or coherent collateration sparsity. We present efficient algorithms which perform optimally when considering structured convex relaxation, that involve only separable (i.e., sparser) constraints over each variable dimension allowing us to efficiently solve them by standard off-the-shelf solvers.
