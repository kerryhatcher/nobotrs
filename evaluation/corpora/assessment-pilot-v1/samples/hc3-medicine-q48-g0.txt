It is generally not recommended to drink alcohol while taking any medication, including rabipur (also known as Rabavert or Rabies Vaccine). Alcohol can interfere with the way the medication is metabolized and can potentially increase the risk of side effects.

Rabipur is a vaccine that is given after exposure to rabies, to help prevent the development of the disease. It is important to follow the recommendations of your healthcare provider and complete the full course of the vaccine as prescribed. If you are experiencing side effects from the vaccine, it is important to discuss this with your healthcare provider. They may be able to recommend ways to manage the side effects or make adjustments to your treatment plan.
