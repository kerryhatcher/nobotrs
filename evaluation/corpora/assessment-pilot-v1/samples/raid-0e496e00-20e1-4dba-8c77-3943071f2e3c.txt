> We introduce the notion of a similar submodule of a module. We show that the similar submodules of a module form a lattice, and that the lattice of similar submodules of a module is isomorphic to the lattice of submodules of the module. We also show that the similar submodules of a module are precisely the submodules of the module that are isomorphic to a submodule of the module. We then introduce the notion of a coincidence site module, and show that the coincidence site modules of a module form a lattice, and that the lattice of coincidence site modules of a module is isomorphic to the lattice of submodules of the module. We also show that the coincidence site modules of a module are precisely the submodules of the module that are isomorphic to a submodule of the module.
