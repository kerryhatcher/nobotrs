> We present a method for active control of the splitting of resonant modes in coupled microcavities. The method is based on the
  use of a tunable microcavity as a spectral filter, which is coupled to a fixed microcavity. The tunable microcavity is driven by a
  laser, and the splitting of the resonant modes is controlled by tuning the laser frequency. We demonstrate the method by
  simulating the splitting of the resonant modes in a coupled microcavity system, and show that the splitting can be controlled
  with high precision. The method has potential applications in the control of quantum systems, such as quantum information
  processing and quantum metrology.
