The full book of outstanding bids and asks for a stock is typically referred to as the "order book." It is a list of all the buy and sell orders that have been placed for a particular stock, organized by price level.You can find the order book for a specific stock by going to a financial market data website or by using a trading platform. Some popular websites and platforms that provide real-time order book data include:Google FinanceYahoo FinanceBloomberg TerminalNASDAQNYSEE*TRADETD AmeritradeInteractive BrokersTo access the order book on these platforms, you will typically need to enter the ticker symbol for the stock you are interested in and then look for a tab or menu option that says something like "Order Book," "Level 2," or "Market Depth."Keep in mind that the order book is constantly changing as new orders are placed and existing orders are filled, so the information you see will not be static. It is also important to note that the order book only shows orders that have been placed through a particular exchange or platform, so it may not include all outstanding bids and asks for a particular stock.
