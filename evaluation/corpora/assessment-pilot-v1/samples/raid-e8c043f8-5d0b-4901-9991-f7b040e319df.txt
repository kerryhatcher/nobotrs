Abstract
Pure Multiple Signal Classification (MUSIC) algorithm finds all zeros in the covariance matrix spectrum of signal subspace and only then it cancels them out. However, this implies that some noise contribution may exist after cancellation. To eliminate the possible noise residue influence on angle estimation in direction of arrival (DoA) problems at low signal to noise ratio (SNR) circumstances;  Moussavi presented an enhanced filter which estimates both angles based on MUSIC algorithm with proper adjustment to zero crossing rate during beam formation procedure . It was claimed that using this method would lead to significant improvement as compared to conventional MUSIC where estimated values are not quite identical to true ones even if there is no error in SNR measurements during parameter evaluation stages or imperfect knowledge about system geometry. Thus in spite of its computational burden related to increased dimension of eigenvalue problem due to application windowing technique, we suggest here employing such algorithm rather than classical Pure-MUSIC when exact SNR value cannot be measured exactly especially under real time conditions without prior availability
