> Let $X$ be a smooth projective surface over a number field $K$.
> We prove that the third non-ramified cohomology group of the universal
> torsor over $X$ is finite.

The paper is by Jean-Louis Colliot-Th\'el\`ene, Pierre Colmez, and
Pierre-Henri Chatonnet.
