Abstract: We study mode splitting in strongly coupled microdisks by means theory and experiment, where the effects of an active component are included within the Hamiltonian model. Although it has been shown that coupling between the components can affect both splitting ratio and modal frequency as well as their stability, the details regarding exact analytical/numerically calculable formulas have not yet been given. In this work, we derive such equations in spectral phase space of the dispersive cavity. A nonlinear coupling strength parameter is introduced to connect this spectral diagram with its two constituting cavities with weakly or no linear interaction at all. While different type of experimental conditions lead to different shape of splitting ratio, the results from numerical simulation confirm our theoretical predictions very well
