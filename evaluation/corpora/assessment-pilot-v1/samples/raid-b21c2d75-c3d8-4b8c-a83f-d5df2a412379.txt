The asymptotic behavior has been studied by many mathematicians and physicists in order to analyze generating series of combinatorial objects whose enumeration can be related with physical models through partition type formulas. In this study, we investigate the case when such coefficients converge towards those of the general term for an infinite-dimensional family of combinatorial systems defined by several parameters (which corresponds to considering smoothing at each variables). Our previous work on this subject gives sufficient conditions to derive almost sharp results under general enough assumptions. We develop here our program aiming at reaching as fast improvement as possible within reachable cases when working near boundaries or special directions in parameter space [1]. We prove that the main difficulty we encountered lies in finding exact estimates of integrals involved which were not achieved satisfactorily up to now, mainly because it requires us also understanding well the problem numerically (this could be seen only roughly so far) but there seems no way except numerical one currently available to find more precise bounds which makes it difficult to reach satisfying results except in specific known examples where the proof remains unpublished and just given heuristically. On the other hand, if one accepts that this integral approximation stays reasonable enough and uses some variants of approximated expressions, faster speeds appear within a significant part of already considered cases. However, as they are quite technical and still lead neither optimal rates nor results for all interesting families, what would mean some non trivial difficulties remain open, these constitute strong evidence of validity of conjectures for most known combinatorial classes for which asymptotic result have been announced so far even though better techniques must then still exist yet undiscovered waiting to emerge elsewhere else.
