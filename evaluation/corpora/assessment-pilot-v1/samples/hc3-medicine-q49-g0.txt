Placing a magnet over a pacemaker can interfere with its function and potentially cause temporary or permanent damage. Pacemakers are designed to be sensitive to magnetic fields, as they use magnetic fields to transmit energy and information to and from the device. However, the strength and duration of the magnetic field can affect the pacemaker's function.

If you accidentally placed a strong magnet over your pacemaker for a few hours, it is important to seek medical attention as soon as possible. It is possible that the magnet could have disrupted the pacemaker's function and caused temporary or permanent damage. Only a trained medical professional can determine the extent of any potential damage and provide appropriate treatment.

It is generally recommended to avoid placing strong magnets near pacemakers, as they can interfere with the device's function. If you have a pacemaker, it is important to follow the instructions provided by your healthcare provider and seek medical attention if you have any concerns about your device or its function.
