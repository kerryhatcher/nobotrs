We construct a test statistic that detects if both slope and intercept parameters are nonzero versus no linear regression model of these data is suitable given that errors have an arbitrary joint distribution. We perform this test on high dimensional datasets when it can be difficult, or even impossible to obtain large sample sizes via cross-validation type methods. Our tests rely solely upon observations from the support region of the unknown density, avoiding estimation error. Furthermore our hypotheses testing method does not require any specification of the underlying sampling process nor its distribution other than being known multidimensional Gaussian at every set observation point within the support region.
