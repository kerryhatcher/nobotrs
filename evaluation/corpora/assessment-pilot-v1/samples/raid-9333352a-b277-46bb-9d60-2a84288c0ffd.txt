> Flux braiding is a technique for manipulating the topological properties of a quantum system. It is a key ingredient in the construction of topological quantum computers. In this paper, we review the experimental progress in flux braiding. We first introduce the basic concepts of topological quantum computation and flux braiding. We then review the experimental progress in flux braiding, including the experimental demonstration of flux braiding in superconducting circuits, the experimental demonstration of flux braiding in semiconductor quantum dots, and the experimental demonstration of flux braiding in cold atoms. We also discuss the challenges and future directions in flux braiding experiments.
