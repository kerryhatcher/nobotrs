We present the handbook for GREAT3, organised into three parts. Part I gives an overview and description of this new edition in the series (chapter~\ref{sec:Overview}), with references to the other editions we refer back to where appropriate. Chapter \ref{sec:Introduction} provides a brief introduction focusing on details particular to the third generation challenge test. It describes various aspects including scope and timeframe, target survey, background cosmological model, science goals and motivation behind the design choices as well as the intended participants. This chapter also briefly explains how to access additional resources required by the potential data challenges setters, such as software packages that allow them to prepare and validate the simulated datasets within their pipeline or system. For the purposes of reproducibility and benchmarking, all these resources were provided to challenge setters together with the official data release upon which they can work independently when preparing their tests. Chapter \ref{chap:LSS-and-HSTtestsetsimulations} presents our simulation methodology - first we provide summary descriptions of previous generations of the lensing accuracy test challenge - GREAT1 and GREAT2 \citep[see][]{great1handbook,great2handbook}, followed by some background information on weak gravitational lensing effects and the large synergy between CMB survey data and ground-based large-scale structure surveys at optical wavelengths, then finally a detailed breakdown of steps involved in preparation and production of multiple types of images used as reference inputs that may be made available to the candidates. The main focus there is placed on simulated imagery based on a realistic sky region containing the target survey area with its accompanying point spread functions and multiplicative field dependent noise patterns, while alternative approaches are discussed in Appendix B\footnote{To keep readability separate for a small number of appendices rather than merge several long ones into one very large file.} (p.\pageref {sec:altexposedfilenotes}). Following chapters explain a few important steps taken during post-processing of those input files such as application of specific instrumental configurations used by survey instruments.
