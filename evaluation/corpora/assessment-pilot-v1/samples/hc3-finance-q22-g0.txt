A full book of outstanding bids and asks for a stock can typically be found on a stock exchange or through a brokerage platform.On a stock exchange, the bids and asks for a particular stock are typically displayed on a platform called an "order book," which shows all of the outstanding orders for a particular security. The order book includes the price of each bid or ask and the quantity of shares being offered at that price.If you have an account with a brokerage, you may be able to view the full book of outstanding bids and asks for a stock through the brokerage's trading platform or website. Some brokerages also provide access to real-time streaming quotes, which can include information on the bids and asks for a stock as well as the most recent trade price and volume.It's important to note that the information on an order book or quote stream can change rapidly, as orders are placed, canceled, or filled in real-time. As such, the information may not always be up-to-date or complete.
