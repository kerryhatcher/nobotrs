If you have a credit card with a balance that exceeds your credit limit, you may be charged an over-limit fee by your credit card issuer. In addition to the over-limit fee, you may also be charged interest on any amount that you charge to your credit card that exceeds your credit limit. The interest rate on this type of balance is typically higher than the interest rate on your regular credit card balance.It's important to keep your credit card balance within your credit limit to avoid over-limit fees and high interest charges. If you are having trouble making payments on your credit card or if you are consistently carrying a balance that exceeds your credit limit, you may want to consider contacting your credit card issuer to discuss your options. They may be able to work with you to lower your interest rate or to develop a payment plan to help you pay off your balance.
