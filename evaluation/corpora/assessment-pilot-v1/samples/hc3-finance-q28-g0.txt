To calculate the movement of one share of a stock, you can use the following formula:Movement of one share = (Current price of the stock - Previous price of the stock) / Previous price of the stockFor example, if the current price of a stock is $50 and the previous price was $40, the movement of one share would be:Movement of one share = ($50 - $40) / $40Movement of one share = $10 / $40Movement of one share = 0.25, or 25%This means that the stock has increased in value by 25% since the previous price.
