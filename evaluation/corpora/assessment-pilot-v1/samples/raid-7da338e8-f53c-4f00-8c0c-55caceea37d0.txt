> We consider the problem of estimating a signal from a noisy observation in the presence of a dynamic input. The input is assumed to be a piecewise constant function of time, and the signal is assumed to be a piecewise constant function of the input. The observation is assumed to be a noisy version of the signal. We propose a soft-thresholding algorithm for estimating the signal from the observation. The algorithm is based on a dynamic programming approach. We show that the algorithm is optimal in the sense that it achieves the minimum mean square error. We also show that the algorithm is computationally efficient.
