If a business has no income, it generally will not be required to pay taxes on that income or file a tax return. However, there may be other taxes or fees that the business may be required to pay, such as self-employment taxes or unemployment taxes, depending on the nature of the business and how it is structured.It is also worth noting that even if a business has no income in a given year, it may still be required to file a tax return if it meets certain criteria, such as having more than $400 of self-employment income or receiving certain types of income, such as Social Security benefits or unemployment compensation.If you are unsure about whether your business is required to file a tax return or pay any taxes or fees, it is a good idea to consult with a tax professional or refer to the IRS website for more information.
