The authors in Part I presented an asymptotic approximation solution to minimize outage probability when a set of users are sharing resources under conditions that include interference management, user allocation, and cooperative processing. This part introduces several nonconvex optimization problems into this framework; specifically, it concerns the maximizing of rate coverage or success probabilities at different power constraints by minimizing an upperbound to the multi-cell downlink outage ratio based on a new expression. A linear approximation scheme and the subsequent algorithm called SCARP (Single Controllable Approach for Rate Probability) are provided over which near optimal performance can be delivered efficiently in order to reach outage targets. Several extensions also follow such as optimizations regarding the number of cells where cooperation is allowed or how much they should share their information among themselves. Finally, some numerical examples demonstrate the efficiency of these algorithms using results obtained with other approaches available in the literature related to cell planning in wireless networks.
