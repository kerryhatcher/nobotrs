Fungal infection: Fungal infections, such as athlete's foot, can cause skin peeling between the toes. These infections are often accompanied by itching and redness.

Allergic reaction: An allergic reaction to a product or substance can cause skin peeling on the hands and feet.

Eczema: Eczema is a common skin condition that causes dry, itchy, and inflamed skin. It can lead to skin peeling and cracking.
