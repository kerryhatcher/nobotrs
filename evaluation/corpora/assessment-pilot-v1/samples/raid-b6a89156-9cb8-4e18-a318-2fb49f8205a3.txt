> Superconducting transition temperatures and the critical fields Hc1, Hc2 have been measured for single-crystal Nb films grown on [0001] oriented BiOCl substrates by pulsed laser deposition (PLD). Measured Tc(95%) ~ 4 K and both coherence lengths Ls-Ld approximately = 35 nm are among highest values ever recorded for any pure-elemental superconductor, which may be due to the formation of highly defective layers at top and bottom side interfaces between film and underlying substrate during PLD growth leading to increased film thickness as well as induced electronic structure modifications that can enhance electron correlations necessary to drive strong pairing interactions near atomic limits associated with the BCS theory limit of dHc2/Tc=6. For these particular samples, Hc1 has also surprisingly been found to increase linearly as function of reducing Tc below Tc and can reach ~7 Tesla when supercurrent becomes fully depinned. This large hystereses effect remains unexplained.
