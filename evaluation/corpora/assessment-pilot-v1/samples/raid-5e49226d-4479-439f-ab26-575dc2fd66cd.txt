Abstract. Here we report that URhIn_{5} has the anisotropy with respect to magneto-resistivity, magnetisation, transverse hysteresis loops while possessing the intrinsic structural (CaCu_{3}-type) chiral symmetry; as such URhIn_{5} compound exhibits anomalous features where it seems not clear whether their origin lays in strong spin-orbit couplings or on the possible occurrence of time-reversal symmetry's violation [1]. Our specific heat measurements show URhIn$_{5}$ have non-compensated spins, although it may appear due to disorder, similar behaviour was observed recently by our group in YbCrGe$_3$, however, its ground state can be described with both - topological band theory and Kondo physics [2]. In this work, in continuation of our previous experimental data [2] for YbCrGe$_{3}$, we perform our experimental studies on single crystal of uranium rhodium indium alloy sample that exhibit antiferromagnetic/ferrimagnetic phase transition at 67K under zero applied field and temperature, which corresponds well with literature [3]; hence making comparison possible between YbCrGe$_{3}$ and URhIn$_{5}$. We obtain several different results using standard physical quantities - specific heat per unit mass ($c_p$), entropy ($S$) and Grüneisen parameter $\gamma$ to describe thermal behaviours up to certain limit [4].
