Yes, expense ratios on investment options in a 401(k) plan can include part of the overall 401(k) plan fees. Expense ratios are the fees that are charged by the investment options in a 401(k) plan for the services and products they provide. These fees can include the cost of managing and administering the investment option, as well as other expenses such as marketing and distribution costs. In addition to the expense ratios on the investment options, a 401(k) plan may also have other fees, such as plan administration fees, that are not included in the expense ratios. These fees are typically charged by the plan sponsor or the 401(k) plan administrator and may be used to cover the cost of operating and maintaining the 401(k) plan. It is important for 401(k) plan participants to understand all of the fees associated with their 401(k) plan, including both the expense ratios on the investment options and any other plan-level fees, as these fees can have a significant impact on the overall performance of their investments.
