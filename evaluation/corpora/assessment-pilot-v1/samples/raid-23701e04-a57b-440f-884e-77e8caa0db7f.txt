> We examine optimal soft threshold estimation in both continuous-and discrete-time linear estimation problems under a hard-input restriction. In both cases, we show that an affine function that varies smoothly over certain subintervals has minimax optimality as opposed to piecewise constant functions or indicator functions otherwise. This establishes continuity beyond these interfaces which contrasts traditional results where such solutions have discontinuities on or possibly within sets of positive measure at those regions. Our analysis provides the first rigorous framework for general input restrictions leading to robustness guarantees without compromising the achievable performance limits across various classes of dynamical models.
