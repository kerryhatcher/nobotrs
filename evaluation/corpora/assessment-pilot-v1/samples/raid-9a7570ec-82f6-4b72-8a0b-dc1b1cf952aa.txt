#### Abstract

> In this article we introduce similar submodules on rings as generalisations of central, almost normal or fully invariant subrings via a functorial approach. We determine some important classes thereof including that formed by symmetrically (left and right) normalising submodules which are called coincidence site modules. An arbitrary ring R can be extended with its coincidence sites so to obtain an extension E(R) whose center has a maximal commutative division ring over every quotient field in each localisation . One thus also obtains a canonical decomposition , where K_{U} is the group field of U=Frac(E/J) which corresponds to Z_U = Frac(Z/J), the residue class ring of zero divisors at U. These results yield new criteria for certain rings to extend as bimodules to their coincidence sites. For example they enable us to verify directly whether Dedekind domains or non-commutative semiprime algebras with units have an unspecified number of nontrivial factoriser ideals or two sided nil subideals respectively. They even show that these properties hold locally up to radicals so are preserved under going modulo prime ideals. They also afford suitable counter examples within Noetherian domains and Artinian von Neumann regular rings respectively. We deduce several consequences about other structural and homological aspects of finite module categories such as global dimensions, flatness and perfectness that turn out to depend essentially upon the category being noetherianly separated.
