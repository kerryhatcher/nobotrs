#### Abstract

We analyze the most up-to-date sample of quasars (QSOs) that have been observed spectropolarimetrically, providing the corresponding positions and Stokes vectors for each object in this set with sufficient accuracy (Gallerani et al., 2016;  Gallerani et al., 2017). We find an overabundance of aligned systems in which more distant objects exhibit lower polarizations while nearer ones show high ones - and we also detect some pairs exhibiting the opposite behavior. This peculiar pattern may represent a strong correlation between these two observational features that could be used to shed new light on physical processes taking place within the source structure or around it. Further analysis, based on additional catalogs and detailed spectrpolarlymeteric campaigns are needed o confirm our preliminary findings
