> Investment decisions based on fundamental analysis have not met with great success, primarily due to problems related to predicting events and market participant behavior as well as other factors (see e.g., Merton 1987). Nevertheless, attempts at prediction continue unabated; however, they mostly yield limited results. A few investment strategies that involve very simple trading rules can often provide satisfactory profits (Zhang et al., 2013). This study focuses largely on such an approach that offers several advantages over complex and time consuming models when choosing assets appropriate for long or mid term portfolio optimization - a method called the “Zipf’s Law” proposed by Kwiatkowska (2015). In particular, this study aims to analyze potential effectiveness of using it for short-term portfolio optimisation under conditions imposed here by using a risk management algorithm presented above (with slightly modified parameters) together with basic assumptions regarding execution model.
