> Online Learning and Dictionary Matching are two exciting recent advances in Machine Learning, which we discuss here as applied to audio classification using phonetic transcripts. Both methods learn directly rather than inferring information through a model or optimization problem. In this way they can potentially bypass some classical difficulties such as non-stationary data distributions and long time horizons. We use SVM's on feature vectors that embed small audio windows within an existing linear codebook of known words, in order to avoid explicit detection of unseen vocabulary during training. Our results indicate that learning a large dictionary online based upon kernel support vector machines may provide good predictive accuracy as well as fast convergence properties when employed jointly with the phoneme segmentation paradigm.
