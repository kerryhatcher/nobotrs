> We study the effect of the electrode density of states on the transport properties of single molecule junctions. We consider a pyridine-linked single molecule junction, where the molecule is connected to two metallic electrodes. The molecule is modeled as a tight-binding chain with a single orbital per site. The electrodes are modeled as semi-infinite chains with a single orbital per site. The electrode density of states is modeled as a semi-elliptic distribution. We find that the electrode density of states has a strong effect on the transport properties of the junction. In particular, we find that the electrode density of states can have a significant effect on the transmission of the junction. We also find that the electrode density of states can have a significant effect on the current-voltage characteristics of the junction.
