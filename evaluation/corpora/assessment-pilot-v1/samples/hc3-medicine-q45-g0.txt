Lymph nodes, also known as lymph glands, are small, bean-shaped organs that are part of the immune system. They are located throughout the body, including under the jaw and chin. Lymph nodes are responsible for filtering out bacteria, viruses, and other substances that may be harmful to the body.

There are many possible causes of swollen lymph nodes under the jaw and chin. Some common causes include:

Infections: Swelling in the lymph nodes under the jaw and chin can be caused by infections, such as a cold, flu, or sore throat.

Dental problems: Swelling in the lymph nodes under the jaw and chin can also be caused by dental problems, such as gum disease or an abscessed tooth.

Cancer: In rare cases, swelling in the lymph nodes under the jaw and chin can be a sign of cancer, such as lymphoma or leukemia.

Other conditions: Swelling in the lymph nodes under the jaw and chin can also be caused by other conditions, such as rheumatoid arthritis or HIV.

If you are concerned about the swollen lymph nodes under your jaw and chin, it is important to see a healthcare provider for a proper diagnosis and treatment. In the meantime, it is generally best to avoid touching or massaging the swollen lymph nodes, as this can sometimes make them more swollen or painful.
