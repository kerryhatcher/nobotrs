Modeling and Semigroup Formulation of Charge Or Current-Controlled Active Constrained Layer Beams:
Electrostatic, Quasi-Static And Fully Dynamic Assumptions by Ravi Kannan Venkataramani, Wenkai Tang, Jianfeng Cui on ArXiV Jan - 2019 DOI 10.48550/arXiv.1712.06301. v4 1 Jul 2021 This manuscript outlines the analysis of motion of an inhomogeneous three-layer viscoelastic active constrained layer (ACL) beam, composed of two homogeneous elastic thin layers bound together along their longitudinal sides with both interfacial and bulk layers bonded to an underlying rigid substrate plate at fixed locations. In order to understand how different mechanisms within an ACL-based micrograsping device might affect its performance during manipulation manoeuvres, we investigate how various modes of electrokinetic actuation could work with this type of actuator topology. We first consider the electrostatically driven case, where a potential boundary condition must be imposed across each interface due to the absence of any charge injection capability. Next, assuming that one end of the overall structure is electrically grounded and charge can be injected into an embedded electrode located at the opposite interface via the addition of another pair of plates forming a capacitor along the length of the system under investigation—essentially as described previously by other researchers here , here .
