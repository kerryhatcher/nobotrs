This paper aims to develop a robust method for medical image segmentation, and focuses on the systematic exploration of machine learning techniques, especially deep learning, to improve the accuracy and efficiency of image segmentation. This study covers all the modes of imaging, such as X-ray, X-ray, CT, MRI, etc. , and presents a comprehensive comparative analysis of the performance of deep learning methods compared with existing methods. In addition, the potential challenges and future development paths of medical image segmentation are discussed. This study has significant clinical significance for various medical applications, such as more accurate diagnosis, treatment and monitoring, and the theoretical study of human anatomy.
