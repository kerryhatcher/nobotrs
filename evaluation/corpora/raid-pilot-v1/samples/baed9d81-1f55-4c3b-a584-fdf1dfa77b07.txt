The Carpet People is a captivating and imaginative tale of a young girl named Emily who discovers a hidden world beneath her family's carpet flooring. In this enchanting land, she meets creatures known as 'Carpet People', beings that have lived in harmony with humans for generations but are now threatened by the advancing technology of the modern world. Emily embarks on an epic journey to save these unique beings, navigate treacherous obstacles, uncover secrets about her own past, and learn valuable lessons about empathy, courage, and the importance of preserving our connection to nature. Along the way, she forms incredible friendships and confronts adversaries willing to do anything to protect their power. With richly drawn characters and a beautifully woven narrative, The Carpet People transports readers into a realm where nothing is what it seems and takes them on an adventure they will never forget.
