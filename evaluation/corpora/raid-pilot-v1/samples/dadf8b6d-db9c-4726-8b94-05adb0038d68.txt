The plot of this thriller is based on a retired CIA agent, Jack Turner, whose past comes back to him. It begins with his living a quiet life in a small town in Montana, far from his former life. But then he receives a coded message from an old colleague, warning him of a threat. Then a bomb explodes in his house, killing his wife and leaving him badly wounded. Consumed with grief and vengeance, he starts on a dangerous mission to track down the killers. As he investigates, he discovers more and more of the truth about his past, and about the truth about “Deathtrap” - a secret operation run by elements in the CIA. Jack finds himself fighting against the very organization that he used to serve. The climax of the book is a climactic showdown between Jack and the head of “Deathtrap” in a secret underground facility. Jack manages to foil the terrorist attack, but not without a heavy price. The novel ends with Jack disappearing into the shadows, leaving his old life behind him forever. It is a tale of vengeance, betrayal and redemption. It is a novel about the dark underbelly of espionage and the lengths to which one man will go to achieve his goal.
