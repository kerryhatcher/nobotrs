"The Carpet People" is a fantasy novel about a microscopic society in the threads of an ordinary carpet. The story is told mainly from the point of view of two central characters, Snibril and Krick, brothers from the Murung tribe. Their home has been destroyed by the ferocious and mysterious Fray, and Snibril, the brave one, and Krick, the learned one, are going to find a new home. During their journey they meet different tribes such as the Dumii, the Mouls, the Pones, all with their own customs, habits and laws of life. At the same time, the over-world is seen, with the human owners of the carpet, who are unaware of the existence of the microcosmic world under their feet. Each step or drop of dust on the carpet means a devastating earthquake or some other cataclysm for the carpet inhabitants, and thus new narrative paths are discovered. "The Carpet People" is a mixture of mythology, political intrigue and the environment of the carpet kingdom, and of human life on earth. The struggles against the Fray and their own differences, the alliances that are made, all add up to the strength of the people. The plot is driven by cooperation, survival and a deep understanding of the environment, and the final battle against the ferocious Fray is a highlight of the book. In the end the novel elegantly brings together the two parallel worlds - the carpet world and the human household - in an unexpected and thought-provoking way. Rich in imagination and allegory, the book is a fantastic plunge into the microcosm under the human's feet.
