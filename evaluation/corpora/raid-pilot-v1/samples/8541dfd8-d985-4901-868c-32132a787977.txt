Medical image segmentation is a crucial task in the field of healthcare, with applications ranging from disease diagnosis to treatment planning. However, the complexity and variability of medical images make it challenging to develop accurate and robust segmentation models. In this paper, we propose a novel self-supervised clustering network for medical image segmentation. Our approach leverages spatial information to guide the clustering process, allowing the model to learn meaningful representations of the image data. We evaluate our method on several benchmark datasets and demonstrate its effectiveness in achieving state-of-the-art performance on various segmentation tasks. Our work provides a valuable contribution to the field of medical image segmentation, offering a new approach to tackle the challenges posed by this important problem.
