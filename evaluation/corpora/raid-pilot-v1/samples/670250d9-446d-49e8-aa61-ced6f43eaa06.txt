Aston Villa have confirmed that Jack O'Leary has signed a two-year deal with the club, keeping him firmly in place for at least another season.

The 21-year-old goalkeeper was an important figure of the team last season as they were relegated to the Championship. He made 17 appearances and kept seven clean sheets amidst tough times for the Birmingham City-based side.

However, his impressive performances caught the attention of various Premier League clubs during the summer transfer window, leading to intense speculation about a possible move away from Villa Park. Yet, he was determined to stay put and fight for his place under head coach Steve Bruce.

This decision will come much like a relief to assistant manager Luke Shaw who had praised O'Leary early on in preseason training, stating that he is 'one of our own'. Villa faithful will also be pleased, having watched promising youngsters make their way out of the club only recently.

With this extension now inked, O'Leary becomes one of Villa's longest serving players along with James Chester and Conor Hourihane - both of whom are currently out injured. The goalkeeper has shown maturity beyond his years in handling such situations and Villa fans will definitely look forward to seeing what promises to be yet another exciting campaign at Villa Park.
