Liverpool manager Rafael Benitez has defended goalkeeper Jerzy Dudek following his mistake in the Champions League final against AC Milan. 

Dudek, who was instrumental in Liverpool's remarkable comeback from 3-0 down to win the trophy on penalties, made a crucial error in the first half of the match. The Polish shot-stopper failed to hold on to a long-range effort from Milan's Clarence Seedorf, allowing Filippo Inzaghi to pounce on the rebound and give the Italian side the lead.

However, Benitez was quick to deflect blame from Dudek, instead praising his overall performance and highlighting the importance of his saves in the penalty shootout.

"Jerzy made a mistake, but he also made some incredible saves throughout the game," Benitez said. "He was crucial in keeping us in the match and ultimately helping us win the trophy. It's important to remember that football is a team sport, and mistakes happen. We win and lose as a team."

Benitez's comments come as a show of support for Dudek, who has faced criticism for his error. The goalkeeper himself has admitted his mistake but remains proud of his overall contribution to Liverpool's historic victory.

"I take responsibility for the mistake, but I also take pride in the saves I made that helped us win the Champions League," Dudek said. "Football is a game of ups and downs, and I am grateful for the support of my teammates and the fans. We achieved something incredible together, and that's what matters."

Dudek's mistake did not prove costly in the end, as Liverpool fought back to level the score through goals from Steven Gerrard, Vladimir Smicer, and Xabi Alonso. The match eventually went to penalties, where Dudek's heroics came to the fore. The goalkeeper famously mimicked the wobbly legs of Milan's penalty takers, which seemed to unsettle them and ultimately led to Liverpool's victory.

Benitez's decision to defend Dudek is a testament to the unity and camaraderie within the Liverpool squad. The manager's support for his players, even in the face of mistakes, has been a hallmark of his tenure at the club.

As Liverpool celebrate the 16th anniversary of their famous Champions League triumph, the focus remains on the incredible comeback and the collective effort of the team. Dudek's mistake may have been a momentary lapse, but his overall contribution to the victory cannot be overlooked.
