This paper presents a novel approach for training 3D seismic fault segmentation models using a small number of 2D slice labels. The proposed method utilizes an attention-based convolutional neural network (CNN) to learn the spatial relationships between 2D slice labels and the corresponding 3D seismic data. The attention mechanism allows the model to focus on the most relevant 2D slices during training, reducing the number of required labels and improving the accuracy of the resulting 3D fault segmentation model. The effectiveness of the proposed method is demonstrated on a synthetic seismic dataset, where the model achieves state-of-the-art performance with only a small number of 2D slice labels.
