The BBC has announced that former England footballer Michael Wright-Phillips will be joining the BBC Sport team as a presenter.

Wright-Phillips, who played for Manchester City and Chelsea, will be starting on the right side of the BBC Sport team, alongside presenters Gary Lineker, Alan Shearer, and Ian Wright.

"I'm really excited to be joining the BBC Sport team," said Wright-Phillips. "I've always been a big fan of the BBC and I'm looking forward to being a part of such a talented and experienced team."

Wright-Phillips' first appearance on the BBC Sport team will be on Saturday, August 20th, as they cover the Premier League match between Manchester United and Liverpool.

"We're thrilled to have Michael on board," said BBC Sport editor, Ed Leigh. "He brings a wealth of experience and knowledge to the team, and we're confident he'll be a valuable addition to our coverage."

Wright-Phillips retired from professional football in 2016 and has since been working as a pundit for various TV channels.
