Into the sordid underworld of New York, where private detective Danny Markham is plunged into a tangle of treachery, murder and conspiracy, when a mysterious woman named Isabella hires him to protect her from unnamed threats. It is not long before he is drawn into a world so dangerous and so ruthless that it makes him question his own morals. From secret societies and concealed enemies, to a trail of blood leading him into the city's darkest corners, he soon realizes that Isabella is something more than a rescued damsel. And when his best friend and business associate, Billy, is found dead under sinister circumstances, the stakes rise further, and he is forced to walk through a labyrinth of lies, while also trying to separate friend from foe in the face of his growing feelings for Isabella. But with every body that turns up, he feels as though his confidences are being manipulated, and the demons of the city are relentlessly driving him to the brink of madness. Antagonized by an unknown enemy, haunted by the memory of his friend's death, he is at breaking point, when he finally discovers that his mentor was the source of his troubles. A book that is a thrilling roller coaster of suspense and intrigue, Deathtrap is an action-packed psychological thriller that finally culminates in a battle against the very source of corruption itself, in a final confrontation that will determine not only the future of Danny Markham, but also the future of New York.
