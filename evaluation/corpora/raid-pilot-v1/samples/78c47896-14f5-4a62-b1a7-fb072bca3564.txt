This paper presents a semi-supervised learning method for left atrium segmentation with mutual consistency training (MCT). Accurate and efficient segmentation of the left atrium from 3D cardiac MR images is important in image analysis, but is often hindered by a lack of labeled data and high intra-class variability. In this paper, we use both labeled and unlabeled data to train a deep neural network. MCT makes the predictions from the unlabeled data with the same label under different views consistent. Extensive experiments on the public left atrium dataset show that our method outperforms the state-of-the-art semi-supervised learning methods. Its accuracy has been significantly improved, and it can be used as a basis for automatic left atrium segmentation from MR images, and can provide important information for further research into heart disease.
