The prestigious tennis tournament in San José was a field of easy winners. Among the top tennis players of the world, number three, Rafael Nadal, awed his opponent, the young Australian Alex de Minaur, and won in straight sets. He has entered the quarter-finals, where he is expected to face American Taylor Fritz. The mighty Swiss Roger Federer mastered the stubborn Tomas Berdych with a 7-5, 6-3 victory, and the audience was very excited to see his usual magic, which was expected to lead him to the finals. Both players were cheerful after their victory and are waiting for their demanding challenge. Nadal said: I’m preparing for a great challenge. The defending champion, Novak Djokovic, also won without any difficulty, destroying his opponent, the Spaniard Roberto Bautista Agut, 6-2, 6-3. He was very competitive and he was demonstrating his style of play which made him a champion last year. The British first number, Andy Murray, also continued the success of the top players by defeating his American rival, John Isner, 7-6, 7-5. He was very solid, he broke Isner's serve when he needed it and he played well with his usual endurance. On the women's side, both Serena Williams and Naomi Osaka easily won. Serena beat the Spaniard Carla Suarez Navarro 6-2, 6-4, while Osaka overcame the Latvian Anastasija Sevastova 6-3, 6-2. The tournament is now in the middle of its course, and it is more and more exciting to watch the best tennis players in the world. The question is, who will win the San José Open trophy? It is a good question to ask the tennis fans around the world.
