As we walk through the halls, dressed in gowns
With pride and hope in our hearts that wear no frowns,
We know this is just the beginning of a new chapter,
One filled with endless possibilities and opportunities for laughter. 

For today marks the day we leave these walls behind,
And step into the world with minds open wide,
Ready to learn from every experience,
Every success, every stumble, all without resistance. 

The future stretches out before us like an ocean,
Full of mysteries yet unknown and unexplored,
But we're ready to dive headfirst,
To face what lies ahead with courage unsurpassed.

This eternal graduation never ends, 
As each day brings forth new lessons to extend,
So let’s embrace it with arms open tight,
Ready to soar into the unknown, take flight!
