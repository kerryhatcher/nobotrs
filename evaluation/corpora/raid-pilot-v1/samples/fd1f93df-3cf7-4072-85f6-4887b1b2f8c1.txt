This paper studies large image segmentation with the help of distributed clustering. The proposed system has the ability to efficiently process high-dimensional data with the help of improved clustering methods, such as k-means and DBSCAN. The experiments are based on real-world images to test the performance and efficiency of the proposed clustering methods. These experiments show that the proposed method has the advantages of combining distributed computing and improved clustering methods in terms of speed, scalability, and accuracy. In addition, these improved clustering methods are tested for the resilience of dealing with the problems of high-dimensional data and noise. These results give a better understanding of the possibilities of future research in this area.
