World No. 1 Novak Djokovic and two-time champion Andy Murray sailed through their first round matches at the ATP San Jose event on Tuesday, with both players showcasing their prowess on court. 

Djokovic, who is looking to continue his dominant form this season, overcame Slovakia's Lukas Lacko in straight sets, winning 6-3, 7-5. The top seed displayed his usual aggressive style of play, consistently firing big serves and powerful groundstrokes to wear down his opponent. Despite a brief wobble in the second set, Djokovic quickly regained control and closed out the match in just over an hour.

Meanwhile, Murray, who is on a quest to regain his form after a series of injuries, defeated American wildcard entry Noah Rubin 6-3, 6-4. The British player, who has had a strong start to the new season, showed glimpses of his former brilliance with his signature defensive skills and precision shots. Although Rubin put up a valiant fight, Murray's experience and superior shot-making abilities ultimately proved too much for the young American.

In other action, fourth seed Roberto Bautista Agut of Spain, advanced to the next round after defeating Germany's Dominik Koepfer 6-1, 6-4. Agut, who has consistently performed well on hard courts, dominated the match from start to finish, showcasing his powerful groundstrokes and impressive court coverage.

Additionally, American Taylor Fritz advanced in an epic three-set battle against compatriot Reilly Opelka, triumphing 6-7(6), 7-6(4), 6-3. The match was a battle of the big servers, with both players launching thunderous serves that often exceeded 140 mph. Ultimately, it was Fritz's mental strength and ability to stay composed during crucial moments that allowed him to emerge victorious.

As the tournament progresses, fans can anticipate more thrilling encounters from these big guns of the tennis world. With Djokovic and Murray showing their class in the early stages, it promises to be an exciting competition filled with exceptional tennis.
