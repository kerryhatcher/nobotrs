The Eye of the Giant is an extraordinary, fantastic novel which plunges the reader into the magical realm of giants, and in which reality merges with myth. The novel is centred on a young and tenacious archaeologist from a small village in Ireland, which is inundated by the stories of legendary giants. The scientist is studying the possibility of the existence of an artefact which was lost in a field at the bottom of a deep pit. There she discovers the Eye of the Giant, a mystical stone which is supposed to hold the spirit of the most powerful giant, Goliathan. In the legends which are handed down in her village, this giant is said to be one of the defenders of earth against a parallel devil world. And the legend says that when earth is threatened, the spirit of Goliathan will choose a worthy human being to avenge her plight. The moment Abigail touches the stone, she is chosen and begins a journey to save her world from the threat of an ever-increasing evil. She is going to have to learn to use the powers of the stone and master them while overcoming her disbelief and her innate fear. The road she takes is filled with supernatural barriers, powerful enemies, and above all, a confrontation with an ancient enemy from the parallel world, who has risen to attack the earth. And as she travels from continent to continent, encounters people who become her companions, and battles with herself, the reader is plunged into an astonishing world, revealing diverse cultures, beautiful landscapes and the most dramatic action scenes. Little by little, as Abigail begins to understand the power of the stone, she gradually finds the strength within herself and realizes that the stone does not only hold the physical power of the giant but also his courage, his wisdom and his indomitable spirit. The character of the chosen one changes and gradually overcomes her own weakness, making her a character that is able to inspire readers to believe in themselves. The final battle, in which she has to make the ultimate sacrifice to save the earth, is full of suspense and raises the reader to a higher level of courage, tenacity and conviction. The epic struggle of a chosen one to accept his destiny, fight the demons and regain the balance in her ordinary life, is a true picture of courage, resistance and transformation. The final end of the novel leaves the reader with the feeling that reality and myth may have merged and with the need to read the next part of this saga.
