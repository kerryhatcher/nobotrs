Semi-supervised left atrium segmentation is an important task in medical image analysis. The ability to accurately identify and isolate specific regions from cardiac images can help improve clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, and prognosis. In this study, we propose a new approach for semi-supervised left atrium segmentation using mutual consistency training (MCT). This method involves using supervisely trained networks to generate pseudo labels for unlabeled data, which are then used to train additional MCT models. We compare our approach to other state of the art methods on three publicly available datasets and show significant improvements in performance using our proposed technique. Our results demonstrate that our method can effectively and efficiently perform left atrium segmentation even when limited amounts of labeling information is available, making it valuable for applications where accurate left atrium identification is critical.
