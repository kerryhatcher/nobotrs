‘Legacy’ is a powerful tale of family, power, and the weight of inheritance. The story is of Elizabeth Hartley, a successful lawyer in New York, who is suddenly thrust into the world of high society and family politics when she inherits a huge English estate from her estranged grandfather. Her life takes a dramatic turn when she leaves New York and goes to settle her grandfather’s affairs in the English countryside. She is met with hostility and suspicion from the locals and distant relations who are angry at her unexpected inheritance. As she delves deeper into her grandfather’s past, she discovers dark family secrets that threaten to shatter her view of her family’s history. The reader is taken on a journey with Elizabeth as she struggles to reconcile her modern independent life with the weight of tradition. She is thrown into the world of politics and high society where she must make decisions that will have profound consequences for her future. Along the way, she finds unexpected allies, enemies and lovers, each of whom test her resolve in different ways. ‘Legacy’ is a story of self-discovery, the power of resistance and the power of choice. It is a story of family and the weight of tradition, wealth and the importance of being true to oneself in the face of powerful forces that would try to impose themselves on you. As Elizabeth attempts to find her way through her new life, she discovers that legacy is not just about what one inherits, but what one creates for oneself.
