Semi-supervised learning is a powerful approach that can improve medical image segmentation performance while reducing the amount of labeled data required. In this study, we propose a semi-supervised contrastive learning framework that enhances label efficiency by jointly training both pixel and feature distribution across multiple categories for medical image segmentation tasks. Specifically, our method leverages adversarial and mutual information losses to align semantic representations between unlabeled images and their corresponding ground truths in an iterative process. Using this optimization strategy, we demonstrate state-of-the-art results on several benchmark datasets with significantly fewer labeled examples than prior methods. Our approach represents a significant advancement towards building robust and efficient deep learning models for medical image segmentation tasks while minimizing human intervention.
