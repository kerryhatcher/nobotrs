A double injury blow has struck Wales as two major sporting events, the Six Nations rugby union tournament and the UEFA European Championship football match against Belgium, have been postponed due to an outbreak of Covid-19 among players and staff.

The decision was made by both the Welsh Rugby Union (WRU) and the Football Association of Wales (FAW), who have confirmed positive cases within their respective squads and teams. The WRU announced yesterday that all matches in the Guinness Pro 14 competition involving Scarlets and Ospreys will also be postponed until further notice.

This news comes after Scotland and Ireland were forced to withdraw from the upcoming Six Nations games earlier this week following the discovery of positive coronavirus cases within their camps. England had already been granted permission to play the opening weekend's game at home against France, without any fans present.

Meanwhile, Wales manager Ryan Giggs expressed his disappointment over the cancellation of the friendly with Belgium but said he understood the importance of keeping everyone safe amidst the pandemic. He also revealed plans are underway for the team to train at their base in Caerleon next week before potentially resuming international duty against Denmark later this month.

In response to the Covid outbreak affecting the squad, the FAW stressed that they will follow all necessary protocols set out by public health authorities to ensure player safety while preventing any further spread of the virus. They also thanked all those involved for coopering fully with these measures thus far.
