In a small, impoverished village tucked away in the shadow of powerful mountains, residents struggle daily to make ends meet and find joy in life's simple pleasures. Despite their hardships, they hold onto an annual tradition that has been passed down through generations – The Feast of the Goat.

This year's feast is no ordinary celebration as rumors have spread about an unidentified beast lurking deep within the forest. villagers fear it may disrupt their sacred event, but instead, they decide to embrace it, offering up more sacrifices than ever before in hopes of appeasing its wrath.

As days pass leading up to the feast, tensions rise among the townsfolk with some demanding more offerings while others question why they needlessly risk angering the monster. Meanwhile, an enigmatic stranger arrives claiming knowledge of the goat god that lives beyond the treacherous forest border. He offers to lead them into the heart of darkness just weeks before the celebrated festival.

Intrigue swells as the townspeople debate his motives and his connection to the looming danger threatening their way of life. Some see him as a savior, sent to usher upon a new era of prosperity, while others view him suspiciously, fearful he seeks control over their rituals or worse yet, manipulate the entire community towards personal gain. As secrets unravel and doubts mount, the people of the outlying farms become increasingly distrustful of one another.

On the night of the eve of the feast, torches blaze bright across the sky, casting eerie shadows on the cobblestone streets. Villagers decked in vibrant costumes parade from house-to-house carrying gifts fit for royalty: casks of wine, baskets overflowing with fruits, golden coins sewn onto cloaks – all meant to honor their beloved tradition.

Just when the festivities are reaching their peak, news arrives that shocks everyone – evidence points towards one particular member of the community causing the disturbance which led the beast’s arrival.
