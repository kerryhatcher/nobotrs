In the bustling city of New York, a young woman named Rachel disappears without a trace. The only clue to her whereabouts is a pair of distinctive boots that she was last seen wearing. As her friends and family begin to search for her, they are haunted by memories of their own about the mysterious girl who always seemed just beyond their grasp.
Told through the eyes of a diverse group of characters, Last Seen Wearing weaves together the lives of various individuals connected to Rachel's disappearance - from her childhood friend struggling with addiction to the wealthy businessman who she met at her job. Each character grapples with the guilt and frustration over losing sight of Rachel in their own way.
As the investigation into her disappearance progresses, tensions rise as the police become increasingly frustrated with the lack of leads. But then, on the anniversary of her disappearance, an unexpected breakthrough is made when a witness finally comes forward.
With new information in hand, the race is on to find Rachel before it's too late. Along the way, the cast of characters must confront their own fears and secrets. Will they be able to unravel the mystery before it's too late? Or will they continue to question what really happened to Rachel, the last person they were ever seen with?
