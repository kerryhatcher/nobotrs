This paper presents a novel way to semantically segment images with two state-of-the-art models, styleGAN and clip. The proposed method is unsupervised, thus avoiding the need for large amounts of annotated data. It shows that styleGAN can produce high-quality, diverse images and CLIP can interpret and understand images in a semantic context. The combination of these two models can achieve effective semantic segmentation. Experimental results show that our method outperforms other unsupervised image segmentation methods in terms of accuracy and speed. This study opens up a new way for image analysis and understanding, which has broad potential applications in fields such as computer vision, robotics and automatic driving.
