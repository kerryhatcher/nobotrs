Combo Loss is a new loss function that can be used to address the imbalance problem of inputs and outputs in multi-organ segmentation. The proposed method combines the advantages of two popular loss functions, namely dice loss and cross entropy loss, to improve the performance of segmentation. In this paper, Combo Loss is evaluated on several benchmark datasets, and its superiority over conventional loss functions is shown. The proposed method can better resolve the imbalance problem, resulting in more accurate and robust multi-organ segmentation. This study offers a new perspective on the design of loss functions for medical image segmentation tasks, and the proposed Combo Loss has the potential to be widely used in the field of medical image analysis.
