This paper presents a novel and robust visual tracking method based on cell-level differential neural architecture search (NAS). To search the search space for effective architectures, the search is based on differentiable NAS. To discover effective architectures, cells are automatically searched by differential NAS. It outperforms the state-of-the-art visual tracking methods, both traditional and NAS-based, on a number of public test data. The results show that the cell-level search strategy and the auxiliary tasks can effectively improve the accuracy and robustness of the tracker.
