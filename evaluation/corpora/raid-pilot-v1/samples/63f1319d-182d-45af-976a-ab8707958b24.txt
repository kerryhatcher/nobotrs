In a distant future, humanity has colonized several planets and established settlements on extraterrestrial bodies. The exploration and colonization efforts have led to both incredible technological advancements and dangerous encounters with alien life forms. As the Earth's natural resources dwindle, tensions rise among the various factions vying for control over the limited remaining resources.

The story follows Aria, an engineer and expert in cryo technology who works for one of the powerful factions. She is tasked with designing and implementing new technologies that would allow humans to survive in harsh environments like Mars or beyond. However, her work puts her at odds with her superiors when she discovers evidence of dangerous experiments they are conducting on human subjects without their consent. 

As she delves deeper into this conspiracy and tries to expose it, she finds herself targeted by highly trained assassins known as Sabre-Tooths - genetically-engineered soldiers with advanced weapons capabilities. With time running out, she must use all her skills and knowledge to escape capture and unravel the secrets behind the Sabre-Tooth program. Along the way, she meets a diverse cast of characters from different sides of the conflict who challenge her views and force her to question her own loyalties.
