‘Engines of Creation’ is a gripping science-fiction novel that examines the complex relationships between technology, humanity and the future. It is the story of Dr Amelia Hartley, a brilliant but lonely scientist who has developed a new technology called the ‘Engine of Creation’. It has the power to alter matter at the molecular level, to allow people to create whatever they desire. The book begins with a demonstration of this invention to a group of wealthy investors and scientists. But the demonstration ends in a catastrophe, resulting in the disappearance of Dr Hartley and the dispersion of her research. The narrative then jumps five years later to a number of different characters, including a former assistant of Dr Hartley, an inquisitive journalist, a ruthless businessman and a government agent. Each of them embarks on a search for the missing scientist and her revolutionary technology, each driven by their own reasons. As the plot unfolds, the reader sees the characters being drawn into a web of danger and moral quandaries, as they grapple with the ‘Engine of Creation’ and its potential to either bring about a new age of prosperity or wreak havoc. The novel also explores themes of power, responsibility and the limits of science. The climax of the book is a breathless race to find Dr Hartley and her technology, and it ends with the reader left to wonder about the potential and dangers of human innovation. ‘Engines of Creation’ is a provocative and intriguing read that evokes many of the major issues in the current debates about technology and society.
