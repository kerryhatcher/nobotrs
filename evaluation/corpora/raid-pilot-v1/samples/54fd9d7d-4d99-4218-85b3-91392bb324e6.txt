British tennis star, Carlos Moya, suffered a surprising defeat in the second round of the Wimbledon Championships, sending shockwaves through the tennis world. The former world number one was beaten by relatively unknown player, John Millman, in a thrilling five-set match.

Moya's shock defeat has left the tournament wide open, with many now questioning who will take the title. The Spaniard's exit follows a series of unexpected results at this year's Wimbledon, including early exits for several top seeds.

Speaking after the match, Moya expressed his disappointment but praised Millman's performance. "I didn't play my best tennis today, and John took full advantage of that," he said. "He played incredibly well and deserved the win."

Meanwhile, Millman was ecstatic with his victory, stating, "It's a dream come true to beat a player like Carlos on such a big stage. I just tried to stay focused and play my game, and thankfully it paid off."
