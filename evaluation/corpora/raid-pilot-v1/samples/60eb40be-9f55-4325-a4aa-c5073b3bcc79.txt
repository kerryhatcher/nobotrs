This paper proposes a three-level Attention Network (TAN) for real-time medical image segmentation, a task that is critical in many medical diagnostics. The TAN mechanism helps to focus attention on the important areas of the input images, ignores the irrelevant parts, and better reflects the interdependencies between the features. The method consists of three attention units: spatial, channel, and scale. These units were evaluated on large CT and MR images, and compared with other methods, it was found that TAN is able to achieve high accuracy and efficiency, and the segmentation speed is comparable to other methods while having a relatively low complexity. It is possible that the proposed method can be a significant step towards improving the efficiency of clinical diagnosis and further advancing medical image analysis.
