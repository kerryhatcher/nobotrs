This article presents the findings of the Future-AI consortium, a multidisciplinary group of experts in artificial intelligence, radiology, pathology and other medical specialties, which was established to develop and set out principles and guidelines for the future use of artificial intelligence in medical imaging. This article deals with the potential benefits and challenges of using artificial intelligence in medical imaging, including privacy, algorithm transparency and clinical implementation. Also included are recommendations for the development, evaluation and implementation of trustworthy artificial intelligence systems in medical imaging. These recommendations aim to ensure that the benefits of artificial intelligence are realised to the full, and the risks are minimized.
