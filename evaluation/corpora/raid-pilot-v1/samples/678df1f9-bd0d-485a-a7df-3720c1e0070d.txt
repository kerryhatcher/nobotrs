Former England international Shaun Wright-Phillips is set to begin his tenure on the right wing for his new club. The seasoned player, affectionally known as SWP, committed his future to his new club after agreeing on terms.

Wright-Phillips, 39, who has previously enjoyed successful stints with Manchester City, Chelsea, and Queens Park Rangers, will utilise his wealth of experience in his new role on the right side of midfield.

Having made the move to the club on a free transfer earlier this week, the winger is expected to slot straight into the first-team setup. The club's manager has spoken highly of Wright-Phillips' potential impact, praising his quick feet, accurate crossing, and sheer work rate.

"SWP brings a wealth of experience and skill to our team. His prowess on the right wing is well-known, and we believe he will greatly enhance our attacking options. Shaun has proven himself at every level of this sport, and we're excited to see him apply his trade here," the club's manager stated in a press conference.

Wright-Phillips himself is excited about the new opportunity and has pledged to give his all for his new club.  

"I've always given everything on the pitch, and it will be no different here. I am looking forward to starting on the right and doing my best to help the team. There's a great group of lads here, and I believe my experience can make a difference," said Wright-Phillips. 

Known for his quick pace and ability to take on defenders, Wright-Phillips has made a name for himself on the right wing. The former England international is expected to play a significant role in the club's attack, providing width and stretching opposition defences. 

The willingness of the coach to accommodate Wright-Phillips in his preferred role bodes well for the club's attacking prospects. As fans eagerly anticipate the start of the season, much of the hope rests on the ever-reliable shoulders of Shaun Wright-Phillips, who's set to begin on the right.
