This paper presents a novel approach to semantic image segmentation, called “dual similarity distillation” (DSD). It applies the concept of distillation to improve the performance of segmentation models by taking advantage of the intrinsic similarities between images. This paper presents a double similarity distillation (DSD) framework to this end, which is based on two kinds of similarity, intra-class similarity, which can capture the common features between the classes of images, and inter-class similarity, which can discriminate between different classes of images. Experimental results show that DSD can effectively improve the segmentation accuracy compared with other methods. Moreover, DSD is not dependent on any single model and can be flexibly integrated into any existing model, making it a flexible and universal semantic image segmentation tool.
