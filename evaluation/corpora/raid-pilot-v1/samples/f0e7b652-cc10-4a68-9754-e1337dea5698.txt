In the small town of Millfield, a series of mysterious and gruesome murders have taken place. The victims seem to have no connection to one another, and the local police are at a loss as to how to stop the killer. As the body count rises, a group of friends who had recently graduated from college decide to take matters into their own hands. They rent a cabin in the woods nearby and set out to trap the killer.

As they lay in wait, they begin to suspect that the killer may not be who they thought it was. They start to question their own motives and the reasons behind their actions. As the night wears on, tensions rise and the friends begin to turn on each other. They realize that they may not be able to trust each other, and that their own secrets and desires may be what led them to this point.

As the killer draws closer, the friends must confront their own fears and insecurities. They must work together to outsmart the killer and survive the night. But with each passing moment, the odds seem to be stacked against them. Will they be able to solve the mystery and escape with their lives, or will they become the next victims of the deathtrap?
