This paper presents a novel attention-based method for semantic segmentation. It combines the self-attention mechanism of the Transformer to capture long-range dependencies, and exploits the spatial scale-free feature in the scalar domain. The architecture is fully attentional, thus removing the need for convolution in the network. Using the Cityscapes and ADE20K data as a test set, we show that the Segmenter outperforms other methods in terms of performance. Moreover, the model is more robust to spatial deformations, which makes it suitable for many real-world applications. We also give an in-depth analysis of the representations of the attentional features of the Transformer in the context of segmentation.
