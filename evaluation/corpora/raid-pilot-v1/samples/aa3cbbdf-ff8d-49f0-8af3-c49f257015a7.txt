This paper proposes a new unsupervised semantic image segmentation method, which uses both CLIP and Stylegan. The method is based on the ability of CLIP to understand both images and texts. With these two strengths, our method is able to identify semantically meaningful regions in images without the supervision of humans and the prior knowledge of segmentation masks. Quantitative and qualitative evaluations showed that the proposed method is able to achieve good results on even difficult scenes where supervised systems have failed. We have made a substantial advance in unsupervised semantic image segmentation.
