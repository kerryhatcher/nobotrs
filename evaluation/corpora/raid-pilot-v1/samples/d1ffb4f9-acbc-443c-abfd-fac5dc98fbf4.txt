This paper presents a new method of weakly supervised image segmentation, introducing the concept of the inter-extremal-points-geodesics (IEPG). This paper suggests that, by analyzing the extreme points of an object boundary, more accurate and faster image segmentation can be achieved. The IEPG method is designed to cope with the complicated shapes and variations of images, thereby overcoming the limitations of traditional image segmentation methods. This paper provides a comprehensive evaluation of the proposed method, which is shown to outperform the existing methods in performance. The results show that the IEPG method is an effective approach to the field of weakly supervised image segmentation.
