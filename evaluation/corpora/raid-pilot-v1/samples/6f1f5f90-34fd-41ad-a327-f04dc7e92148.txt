The story of Werewolf and Dragon tells of the inhabitants of the fictitious little town of Alnwick, in the wilds of the Pacific Northwest, who live side by side with supernatural creatures. But when an ancient dragon, named Ignis, wakes from a long sleep, he threatens to destroy both the human and supernatural world. The beginning of the novel deals with the rich local history and folklore, the town's secret supernatural population, and the group of werewolves led by the alpha, the fierce and mysterious Fenrir. As the story unfolds, a series of powerful earthquakes shake Alnwick, causing many strange phenomena to occur that the characters are unable to explain. Just when the earthquakes have subsided, the terrifying Ignis emerges from the depths of the mountains, and his wrath causes danger, because he begins to wreck havoc. When he tries to take over Alnwick, he encounters resistance from Fenrir and his werewolf pack, which also wants to protect their home. In this book, the two perspectives of Fenrir and Ignis are presented to the reader, enabling the reader to enter into the minds of these strong creatures, to learn about their fears, their reasoning and the history that has shaped them. And as the battle between these two alpha beings increases, Alnwick is drawn into chaos and turmoil. At the same time, the town's human population works to unravel the mystery of the horror that has fallen on their town. A group of strange characters are brought together, each with a personal story and a dark secret related to the supernatural population of Alnwick. The humans and werewolves form an unlikely alliance in the wake of the dragon's rampage, and the story becomes about unity, sacrifice and courage. The book ends with an epic confrontation between Fenrir and Ignis, with a powerful sub-plot that reveals an old, long-standing and deep-rooted hostility between these two supernatural beings. Then the human characters' personal stories are resolved. This book brings to life the colorful world of the Pacific Northwest, with its human and supernatural characters, and with its in-depth exploration of relationships between different races, balancing the raw animal instincts of survival against the values of community, friendship and co-existence. It is an exciting journey, which keeps the reader glued to the page.
