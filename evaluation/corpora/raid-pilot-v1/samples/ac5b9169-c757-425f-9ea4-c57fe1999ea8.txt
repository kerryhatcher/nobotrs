In a world shattered by war and chaos, a young girl named Aria emerges as one of the last surviving humans after being raised by wolves in the wilderness for most of her life. She has grown with their instincts, learning to hunt, survive, and protect herself at all costs. However, when she discovers that she is different from the other animals around her - possessing human-like qualities and memories – she sets out on a journey to find others like her, seeking answers to the questions about her past and her own identity. Along the way, she encounters various challenges including hostile survivalists and mutant creatures, while also building relationships with other survivors, forming alliances and ultimately uncovering secrets that will change everything she thought she knew about. Will Aria be able to embrace her humanity, or will she forever remain a Child of the Wolves?
