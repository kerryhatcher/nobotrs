India’s tennis sensation Sania Mirza has become the first Indian woman to win a Grand Slam tournament. She won the women’s doubles at Wimbledon, partnering the Swiss Martina Hingis in a thrilling match against Russia’s Ekaterina Makarov and Elena Vesnina. The victory marks a significant milestone for Indian tennis. Mirza, a 28-year-old from Hyderabad, had won three mixed doubles at the Grand Slam tournaments before this but it is her first women’s doubles title. Mirza and Hingis have played together since the beginning of the year and have already become a formidable pair on the international circuit. They have won three tournaments in a row and are currently on a winning streak of twelve matches. Mirza, who turned professional in 2003, was the first Indian woman to win a WTA title and enter the top 50 of the women’s rankings. The victory was widely celebrated in India. Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Mirza and Hingis on winning the title. “It’s a moment of pride for us. We are very happy and proud,” he said. The All India Tennis Association (AITA) also congratulated Mirza. “This is not just a personal triumph but a triumph for Indian tennis as a whole,” said AITA secretary-general Hironmoy Chatterjee. Her success will be a great inspiration to the next generation of Indian players.
