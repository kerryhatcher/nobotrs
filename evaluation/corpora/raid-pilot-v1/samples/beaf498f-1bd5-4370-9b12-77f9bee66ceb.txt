Liverpool manager Rafael Benitez has deflected blame from goalkeeper Jerzy Dudek for the team's recent struggles.

Benitez has been under pressure to improve Liverpool's form, with the team currently sitting in eighth place in the Premier League. Some fans have called for Dudek to be replaced, citing his poor performance in recent games.

However, Benitez has refused to blame Dudek for the team's problems. "Jerzy is a very good goalkeeper and he has been with us for a long time," he said. "He has made some mistakes, but every goalkeeper makes mistakes. It is not just about him."

Benitez instead placed the blame on the team as a whole, citing a lack of discipline and focus. "We need to be more disciplined and focused," he said. "We need to work harder and be more determined. We need to be more like the Liverpool team we know and love."

Despite the criticism, Benitez has shown faith in Dudek and has kept him in the starting lineup for the team's next match.
