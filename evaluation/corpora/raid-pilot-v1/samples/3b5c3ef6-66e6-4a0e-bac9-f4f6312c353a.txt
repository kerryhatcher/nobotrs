In the distant land, where shadows cast
As through life’s road I wandered so vast,
A longing filled my heart for home once more
Where love and warmth could never be ignored. 

From golden fields to distant skies so blue,
I roamed with wanderers, wild and true,
With every step I yearned to turn back time
To days of joy that seemed just minutes ago in delightful rhyme.

Yet pride and arrogance had blinded me from within
So I stumbled forth, without thought or kin,
Lost in this maze, this labyrinth of dread,
Once a bright mind now darkened by greed.

But oh how mercy found its way to me at last
As though a ray of light had broken through the thickest glass,
My eyes were opened wide as I began anew
And realized all my dreams lay back in view.  

For there awaited me a father's endless grace;
An arms open wide, welcoming back his prodigal son in haste,
No longer would I starve or feel alone,
For home sweet home was mine forevermore to own.
