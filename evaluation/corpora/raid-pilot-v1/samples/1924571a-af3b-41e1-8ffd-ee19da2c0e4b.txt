In the land of fear and grief,
Where freedom is denied,
There's a story that I need
To help us understand with eyes wide.

A tale of power and might,
Of struggle and pain so deep,
Of a people who've been kept out of sight
And forced to take their fate asleep.

For years they were oppressed and abused,
By a regime that ruled without mercy or care,
Their hopes and dreams destroyed,
Leaving them filled with despair.

But now there are those who want to change,
Who seek to build a new path,
To bring peace to a war-torn range,
To give hope back at last.

They may have their flaws and faults,
Like every other group so far,
But we must remember their past plights,
If we truly want justice for all to share.
