Former Real Madrid and Bayern Munich winger Arjen Robben will not be joining Barcelona, according to Barca president Joan Laporta. 

Robben, who recently announced his retirement from professional football, had sparked rumors of a potential return to La Liga after stating that he was open to offers from top clubs.  

However, Laporta has categorically denied any interest in signing the Dutch international, stating that Barcelona is focusing on other transfer targets. 

Speaking to the media, Laporta said, "I have a lot of respect for Robben, he has had a fantastic career, but at this moment, we are not considering signing him. We have other plans in mind for strengthening our squad."

Robben, 37, had a highly successful career, winning numerous trophies with his clubs, including the Champions League with both Bayern Munich and Real Madrid. He also represented the Netherlands at the international level, earning 96 caps and scoring 37 goals. 

Known for his blistering pace and mesmerizing dribbling skills, Robben was often a nightmare for defenders, cutting in from the right wing and unleashing his trademark left-footed shots. 

Although he had struggled with injuries towards the later stages of his career, Robben remained a highly sought-after player due to his experience and ability to change the outcome of games single-handedly. 

With Robben now officially retired, the focus will shift to Barcelona's other transfer targets as they look to build a competitive squad for the upcoming season. The Catalan giants have already made a number of signings, including the return of striker Sergio Aguero and the additions of Eric Garcia and Memphis Depay. 

Barcelona endured a disappointing campaign last season, finishing third in La Liga and crashing out of the Champions League in the Round of 16. Laporta and his team are determined to revitalize the squad and regain their status as one of Europe's top clubs.
