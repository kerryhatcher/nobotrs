The fusion of the transformer and convolutional neural network models in the Bilateral Awareness Net (BANet) was used to improve the semantic segmentation of the very high resolution urban scene images. The model is conceptually divided into two parts: within-region consistency and between-region discriminability. It uses a pair of mechanisms: the Bilateral Attention Module and the Position-Aware Transformer, which captures long-distance dependencies and local details, and achieves a higher degree of accuracy than the conventional methods. The experiments on remote sensing data sets prove the effectiveness of BANet in urban image understanding. The proposed model also has a wide range of applications in image understanding.
