England and Manchester United defender, Wayne Bridge could miss the remaining part of this season due to a knee injury. He sustained the injury during a Premier League match last weekend against Liverpool where he was forced off the pitch in the 79th minute.

Bridge, who has been instrumental this season in United's defence, underwent a series of tests on Monday to assess the extent of his injury. The results revealed major ligament damage that could potentially sideline him for the rest of the season.

The manager, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer confirmed the news during a press conference on Tuesday. He said, "It's unfortunate for Wayne and for us as well. He has been such an important part of our defence this season. However, injuries are part of this game and we have to deal with it. We wish him the speediest recovery and hope he makes an early return."

This seems to be a major blow to the Manchester United side, who are currently leading the Premier League table with just seven games left in the season. Bridge's absence may have huge implications on the team's performances which could, in turn, affect their chances of clinching the title.

Bridge, 32, has been one of the key figures at Old Trafford this season, having made 28 appearances in all competitions. He's gained admiration for his consistency, work-rate, and with his ability to provide vital assists. His contributions have been recognized as instrumental to United's surge at the top of the table.

The club’s medical staff will continue to monitor Bridge’s progress in the coming weeks. A timeframe for Bridge's recovery is yet to be specified, but Solskjaer has expressed hope that the defender could return before the end of the season if his recovery goes well.

As the team awaits the return of Bridge, Manchester United will have to look on their substitute bench for a proper replacement. The onus now lies on the other players of the side to step up and fill the void left by Bridge's absence.

This recent development adds to the club’s growing list of injury concerns, as they head into crucial late-season fixtures. With or without Bridge, Manchester United is gearing up for a challenging end to what has been an exciting season.
