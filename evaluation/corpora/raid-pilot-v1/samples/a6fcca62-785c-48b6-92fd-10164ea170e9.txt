The new spatially guided self-supervised clustering network (SGSCN) was designed to learn features from unlabeled images, thereby reducing the reliance on a large number of labeled images. Experimental results on several medical image data sets show that our method outperforms the other self-supervised learning methods and image segmentation methods, in terms of accuracy, robustness, and efficiency. It opens up a new perspective on self-supervised learning in medical image analysis, especially in situations where labeled data are scarce or expensive to acquire.
