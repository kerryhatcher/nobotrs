‘Legacy’ focuses on the life of successful New York architect Benjamin Cahill. In spite of his professional success and wealth, he feels an inexplicable emptiness. And when a mysterious package arrives on his doorstep, his happiness turns into an unanticipated crisis. He learns that he is the last of a line of watchmakers, and that his family was able to bend time itself. Intrigued and puzzled, he plunges into his family’s past, and in doing so, he finds himself back in the teeming streets of 18th-century Paris, the hellish trenches of World War I, and the idyllic English countryside of the twentieth century. Every time period reveals to him the extraordinary talents of his ancestors, the obstacles they faced, and their love of their craft. At the same time, he experiences love, jealousy, betrayal, and the weight of his newfound power to bend time, and he does not know whether it is a gift or a curse. He is forced to make a choice between living in the past, exploring the present, and coping with his own unique ability to influence the future. And in doing so, he comes to understand the true value of time, inheritance, and the important influence that our past has on our present and our future. ‘Legacy’ is essentially a gripping novel about understanding the value of time, inheritance, and the importance of our past on our present and our future.
