Sekhmet, goddess fierce and wild
A lion-hearted warrior in the sky
With fiery eyes that blaze a beacon bright
And talons sharp as daggers shining white

She charges forth upon the battlefield
Rainbow mane spreading like an empyreal aura round her field
Her roar trembles the ground beneath her feet
As she seeks victory, sweet to taste and fleet

Bathed in bloodshed, but never faltering
She cleaves through fears with feline slithering
For she is life, raw and untamed
Unfettered by mortal bonds or shame

Through war and strife, she reigns supreme
An unyielding force, unbroken and serene
Sekhmet, the divine warrior queen
