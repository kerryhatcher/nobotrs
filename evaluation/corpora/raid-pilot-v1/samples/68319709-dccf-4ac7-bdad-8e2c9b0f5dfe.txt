Welsh rugby hero Stephen Jones has hailed a remarkable comeback performance by former team-mate Gavin Henson, which saw him help his side to a vital victory. After a long period out of the game through injury, Henson’s masterful performance underlined his standing as one of the greatest players of all time in the Welsh team. His decisive penalty in the closing stages saw the team come from behind to win the game and evoked memories of past success. Jones, who played international rugby with Henson for many years, was quick to congratulate his former team-mate. “Gav has shown tonight why he is one of the most respected players,” said Jones. “He’s had a lot of bad luck with injuries. It’s been difficult for him.” But tonight he has proved he is still the man for the job. The way he took control of the game and made sure we won at the end was vintage Henson.” Jones was also pleased to see the way Henson had handled his comeback game, and praised the player’s coolness under pressure. “That is what separates the greats from the good. Gavin has always had that about him, and it was great to see it again tonight,” said Jones. But Jones was not the only one to praise Henson’s comeback. His fans posted their delight on social media, and his team-mates praised his “character” and “resilience”. And there was even talk of a surprise recall to the national team ahead of a series of crucial games. Henson himself, as always, was modest about his comeback, saying he was just pleased to be back on the pitch doing what he loves and helping his team to a win. As always, the quiet hero left the talking to his play, and his play showed once again that Henson is still a player of exceptional talent and determination, and a sporting icon with much more to give on and off the pitch.
