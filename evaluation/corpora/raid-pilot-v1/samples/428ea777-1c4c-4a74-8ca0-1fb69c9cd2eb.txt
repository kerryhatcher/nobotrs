This paper presents a new self-supervised, clustering neural network, specifically designed for the segmentation of medical images. Unlike other self-supervised learning methods, which require several labeled training examples, our network makes use of the spatial information and the feature extraction ability of convolutional neural networks to obtain a good result. It is based on the clustering procedure, which is mainly guided by the compactness and separation between classes. The performance of the clustering network was evaluated on several standard medical image datasets. The experiment shows that the proposed method greatly improves the accuracy of segmentation and is quite useful for practical medical image analysis.
