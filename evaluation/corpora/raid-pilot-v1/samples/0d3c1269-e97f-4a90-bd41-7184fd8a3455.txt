Semi-supervised left atrium segmentation is a challenging task in medical image analysis. In this paper, we propose a novel approach that utilizes mutual consistency training to improve the accuracy of left atrium segmentation in semi-supervised settings. Our method involves training a deep neural network to predict the left atrium segmentation mask from multiple input images, where each input image is augmented with a random transformation. The predicted masks are then compared with the ground truth masks using a mutual consistency loss, which encourages the network to learn a consistent representation of the left atrium across different input images. We evaluate our method on a large dataset of cardiac magnetic resonance images and demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods. Our approach has the potential to improve the accuracy of left atrium segmentation in clinical settings, where large amounts of unlabeled data are often available.
