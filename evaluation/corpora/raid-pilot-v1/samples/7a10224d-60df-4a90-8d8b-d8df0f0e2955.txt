This paper presents a consensus of experts on the shaping of a trustworthy artificial intelligence in future medical imaging. The guidelines, formulated by the future-AI project, are based on three main pillars: equity, transparency, and ethical considerations. These guidelines also address the risks and challenges that may be encountered, such as privacy and security issues, the potential for bias in data collection and processing, and the need for reliable AI models. The agreement on principles and recommendations provides a guideline for the implementation of a responsible and sustainable artificial intelligence in health care.
