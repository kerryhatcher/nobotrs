Sekhmet, the lion-headed goddess of war,
Stands fierce and proud, a symbol of might and power.
With eyes that blaze like the sun,
And a roar that shakes the earth, she has begun.

Her mane flows like a river of gold,
As she strides forth, a sight to behold.
Clad in armor, she is a force to be reckoned,
A protector of the pharaoh, a warrior queen.

With her fierce gaze, she commands the battlefield,
And with her claws, she cleaves through the enemy's shield.
A goddess of destruction and rebirth,
She brings forth life and death in equal measure.

Sekhmet, the lion-headed goddess of war,
Is a symbol of strength and power,
A protector of the pharaoh, and a warrior queen,
A goddess of destruction and rebirth, who reigns supreme.
