This paper presents a new method for image segmentation of sonar images by means of an iterative deep unsupervised learning method. The aim of the method is to improve the automatic identification and recognition of underwater objects. As a means of accomplishing this, we use a convolutional auto-encoder network to learn a set of high-level features and an iterative unsupervised clustering method to segment the sonar images. Testing on real-world data shows that the proposed method outperforms conventional methods in terms of both accuracy and speed. This method is widely applicable and can be applied to a wide range of marine scientific and industrial applications, including seafloor mapping, underwater archeology and mine detection.
