This book is an action-packed adventure novel, a tale of suspense, set in the wilderness of Montana and revolving around a pack of black foxes. The main character is the professional naturalist John Marrows, who is on assignment to study the mysterious actions of these magnificent creatures in the midst of the advancing hordes of humanity. When two of these rare foxes are stolen, John goes in pursuit, and encounters a poaching ring run by wealthy collectors, who want to catch and sell the foxes for their rare fur. His world begins to collapse, and he is caught up in a dangerous intrigue of greed, passion for the natural world, self-preservation, and law enforcement. With the help of a local sheriff, Allie, and an old Native American, Charlie, who has a close understanding of the land and its creatures, John puts a plan into action to destroy the ring, but soon they are themselves pursued by the very men they are trying to stop. And as the story unfolds, the reader is slowly shown the corruption and the depths of the hatred of nature, up to the highest level. The mystery deepens, and the desire to survive in the midst of humanity grows stronger in John and his companions. The pack of black foxes is shown in its natural environment, and its life and habits are presented as well. In the end, John Marrows confronts the ring’s ringleader, and he must draw on all his intelligence, passion, and passion for nature to win the day. “Black Foxes” is more than an adventure story. It also involves the depths of human greed, the beauties and terrors of the wilderness, and the great need for humanity to understand and preserve the wonders of nature.
