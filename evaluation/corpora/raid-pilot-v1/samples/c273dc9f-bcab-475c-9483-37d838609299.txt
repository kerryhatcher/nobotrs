Welsh rugby icon Stephen Jones has expressed his delight at the remarkable comeback performance of his former team-mate, Gavin Henson, who helped secure a crucial win in the league.

Henson, returning after a long absence due to injuries, put on a masterful display, underlining his status as one of the most influential players the Welsh rugby has ever seen. His decisive points in the closing stages boosted his team to victory, rekindling memories of past glories.

Jones, who played international rugby alongside Henson for many years, was quick to praise his former colleague. “Gav has shown tonight why he is so highly regarded in the sport,” said Jones.

“He’s had a tough time with injuries - it’s been difficult for him. But he’s showed tonight that he’s back in full force. The way he took control of the game and secured the win during those final minutes – it was vintage Henson.”

Acknowledging Henson’s contribution to the game, Jones added, “Rugby is a much better sport with Gavin in it. We've missed him."

Jones also expressed satisfaction at how Henson managed his comeback game. He commended his calm leadership and his ability to steer the team in crucial moments.

"The ability to stay calm and poised under pressure is what defines great athletes. Gavin has always demonstrated those qualities and it was great to see him display them once again," said Jones.

Jones was not the only one singing Henson’s praises. Fans took to social media to celebrate the player’s return, while teammates hailed his "character" and "resilience".

Henson's performance has also sparked speculation that he could be in line for a surprise recall to the national squad ahead of some crucial fixtures in the coming months.

Henson himself was modest about his return, saying "I'm just happy to be back on the pitch, doing what I love and helping the boys get a win."

The quiet hero, as always, left his talking to his deeds, and his deeds only underline what we already know: that Henson remains a player of rare talent and determination - a sporting icon who still has so much to give on and off the pitch.
