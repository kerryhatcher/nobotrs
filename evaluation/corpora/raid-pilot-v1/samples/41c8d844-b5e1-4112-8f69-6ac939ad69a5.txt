“The Calculus Affair” is a book of adventure, whose plot is built around the existence of a great scientist, Professor Calculus, who has invented a powerful sonic apparatus, with the possibility of a mass destruction weapon. The novel begins with the blow of the shards of glass which pierced the house, which is explained as the fault of the professor's invention. But Professor Calculus disappeared, and his friends, a journalist named Tintin and his dog Snowy, came on a trip to look for him. Their search takes them all over Europe, where they encounter several adventures, among others: the meeting with spies, a dangerous car chase and a number of pitfalls. It is then revealed that two rival countries are behind the abduction of the professor, wanting to use the professor's invention for their own ends. Tintin and Snowy, with the help of their friends, must find a way to evade their enemies and save the professor before it is too late. This book is a thriller that puts friendship and loyalty on a pedestal. The Calculus Affair is an exciting adventure that keeps the reader at the edge of his chair until the very end.
