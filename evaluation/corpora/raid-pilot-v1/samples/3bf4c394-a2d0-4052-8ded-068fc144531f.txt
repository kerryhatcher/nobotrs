Chelsea manager, Thomas Tuchel, has denied reports that winger Arjen Robben could be making a sensational return to Barcelona. 

Speculation had been rife that the Dutch international, who previously played for the Catalan club between 2004 and 2007, could be set for a reunion with his former teammate and current Barcelona manager, Ronald Koeman. 

However, Tuchel dismissed these rumors during a press conference ahead of Chelsea's Premier League clash with Manchester City. He stated that Robben is an integral part of his plans for the upcoming season and that there have been no discussions regarding a potential move to Barcelona.

Robben, now 37 years old, has had a storied career that has seen him play for top clubs such as Chelsea, Real Madrid, and Bayern Munich. After leaving Bayern in 2019, he announced his retirement from professional football but made a surprise comeback last year, signing a one-year contract with his boyhood club, FC Groningen.

Since his return, Robben has shown glimpses of his former brilliance, but injuries have hampered his progress. Despite this, Tuchel remains confident in the winger's abilities and believes he can still make a significant impact for Chelsea.

Barcelona, on the other hand, have been actively seeking reinforcements in the attacking department following the departure of Lionel Messi to Paris Saint-Germain. The Spanish giants have been linked with several high-profile players, including Robben, as they look to rebuild their squad under Koeman.

However, Tuchel's denial puts an end to any speculation surrounding Robben's potential move to Barcelona. It remains to be seen whether the Catalan club will turn their attention to other targets or if they will continue their pursuit of the Dutch veteran.

In the meantime, Robben will focus on regaining full fitness and contributing to Chelsea's title challenge this season. With Tuchel's faith in him, the winger will be eager to prove his worth and show that he still has what it takes to perform at the highest level.
