In the marketplace of life,
Culture is bought and sold,
Its value measured in gold.

The stories and traditions,
That make us who we are,
Are traded like commodities,
In a global, consumerist bazaar.

Our art and music,
Our dance and poetry,
Are packaged and shipped,
To the highest bidder, you see.

And though we may protest,
And try to hold on tight,
To the things that make us unique,
In the end, it's a losing fight.

For culture is a product,
To be bought and sold,
And in the end, it's all just business,
As the world turns, and the story unfolds.
