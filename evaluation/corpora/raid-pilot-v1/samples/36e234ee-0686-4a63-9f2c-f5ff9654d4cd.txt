The San Jose Open saw some of the biggest names in tennis breeze through their opening matches, as the tournament got underway in California. 

World number one Novak Djokovic wasted no time in dispatching his opponent, American Bjorn Fratangelo, in straight sets. Djokovic, who is aiming to win his fifth title in San Jose, looked in fine form as he cruised to a 6-2, 6-2 victory. 

In the women's draw, top seed Serena Williams also had a comfortable start to her campaign. The 23-time Grand Slam champion defeated German Laura Siegemund 6-3, 6-2 in just over an hour. Williams, who is making her return to competitive tennis after giving birth to her daughter, showed no signs of rustiness as she dominated the match from start to finish. 

Another big name to progress was Australian Open champion Naomi Osaka. The Japanese star, who is currently ranked number two in the world, defeated American Madison Brengle 6-3, 6-4. Osaka, who is known for her powerful groundstrokes, showcased her skills as she overcame a determined Brengle to secure her place in the next round. 

However, not all the big guns had it easy. In the men's draw, third seed Stefanos Tsitsipas was pushed to the limit by American Tommy Paul. The Greek player eventually prevailed in a thrilling three-set encounter, winning 6-7, 6-4, 6-4. Tsitsipas, who reached the semifinals of the Australian Open earlier this year, showed his resilience and determination to come out on top. 

In the women's draw, second seed Karolina Pliskova faced a tough challenge from American Danielle Collins. The Czech player had to dig deep to secure a hard-fought 7-6, 6-7, 6-3 victory. Pliskova, who is known for her powerful serve, had to rely on her mental strength to overcome a determined Collins. 

As the tournament progresses, fans can expect more thrilling matches and intense battles between the big guns of tennis. With Djokovic, Williams, Osaka, and other top players in the mix, the San Jose Open promises to be a showcase of world-class tennis.
