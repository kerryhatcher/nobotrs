Aston Villa manager, Dean Smith, has agreed to a new contract with the club, keeping him at Villa Park until 2023. The news comes after a successful season for Villa, who secured their Premier League status with a 17th place finish.

Smith, who joined Villa in 2018, has been praised for his work in transforming the team's fortunes. Under his guidance, Villa won the Championship play-offs in 2019, earning promotion to the Premier League. Despite a challenging start to their top-flight campaign, Smith's side showed great resilience and determination to secure their survival.

The new contract is a testament to the faith that the club's owners have in Smith's ability to continue building a successful team. Villa's CEO, Christian Purslow, expressed his delight at the agreement, stating that Smith has been instrumental in the club's progress.

Smith himself expressed his gratitude for the opportunity to continue leading Villa. He highlighted the unity and spirit within the squad as key factors in their success and expressed his excitement for the future.

Villa fans will be pleased with the news, as Smith has become a popular figure among the supporters. His attacking style of play and commitment to developing young talent have endeared him to the Villa faithful.

With his future secured, Smith can now focus on building on the progress made this season. Villa will be looking to strengthen their squad in the summer transfer window and aim for a higher finish in the Premier League next season.

The new contract for Smith is a clear statement of intent from Aston Villa. The club is determined to continue their upward trajectory and establish themselves as a force to be reckoned with in English football. With Smith at the helm, Villa fans can look forward to an exciting future.
