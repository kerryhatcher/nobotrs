The advance in deep learning technology has brought about significant improvements in tracking objects visually, but challenges remain such as occlusions and variations in object pose. To address these issues, we propose a novel approach called CHASE (Cell-level differentiable Neural architecture SEarch) to robust visual tracking. Our method first adopts a multi-object tracking framework that uses a cellular neural network encoder to extract features of objects at both local and global levels, followed by a decoder that generates bounding boxes, distances, and directions of all visible objects from previous frames. We then incorporate differentiable neural architecture search, enabling our system to learn an optimized network structure that improves overall tracking performance on synthetic and real-world datasets. Experiments show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art methods on multiple benchmark datasets in terms of precision and stability, demonstrating the value of our proposed optimization algorithm in improving robustness during tracking.
