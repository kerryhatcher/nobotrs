This paper presents a method for using contrastive learning for efficient segmentation of medical images. The segmentation of medical images is an important part of modern medicine because it enables automatic diagnosis and assists with the process. This paper introduces a novel framework that combines the strengths of contrastive learning and semi-supervised learning, resulting in a significant increase in label efficiency. This paper also presents a detailed study and analysis of several challenging datasets, demonstrating better or comparable performance compared to other state-of-the-art methods. These results will give more insight into the effective use of semi-supervised learning in medical image segmentation, which will lead to further improvements in this area.
