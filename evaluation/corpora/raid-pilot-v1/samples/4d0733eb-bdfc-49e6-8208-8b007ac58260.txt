The Island is a moving story, a novel about an ordinary young man named Max who finds himself stranded on a desert island, uninhabited, after a shipwreck. With no hope of immediate rescue, Max must rely on instinct and innate survival skills to endure this new life, and the story wonderfully illustrates his struggles and his struggles to survive. Max lives alone, he is fighting against the natural dangers of the island, hostile animals, lack of food, and the harshest of weather. Extreme loneliness and the burning desire to return home drive him to undertake an adventurous search for civilization; he painstakingly builds a primitive raft and launches it on unknown seas. When his raft fails, Max faces failure, despair and the chilling prospect of death. But the novel is suddenly brought to a new point, a young girl named Amelia appears on the island. They face new dangers together and have to find new ways of dealing with it, to find strength in their shared misfortune and common companionship. Their relationship develops through the hardships they have to face, which is a moving touch, a deep understanding of the human relationship in the face of adversity. This is a story about the indomitable human spirit, about the strength of unexpected friendships and the unwavering hope that drives people to endure, even in the harshest conditions. It combines adventure, romance and survival into a passionate roller coaster and brings us to a surprising ending that says a lot about endurance, hope and the most basic instinct for survival.
