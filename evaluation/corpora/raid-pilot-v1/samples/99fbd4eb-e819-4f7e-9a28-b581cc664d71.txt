The language of water is soft and sweet,
Its whispers carry secrets to our feet.
It flows with grace, it ebbs with might,
A silent speaker in the depth of night.

It dances on the leaves, it sings in the breeze,
In every corner, it finds its ease.
A universal language, pure and clear,
It speaks to all, without any fear.

From mighty rivers to tiny streams,
It paints a picture, like a dream.
With gentle touch, it soothes the land,
And with powerful surge, it takes a stand.

Water, the language of life and love,
In its depths, we find peace and joy above.
So let us listen, and learn its ways,
And in its language, find our own paths.
