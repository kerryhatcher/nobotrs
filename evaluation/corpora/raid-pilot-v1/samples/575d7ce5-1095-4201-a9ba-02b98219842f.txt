This paper introduces a method of constructing observation sets which satisfy the desired coverage in any given set of observations, without making any assumption about the distribution of the data. We develop a new way of measuring the accuracy of a prediction set, which is robust to the presence of outliers. Our analysis shows that by using this further measure of the accuracy, and some tuning of the coverage, it is possible to obtain good predictions which do not depend on the data distribution or which are restricted to a certain class of observation sets. We show the effectiveness of our method through a number of experiments on simulated and real data.
