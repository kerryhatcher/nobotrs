WALKING TWO MOONS is the story of a thirteen-year-old girl, Salamanca Tree Hiddle, who, along with her eccentric grandparents, travels across the country to her mother’s grave in Idaho. And along the way she tells the mysterious, moving story of her friend, a fellow thirteen-year-old who is dealing with the mysterious disappearance of her own mother and the shocking revelations that follow. As Sal struggles with the pain of her own loss, these two parallel narratives illuminate the hidden strength of these young girls, forced to deal with problems far beyond their years. After desperately wishing her mother would come home, Sal is dismayed to discover the darker truth: that her mother has died in a bus accident and is never coming back. But Phoebe’s mother is unexpectedly found alive, and she is reunited with Phoebe and her strange and enigmatic brother. On her trip back, Sal is forced to face the truth: her mother had run away not only to find herself, but also to come to terms with her impending death. And Sal learns to understand her mother’s feelings, which brings a remarkable growth in her character. The novel concludes with Sal returning home, changed and mature and ready to face the many challenges of life. Throughout the story, the importance of compassion and the ability to see things from the other person’s point of view is repeatedly emphasized. This is a book that tries to open the eyes and hearts of its readers to understand the perspective of others.
