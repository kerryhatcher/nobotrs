A famous geneticist named Joanna Hartfield has discovered a revolutionary technique that can accelerate the evolution of a human being, which may even be used to cure incurable diseases. But her discoveries attract the attention of a secret organization called the Arcanum Society, which wishes to make use of her research for the manufacture of weapons. And the heroine suddenly finds herself in a secret war. During a raid on her laboratory, Joanna is injected with her own formula, which changes her very DNA, and miraculously instead of killing her, the formula gives her marvelous abilities, including the power to heal herself. In her darkest hour, she finds the Mutagen Alliance, a group of resistance fighters, who are bent on defeating the sinister plans of the Arcanum Society. Hartfield has become an invaluable asset, both as a scientist and as a fighter. But despite many victories, the Arcanum Society still continues to mutate victims, turning them into monstrous beings, using them as a weapon to suppress opposition, resulting in horrible consequences. Now, in addition to her battles, Joanna has to look for a cure for these mutations, which makes her research as well as her combat skills even more important. The combination of this struggle within and the escalating conflict on the outside, tests Joanna's resolve and sanity. She must learn to trust her allies and herself. Richly layered with a complex character, whose alliances shift during the film, and with astonishing twists, the film is a gripping exploration of the relationship between power and power, the limits of scientific progress, and the definition of humanity, as well as the courage required to stand up to oppression. In the climax, Joanna makes a life-altering decision that offers hope to the mutated and challenges the reader's perception of what it means to be human.
