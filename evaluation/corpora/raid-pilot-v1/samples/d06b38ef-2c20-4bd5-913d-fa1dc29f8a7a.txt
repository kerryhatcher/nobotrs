This paper introduces a novel approach to a few-shot image segmentation that uses both a global and local contrastive learning mechanism. It proposes a dual-layered neural network that independently emphasizes the discrimination between the global semantic level and the local texture level. In order to quickly generate useful information, a “Masked Memory Projection” module is proposed to project a large number of positive examples into a shared memory bank, and to discriminate the test image from the masks. The experiments conducted on several famous image databases show that the proposed method has an excellent performance on the few-shot image segmentation task, and its performance is significantly better than that of the previous method. The research proves that both the global and local contrastive learning mechanisms can be used to increase the strength of the discrimination of the network, and improve the segmentation efficiency.
