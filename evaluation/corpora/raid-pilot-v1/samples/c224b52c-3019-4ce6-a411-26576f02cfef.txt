In this intricate and suspenseful novel, we follow the story of Professor Maya Patel, a brilliant mathematician at a prestigious university whose career is threatened by a series of calculus-related allegations that she has been accused of plagiarism in her teaching. The accusation sends shockwaves through the academic community and creates rifts within Maya's close circle of friends and colleagues. As she fights to clear her name, Maya discovers a hidden world of calculus-obsessed rivalry, backstabbing, and deceit - one where the truth may be more elusive than she ever imagined. Along the way, she uncovers secrets about her own past and confronts the shadowy figures behind the accusations. Will Maya be able to solve the complex equation that threatens to bring down everything she has worked so hard to achieve?
