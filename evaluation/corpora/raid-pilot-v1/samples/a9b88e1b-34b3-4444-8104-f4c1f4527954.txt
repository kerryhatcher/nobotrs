This setback comes as a blow to Wales, as Ramsey has been a key player for the national team and his absence will be sorely felt.

The left-back had to be substituted in the 43rd minute and further assessments revealed ligament damage. This news will be disappointing for Wales manager Ryan Giggs, as Davies has been a reliable presence in the team's defense.
