We present a new method of interactive image segmentation based on the idea of edge-guided flow, which bridges the gap between the requirements of traditional interactive segmentation and the requirements of real-world applications. Our system relies on the crucial role of the edges for its operation and is thus independent of the inputs of the operator or the properties of the object. This approach improves the system's generalization capabilities and thus increases the quality of the segmentation in a large number of images. We show in several experiments on several image sets that the results of our method are competitive with those of the best interactive methods in terms of accuracy and usability. These results show that edge-flow is a promising and versatile solution for effective interactive image segmentation.
