Embark on an epic journey through time and space in this thrilling tale of technological advancement and its consequences. Follow the brilliant mind of Max Turing, a scientist who has dedicated his life to unlocking the secrets of artificial intelligence and interstellar travel.

As Turing delves deeper into the mysteries of advanced engineering, he becomes increasingly obsessed with creating machines that can surpass human intellect. Alongside a ragtag group of allies, including his resourceful engineer friend Lucas, Turing begins to make strides towards building sentient machines capable of changing the course of history.

However, as Turing's work gains attention from both government agencies and private corporations, he finds himself caught up in a dangerous game of power and ambition. With the threat of war looming over him, Turing must navigate treacherous waters and difficult ethical dilemmas as he races against time to perfect his engines of creation.

This exhilarating story takes readers on a wild ride through a future filled with cutting-edge technology, intricate machinations, and unexpected twists turns. As the stakes get higher and the lines between man and machine blur, only one thing is certain - the fate of humanity rests in Max Turing's hands.
