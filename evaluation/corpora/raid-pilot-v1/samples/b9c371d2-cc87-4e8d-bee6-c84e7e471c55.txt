Mirza, 23, is only the third player to have represented India at both Wimbledon and The Olympics. Before him, Leander Paes was part of the men's doubles team at Wimbledon in 1994, while Somdev Devvarman competed for India in both events during the London Games in 2012.

The former world number one junior singles champion will go up against fellow qualifier Ricardas Berazinis, who beat Serbia's Viktor Troicki on Tuesday in straight sets to secure his spot alongside Mirza on Wednesday in the first round of the grand slam tournament.

Mirza has had an impressive start to the season, winning five titles - including two ATP World Tour titles - since January on the doubles circuit with partner Mahut. However, this will be his first appearance at Wimbledon as a professional.
