The past is the past, it cannot be changed,
A memory, a moment, a fleeting range,
It lingers in our minds, a thought or a dream,
A time that has passed, a moment that seems.

But the future awaits, a new chance to start,
A new journey, a new path to impart,
A time to make new memories, to forge new ties,
To explore the world, and reach for the skies.

So let us leave the past behind, and look to the future with hope,
A new beginning, a new chance to cope,
For the past is the past, and the future is bright,
A time to live, to love, and to make things right.
