Vienna, Austria: 

After a significant time of contemplation and deep reflection, Sonja Ablinger has decided to leave the Socialdemokratische Partei Österreich (SPÖ). She informed her comrades about her decision at the SPÖ party meeting in Vienna.

According to reports, the political climate within the party has been increasingly charged with conflicts for some years now and this made party leader Robert Zadrazil more aware than ever of the need for reforms. In view of this atmosphere of increasing polarization, Ms. Ablinger states that she wishes to take a step back due to personal reasons. There have not been any rumors or speculations regarding a possible defection prior to this announcement. It is yet unfortunate news as we bid goodbye to one of the prominent female politicians from our country. Her contribution will be deeply missed by many.
