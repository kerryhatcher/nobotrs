Former Englishman Shaun Wright-Phillips will go on the right side in his new position at the new club. The veteran player, popularly known as SWP, was in agreement with his new club and re-signed. The 38-year-old, who had also achieved success at Man City, Chelsea and QPR, will use his experience on the right side of midfield. The winger, who came to the club as a free agent earlier this week, is expected to immediately enter the first team. The club's manager also praised the experienced player's potential and praised his "quick legs", "accurate cross" and "good will". "We believe that Shaun's game on the right side is one of the strongest in the league and that he will bring something extra to our offensive game. Shaun has proven himself in every competition and we are very excited to see him use it here," the coach said in a press conference. The winger himself is very pleased with the opportunity and has vowed to give everything to his new team. "I've always given everything on the field, and here I will do the same. I look forward to starting on the right and giving everything to the team. There is a great bunch of guys here and I believe that I can make a difference with my experience," he said. Shaun Wright-Phillips is known for his speed, his attack and thorny twigs that have made him famous as a winger. The former Englishman is expected to play an important role in the offensive game, occupying the right side and stretching the defenses. Coach's will to move him to his favorite position is a good sign for the offensive. The fans, who are already looking forward to the new season, have pinned great hope on the reliable shoulders of the veteran Shaun Wright-Phillips, who will start on the right.
