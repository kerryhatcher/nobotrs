Manchester United have announced that they will be starting their new star player, Michael Wright-Phillips, at right wing in the first team this season. The club has been looking to fill the role after the departure of Antonio Valencia and Nani during the transfer window.

Several players had been vying for the position, including Ashley Young and Adnan Januzaj. However, the decision was made by manager David Moyes, who believes that, with his pace and agility, Wright-Philips is the best player for the job.

“Michael is a fantastic footballer and we are really excited about having him,” said Moynes. “He’ll give us something different out there and I think he’ll fit perfectly into our system.”

The 19-year-old Dutchman signed from Porto last summer for £6 million and scored twice in preseason friendlies. While many fans may be unfamiliar with Wright-Philips, some insiders believe he could become one of the most dangerous players in the league in the coming seasons.

With Phil Jones returning from injury, Moynes also confirmed that Wayne Rooney would begin the campaign as the striker ahead of Radamel Falcao. This means that Marouane Fellaini will be utilized more centrally, replacing Tom Cleverly in midfield.
