“Last Seen Wearing” is a film that focuses on the main character of the investigation of a mysterious disappearance of a young college girl, Lauren Hill, who vanished without a trace after being “last seen wearing” her school uniform. She devoted herself to her studies and was planning to use her vacations to see the world; but her sudden disappearance has caused a complete disaster for her family. As the interrogation continues, we learn more about this person: a veiled adolescent, who was dealing with the pressures and secret alliances of college life. The detectives move from the crime scene to the campus, to the homes of the friends, and even across borders. They encounter some deceptive clues, strange friends, a strangely secretive school principal, an indifferent boyfriend and a lot of chats on the Internet. Meanwhile, the disintegration of the family, the separation of the wife, the devastation of the life of the main character and the burning search for the missing girl in the background, intertwined with the case, so that the rays of the truth can be felt in both of them. Then the investigation, woven with scandalous secrets and surprising revelations, drives the main character to the most relevant truth about the surrounding society and his own torn life. Finally, the case comes to a dramatic end, when a hidden photograph tucked between the pages of a second-hand book reveals the earth-shattering clue to the unimaginable fate of the girl, which leads the main character to a deadly confrontation with the criminal, in which he fights not only for justice, but also for his own life. Finally, the mystery of Lauren’s disappearance is solved in a dramatic finale, leaving both the main character and the reader with the question: how durable is the outward appearance, how many secrets are sown by people.
