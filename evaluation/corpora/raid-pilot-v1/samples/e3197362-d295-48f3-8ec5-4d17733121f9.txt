England's most promising young batsman, Ian Bell, is about to make his debut in the Test match against the West Indies. Bell, a right-handed batsman of twenty-two from Coventry, is in the squad of twelve selected by the English and Welsh Cricket Board. Bell has scored more than 1,000 runs in county cricket this season, including three centuries and five half-centuries. England's coach, Duncan Fletcher, expressed his confidence in Bell's abilities. He has a sound technique and a calm temperament, both essential for Test cricket, he said. Fletcher's statement is all the more important as England's batting has come under severe pressure after a series of poor performances. Bell's appointment is expected to strengthen the middle order and provide some stability. The young cricketer expressed his delight at the opportunity. He had worked hard and was ready to do his best, he said. “I look forward to contributing to the success of the team.” His debut will also mark an important step in his career as he will be the first player from Warwickshire to represent England in Test cricket since Nick Knight in 2001. Bell's appointment will be an added incentive for England as they attempt to regain the lost momentum of the team's batting. The first Test against the West Indies begins next week at Lord's, in London.
