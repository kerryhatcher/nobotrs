Gareth Bell is set to make his England debut in their upcoming Test match against Sri Lanka, according to media reports. The uncapped Lancashire left-arm spinner has been named in the squad that will face the tourists at Headingley on Friday (June 24).

Sources have indicated that Bell's inclusion was based on his impressive form in domestic cricket this season, particularly with the ball. In eight County Championship games so far, he has taken 31 wickets at an average of 9.67, including three five-wicket hauls.

The 30-year-old played for England in two Twenty20 Internationals back in 2015 but did not manage to make a lasting impression. However, he believes he has learned from those experiences and is determined to prove himself during his time as a full international player.

"I would love nothing more than to show everyone what I can do," said Bell, who spoke to Sky Sports about his call up. "You never know when these chances come around, and I want to take them with both hands."

While Bell is still relatively unknown to many fans, there are high expectations of him from some quarters of English county cricket. Yorkshire legend Geoff Boycott told TVNZ: "Gareth Bell is an aggressive spinner with a lot of pace. He's got potential – no question about it. We need a man like him."

This latest addition signals another shakeup within the England squad after they lost both Jimmy Anderson and Stuart Broad due to injury concerns earlier this week. Bell won't be certain to start, though; Joe Root could well opt to stick with Jack Leach or Mason Crane if they feel he provides enough spin out of one end alongside Moeen Ali.
