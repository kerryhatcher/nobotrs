The soft wedding bells chimed in the air,
As Mary and her love exchanged their vows so fair,
In a white gown that flowed like a river of dreams,
Mary looked radiant as she stood on those gleaming steps, with tears streaming gently down her face.

The room was filled with love and laughter,
As friends and family gathered for this special affair to savor.
Roses bloomed with vibrant hues of red and pink,
And the candles flickered brightly, casting warmth upon each single guest.

The newlyweds danced beneath a canopy of stars,
Twirling and laughing, free from all life's bars.
They shared their first kiss beneath the moonlight beams,
Marking the beginning of an endless stream of happy memories and sweet dreams.

As night turned into day, Mary and her lover embraced tightly,
Basking in the happiness that they had only just started tasting.
This was truly one unforgettable wedding day,
A celebration of two souls, who found their way.
