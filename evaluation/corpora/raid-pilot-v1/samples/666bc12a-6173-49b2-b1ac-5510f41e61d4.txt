‘Who Goes There?’ is a novel of spycraft, set in a complex political environment, in which the life of one of its protagonists is in peril. The central figure in the novel is Emily Stansfield, an undercover agent for the CIA, who suddenly receives a ciphered message from an unknown source in the very centre of Moscow. Following this mysterious piece of information, she sets out on a tense and dangerous mission, uncovering secrets and exposing double agents in the highest echelons of the American and Russian governments. The further she goes, the more confused the picture becomes, and the more uncertain the distinction between allies and enemies. In Washington, D.C., her superior, Director Roberts, is negotiating through a tangle of political intrigue and the intrigue of a domestic political espionage service, and trying to follow her every step. Unknown to him, a secret leak from the CIA’s Moscow office is giving Fyodor, a ruthless killer, a warning that her mission has to be completed as soon as possible. When both Director Roberts and she start to uncover the threads of the mystery, they discover a plot to provoke a new Cold War, using the technology of a resurgent communist party. The race is on to find out who can be trusted and to prevent a world catastrophe. ‘Who Goes There?’ is a fast-paced, fast-moving tale of intrigue and the highest political tension, which will keep readers on tenterhooks, unravelling the truth page by page. Its complex espionage plot leads to an explosive climax, as Emily confronts the enemy in a desperate attempt to avert a catastrophe.
