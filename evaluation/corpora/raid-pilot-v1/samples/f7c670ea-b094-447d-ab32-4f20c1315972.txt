The abstract for the academic paper titled "Trilateral Attention Network for Real-time Medical Image Segmentation" is as follows:

In this paper, we propose a novel Trilateral Attention Network (TAN) for real-time medical image segmentation. The proposed TAN integrates three types of attention mechanisms to enhance the performance of the network. The first type of attention is the spatial attention mechanism, which focuses on the spatial relationship between pixels in the input image. The second type of attention is the channel attention mechanism, which focuses on the importance of different channels in the feature maps. The third type of attention is the temporal attention mechanism, which focuses on the temporal relationship between frames in the video sequence. We evaluate the performance of the proposed TAN on the task of real-time medical image segmentation using the CamNet dataset. The results show that the proposed TAN outperforms the state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and speed.
