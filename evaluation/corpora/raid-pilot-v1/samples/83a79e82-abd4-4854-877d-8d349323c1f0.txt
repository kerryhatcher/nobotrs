I AM A JURY is a thrilling novel of justice, revenge, and moral ambiguity. The main character is Mike Hammer, a jaded war veteran and private investigator, who is obsessed with the brutal murder of his best friend, Jack, who is now dead. The novel opens with the brutal murder of Jack, which sets Mike Hammer on an inexorable revenge hunt. Hammer is looking for answers to the questions that have long eluded him. In his search for answers, he meets many different characters, each with their own secrets and motivations, among whom the mysterious and seductive therapist, Charlotte Manning, who is closely connected with the crime. The narrative is filled with suspense and action as Hammer, a lonely and restless man, wanders through the dangerous underworld of New York and confronts ruthless criminals, corrupt politicians, and his own personal demons. But the plot thickens when it turns out that the killer is not only after him, but after his closest associates as well. In spite of the growing danger, Hammer continues his investigation, firmly committed to his idea of justice and his personal code of honor. The novel reaches its climax in the form of a dramatic courtroom scene, in which Hammer, as the judge and jury, confronts the killer in a terrifying and unexpected confrontation. I AM A JURY is a novel about the thin line that separates justice from revenge, about the price that justice and revenge demand from the individual. It makes you think about the nature of morality and the lengths one man can go to in order to stand for justice.
