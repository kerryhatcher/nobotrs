Who Goes There? is the story of the life of a retired police detective who, in his desire to forget his past, retires to a small and apparently peaceful town. But his rest is short-lived when a series of mysterious happenings breaks the peace of the town. John hears noises from the house next door. His neighbor goes missing, and, haunted by the memories of his past, he is forced to investigate, despite his reluctance. As he is drawn into the mystery, he unravels a web of secrets that the town has kept for years. The more he investigates, the more he realizes that the strange events are connected with several cases he has left behind, without a solution. As he unravels these secrets, the reader will be carried on a suspenseful journey to find the truth. Who Goes There? is a thrilling story of deceit, remorse, and revenge. Unexpected turns will keep the reader on the edge of his seat to the end.
