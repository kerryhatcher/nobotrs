Noman is a dramatic novel which takes its theme from the life of a man who is known to the reader only by the name of Noman. It begins with Noman’s awakening in an unknown city and without a memory of his past, though with a vague memory of a woman’s face. The novel proceeds through the course of Noman’s wanderings in the city, where he encounters persons who seem to know him, though he does not recognize them, and where he stumbles upon certain indications of his past life, full of love, betrayal, and a mysterious crime. He meets a woman who is called Lily, who was his lover, and a man called Jack, who was his accomplice in the crime. As he penetrates into the secrets of his past, he is assailed by startling revelations about his connection with the crime, and he learns the sad circumstances of his separation from Lily. He is pursued by the police and a gang of ruffians bent on revenge. He must escape from them, and at the same time he must learn to recall his memories and his love for Lily. The novel is an exciting exploration of the nature of self, memory, and remorse. Its suspense and its rich characterizations provide a compelling reading experience. The novel ends with Noman accepting his past and making a crucial decision which will determine his future.
