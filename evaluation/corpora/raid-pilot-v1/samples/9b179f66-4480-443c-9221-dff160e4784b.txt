This paper presents a new approach to sparse training of object recognition, which combines both global and local contrastive learning. The local branch focuses on the details of the scene, while the global branch focuses on the general context of the image. The model is trained by a contrastive loss function, which encourages the learning of discriminative features. The experiments on several benchmarks have shown that the model outperforms existing one-shot learning methods, proving the effectiveness of the combination of global and local contrastive learning. The work provides a new perspective on one-shot learning and opens up new directions for further research in this area.
