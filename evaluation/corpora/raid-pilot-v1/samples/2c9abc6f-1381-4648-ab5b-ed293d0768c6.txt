This paper proposes a new ternary attention network (TAN) for the real-time segmentation of medical images. The TAN uses three attention mechanisms, namely the channel-wise, spatial, and scale-based attentions, to highlight the most salient features in the medical images. As a result, TAN has the potential to deal with complex images in real time. Experimental results on several standard datasets show that the TAN outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of both segmentation accuracy and speed. Moreover, the TAN is invariant to the types of medical images, indicating its wide applicability in the field of medical image analysis.
