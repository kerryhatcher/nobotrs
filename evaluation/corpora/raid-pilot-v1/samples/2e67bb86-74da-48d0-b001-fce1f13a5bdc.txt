The Scottish football club, Celtic have expressed their dissatisfaction with the decision to play an upcoming friendly match against Bulgarian side Levski Sofia in the country's capital, Sofia.

The game was scheduled for next month and is part of the pre-season preparations of both teams ahead of the new season. However, there are concerns about the security situation in Bulgaria, following recent violence between protesters and police during presidential elections.

In a statement on its website, Celtic said it had been closely monitoring the political developments in Bulgaria and was aware of the tensions that existed in the region.

"We take our safety seriously whether it be away from home or at Celtic Park," a spokesperson for the club stated.

"However, we strongly believe that this particular fixture should not proceed at present due to the political and social unrest in Bulgaria."

The club has also written directly to the Bulgarian FA protesting the decision to host the match. It believes the government needs to work towards creating a stable environment before any international events can be held.
