A Visitation of Spirits is a strange, poignant story, dealing with family, faith and the human struggle with the demons of the soul. The novel takes place in a small, religious town in North Carolina, and its central character, Horace Cross, is a boy of sixteen, a sensitive and gifted youngster, struggling with his sexuality and his identity. The book begins with Horace’s desperate attempt to exorcise the demons that, he believes, are actual spirits occupying his mind. Horace is helped in his effort by his cousin, the minister James ‘Jimmy’ Green, who is torn between his faith, his loyalty to his family and his inability to help Horace. As the narrative unfolds, the reader is taken back into the past, to the Cross family’s past, with its secrets and its unfulfilled dreams, and into the lives of several of the family members, each struggling with his or her own personal demons, in the attempt to conform to the prevailing standards and religious expectations. The novel is full of the brutality of bigotry and the horror of oppressive standards in a small community. The author portrays with extraordinary skill the tragic consequences of intolerant attitudes and the disastrous effects of the inability to express one’s true self. A Visitation of Spirits is a disturbing, mesmerizing tale of a young boy’s search for his identity and the effects of that search on his family and his community. The novel ends on a tragic note, leaving the reader to reflect on the tragic effects of religious and social pressures on individuals who dare to be different.
