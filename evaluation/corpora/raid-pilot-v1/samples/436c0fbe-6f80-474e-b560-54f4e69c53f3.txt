This paper proposes a novel network for semantic segmentation of urban scenes, based on a combined usage of the Transformer and the Convolutional Neural Net. The transformer is used to capture long-range dependencies and contexts, while the CNN extracts local features. It also introduces a bi-attention mechanism, which enhances the interplay between local and global features. Experiments on several standard data sets show that this network outperforms other state-of-the-art methods in terms of both accuracy and efficiency.
