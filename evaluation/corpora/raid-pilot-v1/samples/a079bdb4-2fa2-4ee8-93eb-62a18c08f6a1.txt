This paper describes a new method of learning to recognize medical images based on a semi-supervised contrastive approach, with the aim of improving the efficiency of the learned model and overcoming the shortage of labeled images in medical images. A new loss function is introduced to reward similar features of the same class, and to discourage similar features of different classes. The results of the experiment on several medical image datasets have been compared with the results of existing methods, and the efficiency and accuracy of the segmentation are compared. The results show that semi-supervised contrastive learning can make good use of unlabeled images and achieve the effect of learning to recognize without labeling.
