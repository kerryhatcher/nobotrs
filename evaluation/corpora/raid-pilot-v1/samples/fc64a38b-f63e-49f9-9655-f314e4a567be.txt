A novel, captivating in its story, set in the stern, but beautiful country of Siberia. The story revolves around the life of a young ballet dancer, Anastasia, who, forced to leave Moscow for the sake of the revolution, finds herself in a small Siberian village where she is initially treated as a foreigner. The novel portrays in vivid and realistic detail Anastasia’s difficulties in adjusting to the village, to the severe Siberian winter and the simple village life. But her passion for dance does not weaken. She begins to give dancing lessons to the village children on the ice of a frozen lake, the only open space. The villagers are reluctant at first, but Anastasia’s perseverance and the children’s enthusiasm gradually win them over. In the process, Anastasia falls in love with the village woodcutter, Ivan, and this love affair gives the novel a romance which enhances its story. A beautiful description of the delight and pleasure which Anastasia’s ballet lessons bring to the harsh, monotonous life of the village in the winter, and how they unite the villagers and give them a sense of purpose and hope. The climax of the novel is a performance of ‘The Nutcracker’, given by the villagers on the frozen lake, which is attended by representatives of the neighbouring villages and even by people from Moscow. The success of the performance is overwhelming, and its success symbolises the triumph of art, love and human feeling over adversity. This novel is a heart-warming and inspiring evocation of the strength of the human spirit, and leaves the reader with a new appreciation of the human spirit and its resilience.
