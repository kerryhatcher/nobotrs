The I, the Jury is the gripping story of a retired detective, John Barret, who is unexpectedly dragged back into the world of criminal investigation. The novel begins with the brutal murder of Dan, his longtime friend and former partner. Enraged and wracked with guilt, he is angry at the murder and takes upon himself the role of both detective and juror. As John digs deeper, he finds out that Dan was involved in many dark cases during his career and made a lot of enemies. It is also very strange that Dan's seemingly flawless personal life was full of illicit affairs. Along the way, John is a long way from vengeance, but he is struggling with the moral and psychological battle, and faces the reality that justice may be worth the price he is not willing to pay. Whether it is a reflection of his fallen morality, or a deliberate search for revenge, it is a gloomy fusion of personal loss and vengeance. In the end, the killer's identity and the shocking motives will make the reader think about their concepts of justice and retribution. The reader will also have a shocking understanding of the distorted human psychology and the world's vision of justice.
