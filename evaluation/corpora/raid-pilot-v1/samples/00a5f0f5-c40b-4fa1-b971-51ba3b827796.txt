This article presents a thorough investigation of multi-modal interactions in medical image segmentation. It is known that image segmentation problems call for joint processing of various modalities and efficient interaction through various settings. In this article we will give a critical overview of various image segmentation methods and we will compare them in terms of their performance and their ability to make multi-modal interactions. Also, we will introduce a new method that includes some useful strategies for managing multi-modal interactions. It will be verified through an extensive series of experiments on different image datasets, which will show its effectiveness in overcoming the difficulties of multi-modal image segmentation. This paper is an important contribution to the area of medical image segmentation and especially in the field of multi-modal image segmentation.
