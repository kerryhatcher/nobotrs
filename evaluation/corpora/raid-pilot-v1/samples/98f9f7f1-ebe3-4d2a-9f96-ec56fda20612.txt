Tis' the season to be jolly,
And spread cheer to all that we meet,
When snowflakes fall gently,
Filling hearts with festive delight and glee.

The twinkling lights brighten up our homes,
As friends and families gather round,
To share in traditions old,
And create memories yet unknown.

A time for giving gifts with joyous smiles,
Wrapping love and warmth in paper and bows,
For it is a time for kindness, sharing, and care,
When Christmas magic fills us wherever we go.
