Robbie Williams, one of Britain's most successful entertainers, has been named as the host of this year's Eurovision Song Contest in Liverpool.

The event is being held at M&S Bank Arena on May 12 and May 14, but full details are yet to be revealed by organisers.

Williams announced his appointment earlier today (Thursday) and said he was excited about taking up the challenge.

"I am thrilled that I have agreed to host this iconic show," he said. "To have had such an amazing career myself, it will be fantastic to help celebrate and bring that magic to other musicians."
