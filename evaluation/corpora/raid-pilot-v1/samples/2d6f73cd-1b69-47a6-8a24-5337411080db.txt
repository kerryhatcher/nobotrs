A day to celebrate, with joy and cheer  
A special moment, one dear to our ear  
The candles are flickering, oh so bright  
Giving warmth to the cool autumn night   

Smiling faces, laughter around  
Happiness abound, can be found  
In every smile, in every laugh  
Our spirits lift, no matter how small or big 

Today we gather, to honor this day  
To give thanks for another year’s play  
With love within our hearts  
We blow out those candles and make a wish from afar 

So let us raise our glasses high  
On this wonderful birthday sky  
Fill it with love, happiness, and cheer  
For many more memories, together next year!
