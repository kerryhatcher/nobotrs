Liverpool midfielder Steven Gerrard has expressed his happiness at the prospect of remaining at Anfield for the rest of his career.

Gerrard, who has been at Liverpool since he was just 11 years old, has been linked with a move away from the club in recent months, with Manchester United and Chelsea reportedly showing interest in signing him.

However, the 27-year-old has made it clear that he has no intention of leaving Liverpool, and is instead looking forward to continuing his career at the club where he has won numerous trophies, including the Champions League in 2005.

"I've always said that I want to stay at Liverpool for the rest of my career," Gerrard said. "I love this club, and I feel a huge sense of responsibility to the fans. I want to be a part of this team for as long as I can."

Gerrard's comments come after Liverpool manager Rafael Benitez praised the midfielder's commitment to the club.

"Steven is a fantastic player, and he's shown a great deal of loyalty to this club," Benitez said. "He's always been willing to put in the hard work and make sacrifices for the team, and that's what makes him such an important part of our squad."

Gerrard's contract at Liverpool runs until 2012, and the club is expected to offer him a new deal in the coming months.
