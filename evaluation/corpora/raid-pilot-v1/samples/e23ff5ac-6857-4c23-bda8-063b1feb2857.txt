Wales suffered a setback as two key players pulled out of the training session due to hamstring and ankle injuries, putting coach Wayne Pivac in a difficult position before the forthcoming international matches. The fact that both were instrumental in the success of the earlier matches makes their loss even more painful for a Wales team already struggling with the absence of a number of starters. Liam Williams, one of the most energetic players in the world, could be out for a few weeks, not having recovered from a hamstring injury that he suffered in a mid-week training session. Adam Beard, the tall lock, is also missing with an ankle injury. The one-meter-tall lock is known for his tremendous presence in the lineout and scrum, and his loss could affect Wales's defensive capabilities. The Wales medical staff is working overtime on the men's recovery, but it's not clear if they'll be back for the crucial match against England. Apart from Liam Williams and Adam Beard, Wales already has five other important players on the injury list, which means that they are considerably depleted for the forthcoming matches. “Of course it's not ideal,” said coach Pivac. “But we'll manage, that's what the game is about, we'll make up the difference with other players.” The double injury setback has left the fans and the commentators wondering about who Pivac will call up to replace the two injured players. Wales isn't short of quality replacements, but the loss of two key players hurts the team's hopes. When they go up against England, Wales would like to repeat the success of the Six Nations tournament. But after the latest injury setbacks, the path to victory looks steeper than ever.
