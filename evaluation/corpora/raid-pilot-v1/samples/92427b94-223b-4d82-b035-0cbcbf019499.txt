In 'A Visitation of Spirits', we follow the journey of our protagonist, Emma, as she navigates through a turbulent time in her life. Recently widowed and struggling to come to terms with her loss, Emma begins experiencing strange occurrences that lead her to believe your spirits are visiting her. At first, these episodes are terrifying but soon become intriguing and as Emma tries to uncover more about what’s really happening around her, she delves deep into mystery and darkness. Along the way, Emma meets individuals from both her past and present who seem to be guiding her towards an ultimate truth. With each encounter, she finds herself drawn further into a world where nothing is as it seems and the lines between reality and imagination blur.
