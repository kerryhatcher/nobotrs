The island tells the story of a young woman, a lost soul, who after a shipwreck, comes to the island. We see her fighting for her life, finding food, building a shelter and adjusting to the island. During the course of the weeks, she discovers that the island is not as abandoned as she had thought. She encounters a group of natives who have lived for generations in harmony with nature. Her fear and mistrust of the islanders gradually turn into respect and admiration for their culture, their traditions, their sustainable way of life. She is particularly close to a young islander named Koa, who teaches her the island language and the secrets of survival. A new danger threatens the peace of the island when a band of pirates discovers it and threatens to exploit the island's resources. Emily, now courageous and resistant, rallies the islanders in a common struggle to protect their home. The island is an exciting story of survival, resilience and the power of solidarity. The novel is also a reflection on the respect of cultures, the fight for the environment and the obstinacy of human beings in the face of the world's problems. It ends on a hopeful note. The young woman, now called by the name of Emily, remains on the island, recognizing it as her new home, and continues to defend it against the dangers of the outside world.
