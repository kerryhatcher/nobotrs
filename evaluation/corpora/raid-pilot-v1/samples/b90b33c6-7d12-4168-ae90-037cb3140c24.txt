The abstract for the academic paper titled "Iterative, Deep, and Unsupervised Synthetic Aperture Sonar Image Segmentation" would be:

This paper presents a novel approach to synthetic aperture sonar (SAS) image segmentation using an iterative, deep, and unsupervised learning algorithm. The proposed method utilizes a combination of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph-based algorithms to segment SAS images into target and clutter regions. The CNNs are used to extract features from the SAS images, while the graph-based algorithms are used to iteratively refine the segmentation results. The proposed method is unsupervised, meaning that it does not require labeled training data, and can be applied to a wide range of SAS images. The results of the proposed method are compared to those of a supervised learning algorithm and demonstrate significant improvements in segmentation accuracy.
