Sir Gerrard affirmed that he is more than happy to continue his career at Anfield. Speaking to the press, the Liverpool captain explained that he remains faithful to the Anfield side, despite the rumours that he had received offers from numerous European clubs. “I love this club more than anything and I am happy here. I want to give all I can to help Liverpool achieve their goals,” he said. This pleased Liverpool supporters all over the world, who were apprehensive about his departure. The 34-year-old midfielder also expressed his desire to lead the team to further success after the crucial match tomorrow. “I can see the challenge ahead of us. I can’t wait to lead the team and bring more glory to the Anfield side,” he said. Sir Gerrard, who was born in Liverpool, joined the youth academy at the age of nine, and in 1998 he made his debut for the first-team. In the following 20 years he scored 120 goals, and led Liverpool to several titles, including the Champions League in 2005. Liverpool’s manager, Brendan Rodgers, also rejected the rumours that Sir Gerrard was leaving the team. “Steven is a magnificent player, and a key part of our team. We are very proud to have him,” he said. The rumours about the captain’s future arose after he turned down the first contract offer from the club. The club and Sir Gerrard are expected to sign a new contract soon, enabling him to end his career where he started. Liverpool supporters and Sir Gerrard’s admirers are reassured by this statement. The captain’s dedication to the team, and his leadership skills have helped him achieve iconic status in the football world. Especially at Anfield, where he is the team’s hero.
