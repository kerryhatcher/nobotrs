In days gone by, we roamed without care
A simpler past, free from all our fears
We laughed and we played, in freedom we'd share
Our hearts open wide, with no worries or tears

But as time passed, we learned to see things differently
The future appeared on the horizon, full of promise and change
With hopeful eyes, we embarked upon this journey
Though sometimes we faltered, taking paths that were strange

For it is true what they say, once you begin
You never quite know where your destiny may lead
But though life moves forward, leaving behind all we can win
It does not forget us, even those past memories are guaranteed

So hold onto the lessons that you have been taught
From moments both happy and sad
Remember them well, so that you cannot be caught
By regrets for times that couldn't last

Embrace the present moment and fill each day
With joy and gratitude
Look up at night and let the stars light your way
As you navigate toward your dreams’ victory

For in the end, the past may come back once more
Like a gentle breeze carried by time
And when it returns to haunt our senses, we must learn to ignore
As we live out our love with passion sublime.
