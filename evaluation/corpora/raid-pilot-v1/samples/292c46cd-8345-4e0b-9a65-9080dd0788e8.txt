"Who Goes There?" follows the story of four friends, Alice, Bob, Charlie and David, who stumble upon an ancient map while on their camping trip in the remote woods. The map leads them to a hidden cave with a mysterious artifact that grants wishes. Each friend makes a wish, but things begin to spiral out of control when the artifact starts granting malicious desires instead. As they struggle to reverse the curse placed upon them, they realize that the journey through the woods was never just about finding a lake to swim in or a fire to roast marshmallows over; it was about testing themselves at every step. They have to confront their deepest fears, darkest secrets, and most challenging relationships before they can break the cycle set forth by the artifact and make their way back home unscathed. Will the journey prove too much for any one of them, causing them to lose sight of who they truly are, or will they find enough courage within themselves to overcome this obstacle?
