This paper presents EdgeFlow, a novel method of image segmentation that is based on edge-guided flow. The system is designed to address the limitations of current methods, which either require a high degree of human interaction or fail to produce satisfactory results in complex situations. It is a unified framework that unites the edge detection and the interaction in a single operation. The performance of the method is evaluated on several publicly available image data sets, and it is shown to outperform state-of-the-art techniques in terms of both accuracy and ease of use. It is suggested that the method is a practical solution for interactive image segmentation, with applications in many fields, including computer vision, image processing, and medical image processing.
