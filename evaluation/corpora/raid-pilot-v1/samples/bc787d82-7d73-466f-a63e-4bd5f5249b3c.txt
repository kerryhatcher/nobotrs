Shaun Wright-Phillips is to start in his club's upcoming match on the right wing, the team's manager said today at a press conference. Wright-Phillips, who has played very well this season, will be able to provide width and speed on the right wing. His ability to cross accurately into the box is vital for the team's attacking play. The coach expressed his confidence in the player: "Shaun has been outstanding in practice and in matches so far. His speed, agility and technique make him a danger on the right side. We think he can have a major impact on the game in hand." His selection as a starter is expected to bring more verve to the team's attack. The right winger's understanding with the team's strikers will be crucial in opening up the defence. The decision to start the Englishman means that other players, who were also competing for the right wing position, will have to stay on the bench. The coach, however, insisted that the decision was very difficult and that all players have an important role to play. The public is waiting in anticipation to see how Wright-Phillips will fare on the right wing. His speed, skill and goalscoring ability make him a must-see player in the next game.
