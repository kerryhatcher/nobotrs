‘Noman’ is a story about an anonymous man who goes through life as a stranger, Noman, who rules his life by a principle of total anonymity. Using the deceptions of a nomadic astrologer, a tradesman in the city, a writer and an outcast, he is able to pierce the darkest recesses of human society. This story follows his quest for self-realization while he tries to discover the subtleties of human society. In every event, he silently takes part and observes human nature, the norms of society and the reactions of individuals. His experiences with love, the hopelessness of his situation and his eventual redemption lead him to find beauty in unexpected places. Along the way, Noman boldly questions the pre-determined identity of the person he has become. At the climax, the surprise twist of the story brings out Noman’s secret: that absolute anonymity is his identity. He realizes that he is at once a universal man and a nobody. He understands the paradox of his existence. This is the ending of the novel, and the reader is left with a profound but open-ended conclusion to the question: what is a man? What is a human being?
