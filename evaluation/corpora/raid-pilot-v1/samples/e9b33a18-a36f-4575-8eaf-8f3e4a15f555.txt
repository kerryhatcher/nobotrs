A study is presented which describes a general and efficient model of a segmentation process, which is intended to correct the mistakes in the analysis of medical images in clinical situations. The necessity of great accuracy in the interpretation of medical images is studied, and the errors in the process of segmentation are noted. A new interrogative model is proposed, capable of learning from its mistakes and improving its future performance. The results of this method are highly accurate, and the new system is capable of quickly adapting to corrections. The proposed system operates in a wider framework of algorithms and remains efficient for a wide range of medical images, showing its usefulness in a wide clinical context.
