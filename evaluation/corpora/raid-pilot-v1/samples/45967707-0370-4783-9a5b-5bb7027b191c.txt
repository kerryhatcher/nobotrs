Image segmentation is a critical task in computer vision and has numerous applications in various domains. Deep learning-based methods have shown promising results in recent years, but they often suffer from overfitting and require large amounts of training data. In this paper, we propose a compact convolutional Bayesian neural network (ComBiNet) for image segmentation. ComBiNet is designed to address the issues of overfitting and data efficiency by incorporating Bayesian regularization and using a compact convolutional architecture. We demonstrate the effectiveness of ComBiNet on several benchmark datasets and show that it outperforms state-of-the-art methods while requiring significantly fewer parameters and computational resources. Our results highlight the potential of Bayesian regularization in improving the performance and efficiency of deep learning-based image segmentation models.
