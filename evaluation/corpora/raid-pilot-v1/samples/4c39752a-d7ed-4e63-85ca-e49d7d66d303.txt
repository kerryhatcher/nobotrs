This paper presents a BDIS-based stereo matching for real-time stereopsis in the context of endoscopic surgery. The purpose of the proposed method is to obtain precise depth information from two-dimensional stereo endoscope images, which is necessary for assisting surgeons in surgical procedures. This is achieved by using the Bayesian framework and obtaining stereo correspondences in the framework of probabilistic methods. It also uses inverse searching to increase the search efficiency and make the method real-time. This method has been tested on public datasets and live surgery and shows improved accuracy and speed compared to existing methods. It will be of great importance to the field of computer-aided surgery and is expected to improve surgical precision, reduce operational risks, and thus improve patient safety.
