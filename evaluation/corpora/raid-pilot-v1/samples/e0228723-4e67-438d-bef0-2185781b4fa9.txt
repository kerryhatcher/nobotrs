Benitez spoke in a press conference on Monday: To blame one player for defeat is not my philosophy as a coach. - The game at Anfield saw Dudek in the middle of a storm, especially for the role he played in the decisive goal he gave away. The one from the sharp, but doable Robin van Persie shot. Dudek's error made him a lightning rod for criticism, but Benitez did not want to point the finger at him as the reason for the defeat. - Yes, Dudek made a mistake for the second goal, but there were ten players on the field who could have made a difference. We must share the responsibility together. - Benitez continued: Jerzy is a great goalkeeper. We know that he had a couple of bad matches. But what counts is what he does when we have good moments. - Benitez admits that the defeat at home to Arsenal increased the pressure on his team. But he insists that everyone, including Dudek, is trying to correct the faults. After the defeat, Liverpool is in fifth place in the standings. Benitez said that he is looking forward to a collective game of revenge on Saturday against Southampton, where Dudek is expected to play. The game in the team is the most important thing in the success. - He concluded: I think that from our bad game, we can learn something and face the challenges in the future with more courage. - Many of Liverpool's former managers, including legendary figures like Bill Shankly and Bob Paisley, also respected this philosophy. It is evident that Benitez is also influenced by it and is not going to pounce on individuals when it comes to defeat. It remains to be seen if Dudek will become stronger in the face of the criticism.
