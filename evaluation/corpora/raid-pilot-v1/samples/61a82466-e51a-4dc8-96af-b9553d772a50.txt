In the quiet of the night,
Where the world is hushed and still,
I remember you, dear friend,
And the joy you brought to me.

Your laughter was like music,
Filling my heart with warmth and cheer,
And though you are no longer here,
Your memory will always be dear.

We shared so many moments,
Laughter and tears, both far and near,
And though the road ahead is long,
I will always hold you near.

In the stillness of the night,
As the world around me sleeps,
I will remember you, dear friend,
And the love that you have kept.

So here's to you, dear Werter,
May your soul rest in eternal peace,
And may your memory always be,
A beacon of love and grace.
