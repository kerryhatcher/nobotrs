“Mutation” is a thrilling, unfettered science-fiction adventure which explores the consequences of scientific experimentation. The hero is a brilliant geneticist, Dr. Alex Mercer, who has discovered a revolutionary technique of changing the human genes, and his purpose is to cure all genetic diseases and enhance human abilities. But when he experiments on himself, the experiment goes wrong, he gets superhuman powers, but also irresistible, sometimes terrible side-effects. While Alex struggles with his superhuman powers and his declining health, he is pursued by a ruthless pharmaceutical corporation, GenTech, which is anxious to exploit his discovery for its own purposes. The corporation sends mercenaries to capture Alex, and the chase spreads across continents. While this is going on, Alex’s former colleague, Dr. Lisa Warren, begins to see the dangers of the process of mutation and goes to great lengths to find a cure for Alex and prevent GenTech from producing an army of superhumans. The plot takes a dramatic turn when Alex’s mutation starts to evolve, with catastrophic consequences. As the world slides towards an apocalypse, Alex and Lisa face a terrible dilemma, and they are faced with a difficult choice. “Mutation” is a story of the ethical limitations of scientific progress, the corruption of power, and the indomitability of the human spirit in the face of catastrophe.
