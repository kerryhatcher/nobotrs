Noman is an intelligent, ambitious young man living in the bustling city who has just landed his dream job at one of the biggest corporations in the world. However, his happiness is short-lived as he begins to realize that his job comes with many sacrifices, including his time and relationships.
As he rises through the ranks, Nolan starts to question whether his success is truly worth the cost. He struggles with maintaining a work-life balance and finds himself becoming increasingly isolated from his loved ones. Along the way, he meets new people who challenge him to reevaluate his priorities and consider what really matters most in life.
Despite these obstacles, Noman remains determined to achieve his goals. But as he reaches the pinnacle of his career, he must confront a difficult decision that will shape the rest of his life forever. Will he choose success over love and happiness or will he learn the hard way that true fulfillment can only be found when you put your heart first?
