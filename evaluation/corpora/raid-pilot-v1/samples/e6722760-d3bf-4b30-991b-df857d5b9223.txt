Semi-supervised contrastive learning (SSL) has emerged as a promising approach for medical image segmentation, where labeled data is scarce. In this paper, we propose a novel SSL framework for label-efficient medical image segmentation. Our approach leverages the inherent spatial and temporal correlations in medical images to learn a contrastive representation that can accurately segment the target organs. We evaluate our method on three benchmark datasets and demonstrate state-of-the-art performance with significantly fewer labeled examples than previous methods. Our approach has the potential to revolutionize medical image segmentation by enabling more efficient and accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
