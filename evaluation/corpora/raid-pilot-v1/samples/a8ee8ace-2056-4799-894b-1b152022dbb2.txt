The paper presents a novel approach to few-shot segmentation using global and local contrastive learning. The proposed method leverages both global and local contrastive learning to improve the performance of few-shot segmentation. The global contrastive learning is used to learn the overall structure of the image, while the local contrastive learning is used to learn the local details of the image. The proposed method is evaluated on several benchmark datasets and shows significant improvements in performance compared to state-of-the-art methods. The paper also provides an analysis of the effectiveness of the proposed method and discusses future directions for research.
