My dearest love, my heart's sole desire,
A letter I write with all my might.
To you, my husband, my love, my fire,
Every word is a reflection of light.

In this letter, I pour out my soul,
The thoughts and feelings that have long been held.
Of our love, our journey, our tale to be told,
The moments that have left an imprint on my heart, both young and old.

Through the ups and downs, the laughter and tears,
Our love has stood the test of time, my dear.
With you by my side, I have nothing to fear,
For together, we will conquer all that is near.

In this letter, I vow to you, my love,
My commitment, my devotion, my endless dove.
To cherish you always, to be your faithful glove,
And to love you more with each passing day, like a rising dove.

So, my dearest husband, my heart's true desire,
This letter I send with all my love and fire.
Forever and always, I will be yours, my sincere,
And our love will continue to flourish, my love, my dear.
