Ethiopian runner Selemon Barega and Ugandan long-distance star Joshua Cheptegei have secured a double victory for Africa at the Edinburgh Cross Country race. The highly-anticipated event took place on Saturday in Holyrood Park, attracting some of the world's top athletes.

Barega, the silver medallist at the Tokyo Olympics, showcased his exceptional talent by winning the senior men's race with an impressive sprint finish. The 22-year-old built on his success from the summer games, finishing ahead of Ugandan cross-country champion Jacob Kiplimo. This marks a significant achievement for Barega, strengthening his position as one of the rising stars in long-distance running.

Meanwhile, Cheptegei demonstrated his incredible endurance by dominating the senior men's race. The 25-year-old, who holds world records in the 5,000 meters and 10,000 meters, further solidified his standing as one of the greatest long-distance runners of his generation. Cheptegei finished nearly 20 seconds clear of his closest competitor, fellow Ugandan, Jacob Kiplimo.

Both Barega and Cheptegei's victories highlight the exceptional talent and strength of African long-distance runners. African athletes have a long-standing reputation for dominating the sport and have consistently displayed their prowess on the international stage.

The race also featured a strong performance from Scottish runner Laura Muir, who secured victory in the women's race. Muir, who also competed at the Tokyo Olympics, delighted the home crowd by crossing the finish line first.

The Edinburgh Cross Country race is part of the World Athletics Cross Country Permit series and attracts some of the biggest names in the sport. The race serves as an important warm-up event for many athletes ahead of the World Athletics Cross Country Championships.

The double triumph by Barega and Cheptegei represents a remarkable achievement for African athletes on the global stage. Their victories serve as a testament to the continent's long-standing dominance in long-distance running and provide a glimpse into the future of the sport. As the athletics world turns its attention to upcoming competitions, these two athletes are certainly ones to watch.
