In this weepy tale of Werewolf vs. Dragon, the peaceful village of Eldridge is shattered to atoms when a savage dragon swoops down from the mountains to terrorize the villagers. The hero of the piece, a young blacksmith called Ethan, is a werewolf, and is not averse to keeping the secret to himself for fear of his town’s superstitions. At first he is reluctant to reveal his true nature to the villagers for fear of their reaction. But then the dragon’s attacks become more frequent and more deadly, and he realizes that he is the only one who can save Eldridge. He sets out on a quest to master his werewolf powers, and seeks advice from the wise and mysterious hermit, Morwen, who lives deep in the forest. Meanwhile, the dragon, Ignis, is not the mindless monster the villagers think he is. He is an ancient being, a creature of extraordinary powers who was banished from his home by a cruel dragon-hunter. He sought refuge in the mountains near Eldridge, where he was overcome by fear and anger, and became a danger to the people in the village. As Ethan continues to develop his werewolf powers, he slowly begins to discover the truth behind Ignis’s identity. He realizes that they are both misunderstood creatures, hunted and feared by humans. This makes him resolve to find a peaceful solution. But then the dragon-hunter who banished Ignis arrives in Eldridge, and the conflict escalates beyond his control. Ethan now has to battle not only the dragon, but the dragon-hunter and the villagers’ fear. In an epic battle, Ethan uses the power of the werewolf and his new understanding of Ignis to save his village. Werewolf vs. Dragon is a story of courage, courage, and cooperation. It also contains the themes of fear, misunderstanding, and the destructive nature of prejudice, woven into a thrilling tale of fantasy adventure.
