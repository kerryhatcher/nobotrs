Middlesbrough and Charlton played out an entertaining 2-2 draw in their Championship clash at the Riverside Stadium. The home side took an early lead through striker Britt Assombalonga, who found the back of the net in the 10th minute. However, Charlton responded quickly with a goal from midfielder Conor Gallagher just four minutes later. The visitors then took the lead in the 35th minute, courtesy of a strike from forward Macauley Bonne. Middlesbrough fought back in the second half and managed to equalize in the 67th minute, thanks to a goal from midfielder Lewis Wing. Both teams had chances to snatch a late winner, but ultimately had to settle for a share of the spoils. The result leaves Middlesbrough in 18th place in the Championship table, while Charlton sit in 15th.
