This paper proposes an original way of performing weakly supervised image segmentation by using the IEPG method. The method is based on the extremes of the clicks, which defines the ‘extremes’, and thus on the geodesic lines to obtain better segmentation. The efficiency of the proposed method is demonstrated in various cases, such as medical images and object recognition. The obtained results compare favourably with those of the current weakly supervised image segmentation methods. The research proposed in this paper opens the way for a further study of the weakly supervised learning methods, which will be used to open up new avenues of research in the automation and image analysis fields.
