The development of trustworthy artificial intelligence (AI) is crucial for the future of medical imaging. This paper presents a comprehensive review of the current state of AI in medical imaging and identifies key challenges and opportunities for future development. The paper also outlines guiding principles and consensus recommendations for ensuring the safety, reliability, and ethical use of AI in medical imaging. These principles include transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy, and security, as well as the need for ongoing monitoring and evaluation of AI systems. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration and stakeholder engagement in developing and implementing trustworthy AI in medical imaging.
