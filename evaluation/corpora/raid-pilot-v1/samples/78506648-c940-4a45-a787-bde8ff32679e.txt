"Angle of Repose" follows the story of Emma, a successful architect who has recently lost her husband in a tragic accident. Struggling to come to terms with her grief, Emma throws herself into her work, determined to complete a groundbreaking project that her husband had started before his death. However, as she delves deeper into the project, she begins to uncover secrets and hidden agendas that threaten to derail the entire endeavor. Meanwhile, Emma's relationships with her family and friends begin to suffer as she becomes increasingly consumed by her work. As she navigates the complex web of deceit and betrayal, Emma must confront her own demons and learn to find balance in her life before it's too late.
