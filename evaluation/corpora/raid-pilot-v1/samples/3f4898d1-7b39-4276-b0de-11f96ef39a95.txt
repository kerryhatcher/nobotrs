This academic paper presents a new framework for predictive modeling that is distribution-free and risk-controlling. The authors propose a novel prediction set methodology that utilizes statistical measures of risk and uncertainty to construct sets of predictions with guaranteed performance characteristics. Unlike traditional methods that assume knowledge about the underlying data distribution, the proposed approach allows for flexibility in terms of selecting appropriate distributions. Additionally, the authors demonstrate how their technique can be applied to nonlinear regression models and provide several examples to illustrate its effectiveness. Overall, this work represents an important advancement into the realm of robust and reliable predictive analytics, which has critical applications across many diverse disciplines.
