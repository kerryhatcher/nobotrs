A mystery novel set around the disappearance of a high school girl, the principal character being a slender girl with a slight build, whose first scene is the moment when she disappears one day after school, and the last time she was seen wearing a blue sweater and a pair of blue dungarees, hence the title, 'Last Seen Wearing.' This novel traces the journey of Detective Lieutenant Mendez, who is in charge of the case, and is a tough cop with a mysterious past. While digging up the details of the case, the hidden lies and the suspicious appearance of the ostensible perfect town will emerge, but it takes a dramatic turn when the secret diary is found in the hands of the detective, and the real side of the disappeared girl is exposed. The diary contains a glimpse of a possible romantic relationship with an unknown person, and a suspect is set in motion. While chasing the trail of the girl's disappearance, the reader will also face the unexpected twists of the novel and the slaps of the police, making the reader feel on the edge of their seats. This is a novel that probes the dark underbelly of the suburbs, the lies of the people, and the limit to which one woman can go to get the truth. The ending of the novel is also left unsatisfactory, and the sequel will also be expected.
