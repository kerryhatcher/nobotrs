In the depths of my despair,
I searched for a way to repair
The fracture that divided my soul
And left me feeling so alone and cold.

But as I wandered through the dark,
I realized that the journey back
To paradise would not be an easy one
And that there were many obstacles yet to come.

My heart was heavy with regret
For past mistakes I could never forget
And the memories weighed me down
Like anchors dragged beneath the waves.

Yet still I persevered, driven by hope
And the promise of redemption and reprieve from this hopelessness.
As I climbed higher up each mountain,
I felt the weight of my sins diminish, little by little.

In every breath, I drew strength from above,
From a power greater than myself,
A force that inspired me to keep pushing onward
Toward a future full of love and light.

Through the trials and tribulations, I stood tall,
With the knowledge that God had a plan in store.
His blessings rained down upon me like rain,
Bringing healing and forgiveness to my troubled mind.
