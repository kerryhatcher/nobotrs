When a renowned detective is summoned to solve a series of high-profile murder cases that appear to be unrelated, he must navigate a web of intricate clues and eerie coincidences that lead him down a dangerous path through the city's underbelly. As the body count rises and tensions escalate between the police force and the public, the detective races against time to solve the case before another innocent person falls victim to the killer's twisted plan. But as he delves deeper into darkness, he discovers the truth may be more complex than he ever imagined - with shocking consequences that will leave you breathless until the very last page.
