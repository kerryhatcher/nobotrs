Wales' hopes for the upcoming international fixtures have been dealt a significant blow as two key players have been ruled out due to injuries. The Welsh Football Association confirmed the news, stating that both players sustained injuries during their respective club matches over the weekend.

Star midfielder Aaron Ramsey and defender Ethan Ampadu will not be available for selection due to injuries. Ramsey, who plays for Juventus, suffered a hamstring injury during their Serie A match against AC Milan. Ampadu, on the other hand, picked up a back injury while playing for Chelsea in the Premier League.

The loss of Ramsey, in particular, is a significant setback for Wales. The 30-year-old has been a key figure in the Welsh midfield, with his creativity and goal-scoring ability often proving crucial. Ampadu, despite his young age, has also been a reliable figure in the Welsh defence.

Wales manager, Robert Page, expressed his disappointment at the news. "It's a big blow for us. Both Aaron and Ethan are important players for us. But we have a strong squad and I'm confident the boys will step up," he said.

The Welsh team is set to face Belgium and the Czech Republic in the upcoming World Cup qualifiers. The absence of Ramsey and Ampadu will undoubtedly be felt, but it also provides an opportunity for other players to step up and make their mark.

Page will now have to rethink his strategies and possibly make some adjustments to his starting line-up. The likes of Joe Allen and Chris Mepham could be called upon to fill the void left by Ramsey and Ampadu.

Despite the setback, Wales will be hoping to continue their recent good form. They currently sit second in their World Cup qualifying group, just two points behind leaders Belgium.

The Welsh Football Association has wished both Ramsey and Ampadu a speedy recovery. Fans will be hoping that the duo can return to action soon and help Wales in their quest to qualify for the World Cup.
