This paper introduces a novel dense inverse searching (D.I.S.) Bayesian stereomatching method for real-time stereo-matching in minimally invasive surgery. D.I.S. uses Bayesian probabilistic methods to model the uncertainty of stereo-matching, and uses an inverse searching strategy to reduce the computational complexity. The results of the experiments show that the proposed method has superior performance both in accuracy and speed, making it suitable for real-time stereo-matching. The potential improvements and future research of this work are discussed.
