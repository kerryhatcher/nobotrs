“Black Foxes” is a superb novel, set in the wilds of Alaska, the hero of which is a seasoned trapper. A twist in the life of the character is a meeting with a rare breed of black foxes. The plot revolves around the protection of black foxes from ruthless poachers, who seek to obtain their valuable fur. A new love enters the lonely life of Jack — a biologist, who shares his love for the wilds. They fall in love in the harsh winter of Alaska. The two heroes are torn apart by the weather, dangerous animals, and the poachers. Black foxes, with their innate slyness and intelligence, become the central characters of the novel and the symbol of the harsh beauty and fragility of nature. “Black Foxes” is a story of love, survival and the fight against greed. The relationship of people and animals is also portrayed, raising questions about conservation and the ethics of the treatment of animals. The plot of the novel, combining suspense and vivid descriptions of the wilderness of Alaska, makes the book a real adventure.
