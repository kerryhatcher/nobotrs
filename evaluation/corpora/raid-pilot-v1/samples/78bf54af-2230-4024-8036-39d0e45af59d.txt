The story begins with an Introduction to five main characters who find themselves stranded on a deserted island after a plane crash. They have different backgrounds, personalities and skills which they utilize to survive in this new environment. The group consists of a doctors, two engineers, an artist and a survivor. As time goes by, they must work together to build shelter, find food and water, navigate and signal for help. Throughout their journey on the island, they face many challenges including harsh weather conditions and dangerous wildlife. However, their greatest difficulties come from internal conflicts arising from differences in opinions and values. Will they be able to overcome these obstacles and make it off the island alive?
