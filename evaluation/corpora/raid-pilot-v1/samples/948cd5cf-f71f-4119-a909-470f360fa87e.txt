A Lingering Shadow, by distilling suspense and unearthed secrets, takes us to an idyllic but mysteriously remote Hameline town. The story is of a weak artist who finds herself drawn to an ancient, empty house that she feels she can't stay away from. Soon she becomes obsessed with the mansion's secrets and, gradually, the shadows appear in her paintings. These shadows, which soon become lifelike, seem to come to life and control her waking life. At the same time, she meets a local man, Adam, a charismatic man who, although he knows the town, is strangely silent about his past and the town's history. The story takes a dramatic turn when it is discovered that the mansion was the scene of a series of gruesome murders, which were irrevocably connected with Adam's family. When the old occult practices, family feuds, and curses come to light, the shadows appear to intensify, which drives her to the edge of insanity. Adam, in his quest to save the painter, has to deal with the sepia-colored horrors of his family's history and confront the evil forces in order to get rid of the shadows that linger in the artist's life. A climax unfolds on the eschatological background, the family secrets and the struggle to fight the shadows of the past, a masterfully mixed magical-mystical tavern with the secret and earthly secrets of the Hameline town. The novel is deeply moving and disturbing, and examines the conflict between light and darkness, past and present, and reality and illusion. Deeply delve into the question: Is it possible to really escape the past, or do shadows linger forever?
