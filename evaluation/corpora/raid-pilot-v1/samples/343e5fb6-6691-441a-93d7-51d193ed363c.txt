The world of dance has been shocked today by news of the sudden retirement announcement from renowned choreographer Moya.

With over three decades in the industry, Moya's choreography has captivated audiences and inspired countless dancers around the world. However, after much reflection and deliberation, she has taken the difficult decision to step down from her career due to failing health.

In an official statement released today, Moya said: "I am deeply sad to announce my retirement as a choreographer. Over many years, I have poured all of myself into my work, watching it blossom under different lighting conditions and being fortunate enough to collaborate with some incredible artists,"

The Dance Institute where Moyas choreographies has been taught for many years expressed their grief while paying tribute “it is a great loss to our community, who will miss her remarkable talent and unique approach towards dance,” the institute added.

Fans and students alike are expressing their disappointment about the end of this era, but also wishing Moya good wishes for her future health and happiness that comes first now. There will be a final showcase performance scheduled to take place next month featuring some of Moyas most iconic pieces. After that, she plans on spending more time with family and friends.

There’s no denying that Moya's impact on dance is profound and her legacy will continue to inspire generations to come through her stunning artistry and innovation within the field.
