As tensions rise over migrants in Australia, some are concerned that the city of Melbourne could become another ground zero for violence and unrest. The latest incident took place on Tuesday night when riot police stormed a group of protesters outside the Department of Immigration and Border Protection in the CBD. Witnesses reported seeing several officers armed with batons being used to clear the scene.

One man was detained by police for allegedly assaulting an officer during the protest. Several others were arrested for other offenses related to public order disturbances. In response to the incident, Victoria Police Commissioner Ken Lay said his forces had taken steps to ensure public safety. “Our job is to uphold law and maintain peace and order in Australia,” he said.

But amid reports of increasing aggression towards asylum seekers and immigrants, groups like Multicultural Affairs Minister Djamila Savic worry that these incidents could trigger further tension. They urge all sides involved to exercise restraint and work together to find peaceful solutions to these issues. In fact, recent studies have shown that immigration has more positive effects than negative ones on overall well-being of people and economy, but it’s important how we treat them and make sure they are integrated into our society.
