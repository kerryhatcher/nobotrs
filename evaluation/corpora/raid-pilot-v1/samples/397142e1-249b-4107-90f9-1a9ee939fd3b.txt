Arsenal manager Arsene Wenger has expressed his belief that former Chelsea striker Didier Drogba could have a future at club level, despite turning 40 in March and spending three years out of the game since leaving Galatasaray.

Drogba, who won four Premier League titles with Chelsea, had hinted earlier this month that he may be ready to hang up his boots, citing a series of injuries that had made it difficult for him to maintain the intensity required at the highest level. However, speaking on Thursday about players who are currently out of contract or approaching the end of their deals, Wenger said he was keen to explore the possibility of keeping Drogba around.

"We've already spoken and have an open conversation," he said. "Some players can continue playing until 36-37, depending on how good they look physically and mentally."

The Frenchman added: "I know he misses football, so it's a question to see if we can find something to fit into our squad that would satisfy him. He's played at such a high and intense level throughout his career and I don't want to close any doors on those guys whose passion for the game still burns."

Wenger also expressed his willingness to give another chance to midfielders Mathieu Flamini and Thomas Partey, both free agents after their contracts expired earlier this month .

"Matthieu is a good example of someone who came back from injury and showed good form last season," Wenger said ."Partey, too, showed good potential when he came here [on loan from Atletico Madrid]. Both of them still have something left to offer."
