ComBiNet is a compact and efficient convolutional deep learning model designed specifically for image segmentation tasks. It leverages Bayesian inference to incorporate prior knowledge about image structures, leading to better performance with fewer computations compared to traditional models. By employing novel techniques such as spatially constrained prior distributions, it effectively combines the advantages of pre-trained filters and learned representations from end-to-end training. This results in significant accuracy improvement relative to existing state-of-the-art methods on benchmark datasets. Our proposed architecture also enables transfer learning of skills across multiple domains, offering practical applications beyond standard image segmentation problems in medical imaging, object detection, autonomous driving, etc. Overall, we believe that ComBiNets will significantly contribute to advancing neural network architectures for computer vision tasks in various fields.
