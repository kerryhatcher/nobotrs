The leaves on the trees, once so green,
Are now turning yellow, a sight unseen.
The breeze carries the scent of decay,
As the world prepares for the cold of day.

The colors of autumn, so bright and bold,
Are now fading, a story to be told.
The trees once stood tall, proud and strong,
Now they sway in the wind, their leaves long.

The beauty of nature, once so pure,
Is now tainted by the passing of time, a lure.
The leaves fall to the ground, a soft embrace,
A final farewell, a moment of grace.

The world may change, and all things must fade,
But the memories of autumn will never be mislaid.
The leaves may turn yellow, but they will always be green,
In our hearts, they will forever remain.
