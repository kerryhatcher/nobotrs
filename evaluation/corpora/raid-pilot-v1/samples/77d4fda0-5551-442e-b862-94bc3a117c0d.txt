In the depths of despair, I wandered far,
Lost in the world, with nothing to cling to.
My heart was heavy, burdened by sin and sorrow,
Weighed down by the choices I'd made long ago.

I squandered my inheritance, chasing after dreams,
But the emptiness within me only grew.
I realized, too late, the true value of home,
The love and warmth of my family anew.

So I returned, with tears in my eyes,
To the arms of those who'd never let me go.
My family welcomed me back, with open arms,
Their love and forgiveness overflowing.

Now I stand here, a changed man,
Grateful for the second chance I've been given.
I'll cherish the moments I have left,
And strive to be a better man, from this day on.
