In gardens, where green grasses sway
And sunbeams warm and bright do play,
The leaves on trees are changing hue
From crisp green to that shade of blue.

In summer, when the days are long,
And nature's beauty is a song,
These green forests roam afar,
With vibrant colors everywhere.

As autumn comes with winds so cold,
The leaves drop to the ground below,
Leaving bare branches high above,
A stark contrast in this scene of love.

Yet even as they turn to gold,
There remains an inherent charm,
For though these trees may stand alone,
They show us all we can be grown.

So let us cherish every day,
While life keeps turning in its way,
For just like leaves, we too will fall,
But always rise again, you’ll see, it never makes us small.
