Wales has secured victory over Italy in Rome, winning their first Six Nations Championship match in three years.

The Welsh players celebrated wildly after they scored two tries to lead 10-5 in one of the final moments of the game at Stadio Olimpico - home ground for Italy. The stadium buzzed with energy as Wales' fans packed into the stands, cheering and singing their team on throughout the match.

Their last win came against Australia in October 2019, so it was a much-needed and emotional success for them that will give the team an early boost of confidence heading toward the rest of the Six Nations campaign. Head coach Wayne Bennett said after the game that he was thrilled with his side's performance. He praised their discipline and strength, even when faced with tough challenges on defense.

"Italian rugby is always a force to be reckoned with and today we experienced just that," he said. "But my boys stood tall under pressure and I couldn't ask more of them."

Player of the match Jonathan Davies had a standout performance in this fixture, scoring a crucial try that left the Italians struggling to keep up. His impressive display earned him praise from both Bennet and the Welsh players around him.

With many experts predicting that England would win the tournament outright, some believe Wales could still give England a serious run for their money if they maintain form and build momentum through the next few weeks leading to the finale.
