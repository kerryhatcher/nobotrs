This paper presents an innovative approach to urban scene semantic segmentation that combines the strengths of both convolutional and transformational networks. By leveraging the local spatial dependencies captured by convolutions with the global context understood by attention mechanisms, we propose a bilaterally aware network that is able to accurately classify objects and regions in very fine resolution image inputs. Our method employs multi-scale feature pyramids and bidirectional connectivity between adjacent scales to achieve high levels of accuracy across multiple resolutions. The results show that our architecture outperforms state-of-the-art methods on several benchmark datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness for the task of high-resolution urban semantic segmentation.
