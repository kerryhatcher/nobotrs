Arsenal manager Arsene Wenger has offered former Chelsea striker Gianluca Mutu the chance to train with the club and potentially earn a contract.

Mutu, 37, has been without a club since leaving Sunderland in 2015 and has been training on his own in Italy.

Wenger has been impressed by Mutu's performances in training sessions with his son, and has invited the player to join the Gunners for pre-season training.

"Gianluca is a very talented player and I have always admired his ability," Wenger said. "I have spoken to him and he is very keen to come and train with us.

"We will give him the opportunity to prove himself in pre-season and if he performs well, we will consider offering him a contract."

Mutu won the Premier League title with Chelsea in 2005 and has scored 17 goals in 53 appearances for the Gunners.
