Steven Gerrard, the former Liverpool captain, has expressed his happiness and contentment with life at Anfield. The legendary midfielder, who spent his entire playing career at Liverpool, now serves as a coach for the club's youth team.

Gerrard, who has achieved legendary status at Anfield, revealed how he enjoys being back at his beloved club and cherishes his role within the youth setup. He described the opportunity to work with the youngsters and pass on his knowledge and experiences as incredibly fulfilling.

Having retired from playing professional football in 2016, Gerrard returned to Liverpool as an academy coach. Since then, he has been gradually working his way up the coaching ladder within the club. Last year, he took charge of Liverpool's under-18 team, and he has been developing his coaching skills and honing his techniques.

Speaking about his current role, Gerrard expressed his gratitude for being given the chance to coach within the club that means so much to him. He emphasized the importance of the youth setup, claiming it is the lifeblood of the club and integral to its future success.

Gerrard's influence is already evident, with the Liverpool under-18 team enjoying a successful season. Under his guidance, the youngsters have displayed an attractive and dynamic playing style, which has seen them secure impressive performances and results.

Despite occasional speculation linking him with managerial positions elsewhere, Gerrard reaffirmed his commitment to Liverpool. He made it clear that his sole focus is on nurturing the talent within the club and contributing to Liverpool's future achievements.

Gerrard's passion for Liverpool is unquestionable, and to see him so content in his role at Anfield brings joy to fans and former teammates alike. His dedication and determination to help the next generation fulfill their potential is a testament to his character and love for the club.

As Liverpool's academy continues to produce talented young players, including Trent Alexander-Arnold and Curtis Jones, Gerrard's role becomes increasingly vital. The club and its supporters will undoubtedly be delighted to have Gerrard back at Anfield, where he is not only inspiring the next generation but also keeping his legacy alive.
