The development of artificial intelligence (AI) has revolutionized various industries, including healthcare. With its potential to improve medical imaging accuracy and speed, AI is increasingly being integrated into clinical workflows for image analysis and interpretation. However, concerns arise about the reliability and trustworthiness of AI systems in this setting. In this paper, we outline guiding principles for building trustworthy AI in future medical imaging using consensus recommendations from a group of experts in the field. Our framework considers multiple dimensions such as data quality, model transparency, human oversight, ethical implications, and patient privacy. We also propose guidelines on how to measure and evaluate AI performance, validate models on new datasets, and incorporate knowledge exchange between humans and machines. These recommendations provide practical insights that can help ensure the responsible use of AI technologies with high stakes, ultimately leading to better outcomes for patients and improving healthcare delivery.
