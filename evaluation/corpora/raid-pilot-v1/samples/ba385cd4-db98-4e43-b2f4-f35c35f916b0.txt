Liverpool midfielder Steven Gerrard has expressed his joy at being back at Anfield, where he made an incredible contribution to the team in their Premier League victory over Manchester United on Saturday. The ex-England captain returned to the club after signing a two-year contract and says he is relishing the opportunity to play for the Reds again.

"It feels brilliant to be playing for Liverpool once again," said Gerrard. "It's been five years since I left, but it still feels like home here. I knew when I signed that this was where I wanted to be."

The 35-year-old played a crucial role in Liverpool's attack against United, setting up a late goal with one cross that disrupted the visiting defense. And despite some concerns about his form earlier in the season, Gerrard says he is fitter than ever before.

"I feel really strong physically," he said. "I've worked hard in the last couple of months and I'm feeling great. It took me time to adapt to Liverpool's style of football, but now it feels very familiar."

With Liverpool leading the league by three points ahead of their crucial encounter with Tottenham Hotspur next weekend, Gerrard is excited about the future of the team. And he believes they are capable of going all the way to win the championship this year.

"Liverpool have always had a great history, and if we can keep our current squad together and continue working hard on our fitness and performances, then anything could happen," said Gerrard. "We want to make sure we enjoy every moment of this season and give everything we've got to try and bring down the first league trophy since I've been here."

For Gerrard, there were few better places to be than Anfield as the stadium watched him lift their first champions league Trophy. And while it may not be possible to replicate those moments, he will make sure that he continues to give an outstanding account of himself whenever he takes to the pitch for Liverpool.
