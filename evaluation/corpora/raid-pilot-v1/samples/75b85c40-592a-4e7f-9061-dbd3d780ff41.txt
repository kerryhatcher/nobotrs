This paper presents a detailed study of the multi-modal interaction for referring image segmentation. The framework is to use visual, textual and interactional information to improve the accuracy and efficiency of the image segmentation. The model is to understand and interpret complex interactions, which can be used to segment images according to the special requirement of the sample. The experimental results show that our model is superior to the existing methods in terms of segmentation quality and interaction efficiency. The research results have great significance for the development of image segmentation technology, and the framework has a broad application in the fields of computer vision, artificial intelligence and human-machine interaction.
