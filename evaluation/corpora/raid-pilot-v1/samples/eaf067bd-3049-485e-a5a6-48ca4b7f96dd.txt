Celtic FC, the Scottish football club, has a problem with the date of the game with the Bulgarian Ludogorets. The club is concerned about the time of the game, which could have a major impact on their domestic performance. The game, which will be played in Bulgaria, is set for November 25. The Scottish team claims that the game takes place at a time when it would be impossible to prepare for the next Scottish league match. The coach of the team, nge Postecoglou, said in a press conference: “It's too short for us, we're not happy about it." The club has already contacted the European football associations about the possibility of changing the date of the game, but there has been no answer from UEFA so far. Celtic are currently second in their group behind Bayer Leverkusen in the UEFA and the match with Ludogorets could have a decisive role in their ambition to reach the quarter-finals. The club is worried that the game can lead to an imbalance in their domestic performance. The problem is exacerbated by the flu epidemic, which has made the timetable even more crowded than usual. Despite their complaints, Celtic must prepare for a difficult week with a decisive match with Ludogorets and then a key Scottish league match.
