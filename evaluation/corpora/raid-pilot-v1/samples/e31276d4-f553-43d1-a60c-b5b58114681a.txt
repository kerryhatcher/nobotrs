Mido has made his third public apology for his controversial tweets about the World Cup match between France and England last summer. The Egyptian footballer had originally come under fierce criticism from fans and media on social media after making negative comments following the final whistle of England's 3-2 win.

Reacting to mounting pressure, Mido issued an initial statement calling back his words and offering his condolences to those offended by them. But even this was seen as insufficient, prompting him to give a more detailed explanation of his reasons behind his outburst. In a second apology video recorded over the weekend, he said:
"I realize now that my comments were misconstrued and unfair," the Egypt player explained in a tearful speech. "It wasn't intentional. I just wanted to vent what I thought at that moment of frustration on Twitter."
"When I saw how I hurt people with what I said, I felt terrible", he added. "Everyone knows me very well. You know who I am, you follow me on social media and that is why I apologize profusely with all my heart because I didn't want it. It may have been something unnecessary but it is still my mistake."
He concluded by saying
"Let’s leave this topic behind, and move forward. We need to focus on next month’s EU Euro which will see our teams take off."
