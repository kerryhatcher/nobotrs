Wales is dealing with a significant setback as two key players suffer injuries ahead the forthcoming international matches, giving head coach Wayne Pivac a selection headache. 

Full-back Liam Williams and lock Adam Beard both pulled out of training sessions this week suffering from hamstring and ankle injuries respectively; creating distress within the squad ahead of their much-anticipated clash against England. 

Having been instrumental in the victories earlier in the season, their loss is expected to be felt deeply by a Wales team already grappling with a string of injuries and absences from their starting lineup. 

Liam Williams, one of rugby's most dynamic players, could be facing several weeks out having failed to recover from a hamstring injury that he picked up during a training session in mid-week.

Adam Beard, the towering lock, is also sidelined with an ankle injury. Known for his towering lineout and scrum prowess, the absence of Beard could weaken Wales defensive capabilities. 

While the Welsh medical team is working round the clock for their speedy recovery, sources speculate that they may not be declared fit to play before the crunch match against England.

Apart from Liam Williams and Adam Beard, five more key players are already on the injury bench for Wales, making them significantly depleted as they head into their upcoming games.

"Of course it's not ideal," admitted coach Pivac. "But we have to deal with it; that's what rugby is about. It will give an opportunity for other players to step up."

The double injury blow has left fans and pundits speculating on who Pivac will draft in as replacements. There's no shortage of quality options, but the loss of such key players is undeniably a blow to Welsh hopes. 

Wales will be hoping to replicate their Six Nations triumph when they face up against England. However, with these recent setbacks, the road to victory seems more challenging than ever.
