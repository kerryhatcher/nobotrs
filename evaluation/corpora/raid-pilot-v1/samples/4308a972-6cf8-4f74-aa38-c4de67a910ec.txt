The paper presents a novel approach to image segmentation that utilizes comprehensive multi-modal interactions to improve the accuracy and robustness of the segmentation results. The proposed method integrates multiple modalities, including visual, auditory, and haptic information, to enhance the segmentation process. The visual modality is used to provide contextual information, while the auditory and haptic modalities are used to provide additional cues for segmentation. The paper demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed method through experiments on various datasets and shows that it outperforms state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and robustness. The results highlight the potential of multi-modal interactions for improving image segmentation and pave the way for future research in this area.
