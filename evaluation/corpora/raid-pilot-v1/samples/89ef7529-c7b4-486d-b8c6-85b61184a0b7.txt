In the heart of the forest, where the leaves rustle and sway,
A dance of ants is taking place every day.
With their tiny feet and their antennae so fine,
They move in perfect harmony, a sight divine.

The worker ants, so diligent and strong,
Carry their food and their young, never wrong.
They march in a line, so neat and so tight,
A symphony of movement, a wondrous sight.

The drones, so brave and so bold,
Take flight in the air, their stories to be told.
They mate with the queen, their duty complete,
And then they fall, their life's work now complete.

The queen, so wise and so grand,
Lays her eggs, her legacy to expand.
She rules with her strength, her spirit so bright,
A beacon of hope, a guiding light.

So let us learn from these ants, so small and so few,
The importance of teamwork, of working through.
For in their dance, we see the beauty of life,
A harmony of purpose, a wondrous strife.
