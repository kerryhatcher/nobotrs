This research aims to develop a comprehensive multi-modal interaction system for referring image segmentation. Such a system enables users to input reference words in two modes, visual and textual, thereby enhancing accuracy and efficiency in performing semantic labeling tasks. A series of experimental investigations were conducted on different benchmark datasets using various state-of-the-art models, including visual CNNs and language-based transformer networks. The findings suggest that fusing both mode's attention mechanisms leads to better model performance than adopting either modal individually. Thus, this study highlights the importance of leveraging multi-modality for more sophisticated referring image segmentation systems.
