Large-scale image segmentation is a critical task in computer vision applications. In this work, we present a novel technique to perform image partitioning utilizing distributed clustering algorithms such as K-means and hierarchical clustering. Our approach aims at addressing the challenges of large data processing by dividing the image into smaller subimages that can be processed independently and then combining their results to derive a final output. We also propose an adaptive method to rearrange the partitions of small clusters with minimum communication overhead, which improves scalability. The algorithm converges faster than conventional techniques by leveraging MPI (Message Passing Interface) for parallel communication between nodes. Results show that our proposed method outperforms other state-of-the-art methods, achieving superior accuracy with reduced computational time and communicative cost.
