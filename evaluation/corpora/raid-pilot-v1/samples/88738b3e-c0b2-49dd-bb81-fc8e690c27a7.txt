This paper describes an efficient and general framework for the correction of misclassifications in the clinical evaluation of medical images. The proposed framework integrates machine learning with human-computer interaction to improve the accuracy of the analysis of medical images. The performance of the framework is evaluated on different types of medical images such as MR, CT and ultrasonography. The results show that the proposed framework significantly reduces the number of misclassifications, thereby increasing the reliability of clinical evaluations. The results contribute to the current work of improving the accuracy of medical image analysis, which is of paramount importance for the diagnosis and treatment planning of patients.
