Artificial intelligence (AI) has the potential to revolutionize medical imaging, but its adoption and implementation must be guided by principles that prioritize patient safety, privacy, and ethical considerations. This paper presents the FUTURE-AI initiative, which aims to establish guiding principles and consensus recommendations for trustworthy AI in future medical imaging. The initiative brings together experts from various disciplines, including radiology, computer science, ethics, and law, to develop a framework that addresses the technical, ethical, and legal challenges associated with AI in medical imaging. The resulting principles and recommendations cover areas such as data privacy, algorithm transparency, bias mitigation, and accountability. By adhering to these principles, stakeholders can ensure the responsible and ethical deployment of AI in medical imaging, ultimately benefiting patients and healthcare providers.
