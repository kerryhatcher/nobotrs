This paper proposes a new compact convolutional Bayesian neural network, designed for image segmentation. It attempts to address the problem of the high cost of computation and large memory space required by current convolutional neural networks, whose high-dimensionality is crucial for image segmentation tasks. It also employs Bayesian optimization for determining the hyperparameters of the network and maximizing its output, while preserving its compactness and efficiency. ComBiNet achieved excellent results on several test data sets, demonstrating its superiority in terms of speed, accuracy, and memory.
