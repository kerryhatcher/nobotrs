Abstract: 

Medical image segmentation is a critical step towards extracting valuable information from medical images, enabling improved diagnosis and treatment. While much progress has been made in this area, current methods remain vulnerable to various challenges such as poor illumination, contrast variation, and anatomical complexity. This work presents a comprehensive review of state-of-the-art deep learning techniques for general medical image segmentation, focusing on their strengths and weaknesses. We discuss hybrid approaches that combine multiple model architectures to improve performance across diverse imaging modalities and pathologies. Finally, we explore future research directions aimed at enhancing the robustness and generalizability of these models, including transfer learning, multi-task learning, and active learning strategies. Overall, this work provides insights into the current landscape of general medical image segmentation and identifies promising avenues for addressing remaining challenges.
