EdgeFlow is a novel interactive segmentation method that utilizes edge-guided flow to achieve practical and efficient segmentation of images. The method is designed to handle complex and dynamic scenes, where traditional segmentation techniques may struggle. Edge-guided flow is used to guide the segmentation process by identifying and tracking edges in the image. This allows for more accurate and precise segmentation, even in the presence of noise and occlusions. The method is also designed to be interactive, allowing users to adjust the segmentation parameters and view the results in real-time. The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of EdgeFlow, demonstrating its effectiveness on a variety of image datasets and use cases. Overall, EdgeFlow represents a significant advancement in the field of interactive segmentation, providing a practical and efficient solution for a wide range of applications.
