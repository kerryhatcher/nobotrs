The paper proposes a new approach to risk-controlling prediction sets, which are used to make predictions with a specified level of confidence while controlling the risk of making incorrect predictions. The proposed approach is distribution-free, meaning that it does not require any assumptions about the underlying distribution of the data. Instead, it uses a combination of statistical techniques and optimization algorithms to construct prediction sets that meet the desired risk constraints. The paper also provides theoretical guarantees for the performance of the proposed approach, and demonstrates its effectiveness through simulations and real-world examples. Overall, the paper presents a novel and powerful tool for risk-controlled prediction in a wide range of applications.
