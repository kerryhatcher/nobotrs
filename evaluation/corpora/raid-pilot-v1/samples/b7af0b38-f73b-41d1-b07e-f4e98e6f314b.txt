In the realm of youth, where dreams take flight,
And innocence dances in the light,
There lies a world of pure delight,
Where beauty reigns and all is bright.

The sun that rises in the east,
A golden orb that brings the heat,
Its rays that touch the earth below,
And all that lives, does glow.

The sky above, a canvas grand,
With hues of blue and white at hand,
A masterpiece that never ends,
A sight that truly transcends.

The trees that sway in gentle breeze,
Their leaves that rustle with such ease,
A symphony of nature's song,
A chorus that will never wrong.

The rivers that flow, so free,
Their waters clear as crystal can be,
A life-giving force, so pure,
A beauty that will endure.

The mountains that stand tall and proud,
Their peaks that touch the clouds,
A testament to nature's might,
A sight that fills the soul with light.

In this world of juvenilia,
Where beauty reigns in all its glory,
We find a sense of peace and calm,
A sanctuary from the storm.

So let us cherish this gift so true,
And bask in nature's beauty anew,
For in its presence, all is right,
And our hearts will forever soar in flight.
