In this chilling novel set in the supposedly idyllic village of Hollow Creek, a series of mysterious disappearances and terrifying events gradually envelope a young journalist, named Emily. At first, returning to her home, Emily felt a sense of nostalgia and longing for a simpler life. But the peace of her memories is shattered by the news of several disappearances from the locality. As she digs deeper into the mystery, she uncovers a tangle of lies and secrets that have dominated the life of Hollow Creek for years. Then threatening letters arrive, from whom? But she doesn't stop digging. She discovers a sinister cult that has been active in Hollow Creek for many years. When she tries to solve the puzzle, she is forced to look into the past, and when she confronts the cult, she has to fight not only for her life but also against her past. Shadows Linger is a riveting portrayal of the darkness that can lurk in a seemingly peaceful place, a story of courage and courage. The tale is a relentless pursuit of the truth, even when the truth is not what one expected.
