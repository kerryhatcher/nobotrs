This paper proposes a novel semi-supervised learning method for the segmentation of the left atrium in cardiac MR images. The proposed method, mutual consistency training (MCT), makes use of both labeled and unlabeled data to enhance the performance of the model. MCT encourages the model to produce the same results for the same input under different conditions, which improves the generalization ability of the model. Experiments conducted on a public dataset show that the proposed method is superior to other semi-supervised learning methods in terms of accuracy, robustness, and efficiency. It contributes to the development of semi-supervised learning methods for medical image analysis, especially for cardiac MR images.
