Semantic segmentation is a critical task in computer vision that involves assigning a label to each pixel in an image based on its semantic meaning. While recent advances in deep learning have led to significant improvements in image-based semantic segmentation, there are still challenges in accurately segmenting objects in complex scenes with ambiguous or occluded regions. In this paper, we propose a novel approach to mining contextual information beyond image for semantic segmentation. Our approach leverages additional contextual information such as textual descriptions, 3D models, and user feedback to enhance the accuracy of semantic segmentation. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach on several benchmark datasets and show that our method outperforms state-of-the-art image-based semantic segmentation methods. Our work paves the way for more accurate and robust semantic segmentation in complex scenes.
