Diplomaten: EU will lift sanctions against Belarus

The European Union (EU) is considering lifting sanctions against Belarus, according to diplomatic sources. The move comes after a meeting between EU foreign ministers and Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko in Minsk on Monday.

During the meeting, Lukashenko pledged to release political prisoners and hold free and fair elections in Belarus. The EU has imposed sanctions on Belarus since 2006, including economic sanctions and travel bans for Lukashenko and other officials.

The diplomatic sources said that the EU is willing to give Belarus a chance to reform its political system and improve human rights. However, they also warned that the decision to lift sanctions will depend on Belarus's actions in the coming months.

The Belarusian government has been criticized for its crackdown on opposition protests and the suppression of free speech and media. Lukashenko has also been accused of human rights abuses, including the use of torture and extrajudicial killings.

The EU's foreign ministers will meet again in Brussels next week to discuss the situation in Belarus and decide on the next steps.
