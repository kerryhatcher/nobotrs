This paper proposes a new approach to semantic segmentation, focusing on contextual information that goes beyond the image itself. The problem is that, in the field of semantic segmentation, there are few methods that can exploit the rich information in the context of the image, while most of them rely on the visual content of the image. Using the object relationships, the category of the scene, and the spatial layout of the scene, we propose a new framework that exploits the object relationships, the scene category, and the spatial layout of the scene. Our method uses a deep learning model to obtain visual features, and a graph-based model to obtain spatial layouts. Experimental results on several public datasets show that the performance of our method is better than that of state-of-the-art methods, indicating the importance of extracting the context for semantic segmentation.
