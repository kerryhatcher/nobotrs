In the warmth of February sun,
A day of joy and laughter spun,
With friends and family gathered round,
We shared a happiness profound.

The air was filled with love and cheer,
As we danced without any fear,
Lost in the moment, pure and true,
A memory that will forever be with me and you.

We laughed until our sides ached,
And the memories we made,
We'll cherish for all our days,
In the warmth of that happy February sun.
