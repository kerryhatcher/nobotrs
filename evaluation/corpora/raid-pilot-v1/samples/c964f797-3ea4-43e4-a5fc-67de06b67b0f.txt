As the sun sets and clouds gather,
The river continues to flow steady, peaceful.
Its waters deep in hue, so dark, mysterious.
What secrets does it hold? What stories has it seen?

The gentle current whispers tales untold,
Of love and loss, joy and sorrow, bravery and defeat.
It carries with it memories from ancient times,
From civilizations long gone and empires that fell.

But as the darkness descends,
And stars begin to twinkle above,
A new rhythm takes control.
For the night has come alive.

Guided by the moon,
The creatures of the water stir,
Moving in synchronistic harmony.
Fishes glide gracefully beneath the surface,
While eels slither silently through the depths.

As night gives way to dawn,
The river's beauty never ceases,
Its power always impressive.
An essential part of life for those who dwell nearby.
