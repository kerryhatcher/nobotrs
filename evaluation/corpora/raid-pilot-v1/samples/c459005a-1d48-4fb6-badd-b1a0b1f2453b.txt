This paper proposes a new method called “dual similarity distillation” for semantic image segmentation. It takes advantage of the similarity between spatial features and channel features to construct twin distillation losses. This enables a larger amount of knowledge to be transferred from the teacher model to the student model. In experiments, the proposed method has achieved better results than the existing methods. It also has the advantages of small datasets and light models, which has a practical significance for real-time applications on mobile terminals and terminals.
