This paper presents the Segmenter model, a novel application of the Transformer architecture to semantic segmentation. In contrast to the convolutional neural networks, which have dominated this task, the Segmenter model exploits the self-attention mechanism of the TransformNets to capture long-range and complex dependencies in the data. We propose a new architecture which is a hybrid of both TransformNets and convolutional neural networks, and demonstrate its effectiveness on several well-known benchmarks. Our experiments show that the Segmenter model can achieve better performance than the state-of-the-art on both accuracy and efficiency. The Segmenter model also opens up new possibilities for the use of TransformNets in other tasks.
