Double Similarity Distillation for Semantic Image Segmentation

In this paper, we propose a novel approach for semantic image segmentation using double similarity distillation. Our method leverages the similarity between the input image and the teacher model's output, as well as the similarity between the teacher model's output and the student model's output, to improve the performance of the student model. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method on several benchmark datasets, achieving state-of-the-art results in some cases. Our approach can be easily integrated into existing semantic segmentation models, making it a valuable tool for improving the performance of these models.
