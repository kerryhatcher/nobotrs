Top-seeded tennis stars made light work of their opponents in the second round of the San Jose Open, with the tournament's big guns easing through to the next stage.

World number three, Alexander Zverev, showed his class with a straight-sets victory over American wildcard, Tommy Paul. The German star, who is the top seed in San Jose, was in imperious form, dispatching Paul 6-3, 6-2 in just over an hour.

Second seed, Stefanos Tsitsipas, also had a comfortable day at the office, defeating Frenchman Lucas Pouille 6-4, 6-2. The Greek player, currently ranked sixth in the world, was in control throughout the match, showcasing his powerful serve and precise groundstrokes.

Meanwhile, third seed Matteo Berrettini had to dig deep to overcome a spirited challenge from American Frances Tiafoe. The Italian eventually prevailed in a three-set thriller, winning 6-7, 6-3, 6-4.

In the women's draw, top seed Naomi Osaka cruised into the third round with a 6-2, 6-3 victory over American Alison Riske. The Japanese star, who is currently ranked second in the world, looked in fine form as she dispatched Riske with relative ease.

Second seed Simona Halep also advanced to the next round, defeating Russian Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova 6-3, 6-4. The Romanian, who is currently ranked third in the world, showed her class with a solid performance.

The tournament's big guns will be back in action in the third round, with fans eagerly anticipating more top-class tennis in San Jose. With the likes of Zverev, Tsitsipas, Berrettini, Osaka, and Halep all in action, the stage is set for some thrilling encounters.
