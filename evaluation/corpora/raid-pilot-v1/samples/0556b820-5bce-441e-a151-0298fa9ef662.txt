This paper presents a new deep learning-based unified and efficient image segmentation method, K-net, which is a multi-level neural network that understands images at various levels. The main purpose of this method is to overcome the main limitations of existing methods, such as the problem of inaccurately determining the boundaries of the object, the problem of understanding the fine detail, and the difficulty of working on complex images. This paper also proposes a reasonable training strategy to guide the learning of K-net. It is verified by the extensive experiment on the standard image-based benchmarks. K-net is always superior to other methods in terms of effectiveness and robustness, and this research lays the foundation for future developments in image processing, computer vision and other applications.
