The Bulgarian team has at first arranged the meeting for the 16th of September, which according to Celtic is against the UEFA rules, which prohibit matches to be held within 24 hours of the Champions League or the UEFA Cup without the permission of the ruling body. “We do not agree with the date proposed, which is clearly against the UEFA regulations, and we are in discussions with the relevant authorities to resolve this matter,” the spokesman said. The club also stated that the dates, one after the other, would be an overload on the players and a risk for the purity of the game. However, Ludogorets denies the charge and says it had informed UEFA before confirming the date. “We have made all the necessary procedures before announcing the date of the match and have not received any objections from UEFA,” a spokesman for the team said. This is not the first time that Celtic has complained about the date of a match. Last year, the Scottish team also complained about a meeting that was too close to the Champions League. So far it is still unknown whether the game will take place as planned or if UEFA will make a change. This is an unexpected event in an already eagerly awaited meeting between the two teams. Celtic is currently second in the Scottish league, while Ludogorets is first in the Bulgarian, and both are trying to stay in shape and in the spirit of the players, while following the UEFA regulations, given the very busy football calendar.
