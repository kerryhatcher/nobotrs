Sania Mirza, the tennis player from India, has become the first Indian woman to win a WTA event, underlining her special position in the sport. She won the title with her Ukrainian partner Nadiya Kichenok in the doubles category of the Monterrey Open in Mexico, defeating the second-seeded pair of China's Zhaoxuan Yang and Brazil's Luisa Stefani. From a long hiatus caused by pregnancy and injuries, she has been in a comeback mode, and this triumph comes just two years after she returned to the professional tennis circuit. Her tenacity and perseverance have helped her climb the rankings even amidst the pandemic. Her winning streak and other achievements only enhance her credibility as a professional player, one who has been playing the game since the age of six. She is not only the most ranked Indian woman tennis player but has also been awarded the prestigious Arjuna award as well as the Padma Shri by the Indian government for her sporting excellence.
