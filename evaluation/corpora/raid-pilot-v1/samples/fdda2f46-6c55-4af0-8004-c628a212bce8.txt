In the second round of the tournament, the more prominent tennis players went through with a minimum of trouble. The world number three Alexander Zverev showed his power with a straight-sets victory over American wild card Tommy Paul. The German, the top seed in the tournament, swept the American 6-2, 6-2 in just over an hour. The second seed, Stefanos Tsitsipas, had a similar easy game over the Frenchman, Lucas Pouille. The Greek, ranked sixth in the world, had no trouble in controlling his opponent and made use of his mighty serve and nimble groundstrokes. The third seed, Matteo Berrettini, was pushed to the limit by the challenge of the American Frances Tiafoe. Berrettini won in three sets, 6-7, 6-3, 6-4. In the women's draw, the top seed, Naomi Osaka, had a relatively easy game against the American, Alison Riske, winning 6-2, 6-2. Simona Halep, the second seed, also had no trouble in getting past the Russian, Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova, 6-2, 6-4. The Romanian, currently ranked third in the world, showed her power and class. All the big guns are back in the third round, and the fans are eagerly anticipating more top-class tennis in San Jose. With Zverev, Tsitsipas, Berrettini, Osaka and Halep all on show, there are some wonderful matches in prospect.
