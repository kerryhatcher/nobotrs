In various medical applications, such as the segmentation of the plurality of organs from the x-ray, the arrangement of the voxel is not uniform, which is known as the "output imbalance"; and there is an imbalance between the slices of the x-ray which contain different numbers and kinds of organs (the "input imbalance"), which has a bad effect on the results of the segmentation. This academic paper proposes a new strategy of balancing the input and output imbalance, thereby improving the segmentation result. Combo loss. Experimental results of multi-organ segmentation are compared with those of previous research and prove that Combo Loss can be used to improve the accuracy of organ segmentation.
