CHILD OF THE WOLVES is a wonderful, magical story of survival and love, set against the harsh wilderness of Alaska. The story follows the fortunes of a wolf-dog, or husky, pup called Granite, who is soon orphaned, through a series of tragedies, and who has to struggle for survival in the icy tundra, against hunger, cold and fearsome beasts. After a while, he stumbles on a pack of wolves, led by the hardened Thunder. Initially rejected by the pack, Granite’s determination and courage gradually win over Thunder and the other wolves, and he learns to hunt, to respect hierarchy and camaraderie. And he is now completely at odds with the life of a domestic dog, which he has only a dim memory of. Yet conflict looms in the form of rival packs, human hunters and the harshest winter. Torn between his own origins and his new life, he has to overcome these challenges with a wolf’s heart and the soul of a dog. Then a devastating war between packs sends him on a mission to rescue his family, resulting in unexpected alliances and a harrowing personal revelation. He is courageous and resourceful in the face of danger, and this not only earns him respect and acceptance but also makes him question his true origins and his destiny. But his life takes a new turn when he crosses paths with humankind again. He is now confronted with a great dilemma. Child of the Wolves is an incredible, absorbing and powerful story about the power of love, the battle for survival and the eternal bonds of family, told through the eyes of a wolf-dog.
