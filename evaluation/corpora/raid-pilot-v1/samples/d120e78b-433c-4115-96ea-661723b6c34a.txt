Former Arsenal coach Arsene Wenger is trying to give hope to the career of the troubled Romanian player Adrian Mutu. The former player of the blues, Adrian Mutu, had an exceptional career as a footballer, which had a lot of turbulence and trouble in the field. He said: "He is a talented player, and his personal struggles do not cancel his skills on the field. He is very tenacious, very resistant, and he has shown it in his battles.
