Middlesbrough and Charlton both drew in their efforts to climb out of the bottom three of the Sky Bet Championship on Saturday afternoon at The Riverside Stadium. In a tense, head-to-head match, neither team was able to take a commanding lead, with an equaliser late on from Liam Henry leaving the final result as 2-2.

Despite their best attempts to keep up with the relentless attacks from one another, Middlesbrough's defence had been struggling all game long. It seemed that they were unable to contain the speedy forwards of the opposition who kept finding space between defenders and pushing forward into dangerous attacking positions.

As the second half got underway, things continued much in the same way. Charlton went close once again when John Utwin fired over the bar after being set through clear by a powerful surge downfield from Jake Brown. But it wasn't until the last ten minutes of stoppage time that we saw the breakthrough.

After some great play involving Anthony Omeoaku and Marvin Ellison on the wing, Henry slammed his deflected shot past Jason Steele to leave the home supporters cheering. With just two games left before the international break, Middlesbrough will be hoping to build on this point next weekend against Brentford and gain ground on the relegation zone.
