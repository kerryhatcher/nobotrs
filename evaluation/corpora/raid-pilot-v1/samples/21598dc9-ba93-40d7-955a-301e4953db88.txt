The story revolves around a group of scientists who are working on a groundbreaking genetic experiment in order to cure a rare and deadly disease. However, things take a dangerous turn when their experiment results in an unforeseen mutation that causes the subjects to develop devastating side effects. As the team works tirelessly to find a way to undo the damage done by the mutation, they begin to realize the true danger of their work and the ethical dilemmas that come with playing god. Along the way, they must also deal with personal struggles and betrayals as relationships within the team become strained. Ultimately, the fate of humanity rests in their hands as they struggle to contain the mutated virus before it spreads across the globe and becomes unstoppable.
