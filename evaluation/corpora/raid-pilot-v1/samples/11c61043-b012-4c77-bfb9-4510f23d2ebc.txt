In the garden of paradise,
Where the sun shone bright and free,
There was a world of beauty,
Where all creatures lived in harmony.

But one day, a serpent came,
With a sinister plan,
To deceive the innocent,
And lead them astray from the land.

The serpent whispered lies,
And the people believed,
And soon the garden was filled,
With fear and doubt and grief.

The once-proud paradise,
Was now a shadow of its former self,
A place of darkness and despair,
Where all creatures lived in strife.

But even in the midst of pain,
There were those who still believed,
In the power of love and hope,
To heal and restore what had been grieved.

And so they worked and they prayed,
To bring back the light,
And little by little, day by day,
The garden began to take flight.

The sun shone once again,
On a world that was free,
And the people lived in peace,
In the paradise that was meant to be.
