In this work, we introduce a novel deep learning architecture called "Segmenter" that utilizes transformers for semantic segmentation. The model is designed to process inputs in chunks and produce output masks, enabling it to effectively handle large input sequences with minimal computational resources. The architecture consists of an encoder-decoder framework similar to previous transformers models, but modified specifically for multimodal data. We also propose a new method for training transformer-based models on imbalanced datasets by optimizing directly the loss function. Our empirical results demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed method on several benchmark tasks, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines while achieving significantly faster runtimes than previous transformer-based solutions.
