Ireland emerged triumphant from their 62nd-minute penalty shootout victory over Belgium, with goalkeeper Darren O'Sullivan making a crucial save in extra time to send his country through to the last 16 of Euro 2020.

Speaking after the game, O'Sullivan was quick to hail Italy for their performance and praised their attacking qualities. He said: "They were an incredible team, they had some amazing players on the pitch at all times."

"All credit to them, but we fought hard and when it came down to the last minute, we knew that we could do this if we stood strong!"

Ireland will face Spain or France in the next round.
