A trilateral attention network is proposed in this study to enhance the accuracy of real-time medical image segmentation. This method uses a multi-scale feature hierarchy, where features from different scales are modeled independently and then combined by applying an attentional mechanism designed specifically for medical imaging. The attentional mechanism allows the model to focus on relevant regions at different scales, enabling improved performance even in challenging scenarios where these regions may overlap or be separated. Experiments have been conducted on both synthetic and real medical images using a state-of-the-art deep learning algorithm and demonstrated that our proposed method outperforms other methods in terms of mean squared error, accuracy, and speed compared to the original baseline.
