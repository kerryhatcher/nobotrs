This paper presents an investigation into the concept of using contextual information not only contained in the image but also in the external environment to enhance semantic segmentation. The proposed method, which makes use of both visual and non-visual information, is shown to outperform conventional methods that rely solely on in-image information. The framework of the proposed method is also demonstrated to be scalable and adaptable. This study is a significant contribution to the field of computer vision, and it gives a more in-depth understanding of the role of context in semantic segmentation.
