“Angle of Repose” is a novel of deep introspection, with two alternating stories that cross in time. The first story is that of a retired schoolmaster, Lyman Ward, who, physically crippled by a disease of the bones and estranged from his son, having taken up the task of a biographer of his grandparents, to escape his own ennui. Confined to his wheelchair, Lyman is constantly going over old letters, documents, and his own thoughts in an attempt to accomplish his final purpose: to write an honest biography of his grandmother, a woman artist from the East who, after marrying a gold prospector, came west in the nineteenth century, where she pursued her American dream. Amid the richly detailed chronicle of their lives, Lyman manages to trace a unique American pioneering spirit, capable of courage, innovation, and adaptation. Lyman’s struggle is beautifully interwoven with the struggles of Susan and Oliver. A refined and educated woman, she finds herself attempting to build a home among the arid and unrefined mine camps. Contrasting with the practical and stoic Oliver, who is faced with the challenges of an engineering career that has sunk in disillusionment, the couple struggles to reconcile themselves, and their difficulties, the distance between them, and their lack of ambition lead to an affair with another man, further straining their relations. And yet, in spite of the constant theme of struggle and disillusionment, the characters find the “angle of repose,” the stable angle at which the rock collapses after falling, a metaphor for compromise with life. As Lyman enters into the ins and outs of his grandparents’ lives, he sees the trials, passions, and compromises of their struggles, and is able to face his own struggles, the disappointments of his estranged son and the hopelessness of his failed marriage. Aside from the deep insight into human relations, the novel portrays the American West in magnificent detail, with its magnificent mountains, whose grandiose heights mirror the ups and downs of life. This gripping novel is a testimony to the strength of the human spirit, to finding peace and stability in the face of adversity, to finding one’s own angle of repose.
