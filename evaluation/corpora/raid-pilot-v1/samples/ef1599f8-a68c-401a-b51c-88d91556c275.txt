Wet mourning banners flutter in the breeze,
As we gather here to say our final goodbyes.
For Werter, who once was here with us,
Is now departed from this earthly skies.

Though his memory lingers like sweet perfume,
His physical form is no longer near.
And so it's hard for us to feel the joy of moonlight,
When there's nothing left but an echo of his cheer.

But still within our hearts he shall reside,
A shining light that guides us through the darkest hour.
The world may seem empty without his soft side,
Yet we know he lives again in celestial power.

So let us raise our glasses high above,
In tribute to a soul forever bright.
For though Werter's time on Earth has passed away from love, 
He remains immortal in our sights.
