This paper proposes a novel method for the segmentation of sonar images using iterative, deep, and unsupervised learning methods. The method overcomes the shortcomings of existing methods, such as the sensitivity to noise and the inability to deal with changes in the environment. The iterative procedure gradually improves the quality of the result, the deep learning model captures the complex structures of the sonar images, and the unsupervised learning method obviates the need for the labeled training images. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms existing methods in terms of both accuracy and robustness, and that it is a promising method for automatic segmentation of sonar images.
