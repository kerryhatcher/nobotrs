As long as you're happy,
My dear, my heart will be,
For in your joy, I find my own,
And in your smile, I see.

The world may be a place of woe,
With troubles and strife galore,
But if you're happy, my love,
Then I'll be happy too, forevermore.

For happiness is a precious thing,
That fills our hearts with light,
And in its presence, all is right,
And all is calm and bright.

So let us cherish every moment,
When happiness is near,
And let us hold it close, my dear,
And let it fill our hearts with cheer.

For as long as you're happy,
My love, my heart will be,
And in your joy, I'll find my own,
And in your smile, I'll see.
