Aston Villa's midfielder, Jack O'Leary, has agreed to a new contract with the club, ending speculation about his future. The new deal will keep him at Villa Park until the summer of 2024.

O'Leary, 23, has been with the club since he was eight and has risen through the ranks to become a key player for the Villans. He has made 150 appearances for the club, scoring 25 goals and providing 20 assists.

The Irish international's previous contract was due to expire at the end of the current season, leading to speculation that he could leave on a free transfer. However, this new deal will put an end to those rumours and secure his services for the next three years.

Aston Villa's manager, Dean Smith, expressed his delight at the news. "Jack is a vital part of our team and we're thrilled that he's committed his future to the club. He's a fantastic player with a great attitude and we're looking forward to seeing him continue to develop and contribute to the team's success."

O'Leary also expressed his happiness at signing the new contract. "I'm delighted to commit my future to Villa. This is the club I've grown up with and I feel a deep connection to it. I'm excited about the direction we're heading in and I'm looking forward to contributing to our success in the coming years."

The news of O'Leary's contract extension will be a significant boost for Villa, who are currently in the middle of a challenging Premier League campaign. The club will be hoping that the midfielder's commitment will help to inspire a turnaround in their fortunes.
