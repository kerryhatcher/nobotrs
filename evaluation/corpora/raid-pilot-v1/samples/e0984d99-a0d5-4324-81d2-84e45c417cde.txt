The language of water is soft, like whispers in the night
A gentle breeze that rustles leaves and carries with it flight
It flows without words, yet communicates a tale so grand
An eloquent speak of nature's beauty and its touch on humanity's hand. 

From mighty rivers to trickling streams, it's voice is heard around
Its melody serene, tranquil and profoundly sound
It tells tales of love, passion and life's journey long
Of ancient times and memories forever gone

In droplets glistening under the sun's warm gaze
A universe vast with secrets to unveil and amaze
Underneath it lies a world unknown and alive
A paradise untouched by human strife.

Through water we all connect, for its essence runs deep
Within us, it thrives and gives meaning to our sleep
For without it, there would be no life or love
Just silence deafening, emptiness above.

So let this silent speaker ignite your soul
And hear the rhythmic beat of the waves that toll
Let the language of water wash over you
As you sway to its symphony forever true.
