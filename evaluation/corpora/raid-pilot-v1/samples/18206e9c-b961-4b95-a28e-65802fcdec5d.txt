The prehistoric adventure novel ‘The Saber-tooth’, is the tale of a young cave-man called Thak. He is an outcast amongst his own tribe for his inquisitive mind and his extraordinary abilities. One day, however, a sabre-tooth tiger begins to threaten the safety of the tribe. It is up to Thak to save them. His parents abandon him. But at the age of twelve he learns to read and writes. This gives him a valuable insight into the workings of his own body. He is also able to observe the behaviour of the animals and their habits. So he is able to realize that the sabre-tooth tiger is not just an animal but a mother defending her cubs from their own kind. With nothing but his spear and his knowledge of the beast, Thak sets out to confront the tiger. Along the way he learns about the importance of courage and the importance of living in harmony with nature. ‘The Saber-tooth’ is also the story of Thak’s relationship with his tribe. Gradually they come to respect his intellect and his courage. And the climax is a confrontation between Thak and the sabre-tooth tiger that tests his courage and his wisdom. Thak is victorious and saves his tribe. At the end of the novel, Thak realizes that he is a leader and he is respected by the tribe. ‘The Saber-tooth’ is a spirited and a thought-provoking story of survival, courage and understanding.
