The paper presents a Bayesian dense inverse searching algorithm for real-time stereo matching in minimally invasive surgery. The algorithm is designed to efficiently search for the best match between two stereo images in real-time, which is crucial for minimally invasive surgery. The algorithm uses a Bayesian approach to estimate the probability of a match between two stereo images, and then uses this probability to search for the best match. The algorithm is tested on a dataset of stereo images, and the results show that it is highly accurate and efficient. The paper concludes that the Bayesian dense inverse searching algorithm is a promising tool for real-time stereo matching in minimally invasive surgery.
