A Visitation of Spirits is a story of horror, spiritualism and personal growth. The novel is set in North Carolina in the heart of a rural, deeply religious and politically conservative region, and the hero is Horace Cross, a bright, lonely, and emotionally confused sixteen-year-old African-American, who struggles to accept his homosexuality in the midst of a deeply religious and conservative society. Accordingly, he is tormented by visions, and he longs to become a bird in order to escape the confines of his life. His older cousin, the scholar and minister James Greene, embarks on a spiritual quest to resolve his doubts about Christianity, religion and morality, partly inspired by his concern for Horace. The story also depicts the family history of the Crosses, which is suffused with a continual struggle with the collective memory of slavery and oppression. As the novel progresses, Horace's inner conflict intensifies, and chilling accounts of possessions and exorcisms abound with tragic tales of desperation and despair. In the climax, a turbulent night of confessions and confrontations reveals secrets long hidden, leading to a powerful and poignant climax. The novel ends with a haunting conclusion, challenging the reader to ponder the concept of evil and the complexities of human nature.
