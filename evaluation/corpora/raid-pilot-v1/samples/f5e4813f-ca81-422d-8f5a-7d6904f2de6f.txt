The Carpet People is a wonderful, enthralling novel, taking the reader into the magical world of a living-room carpet. The story revolves round two brothers, Spiro and Tam, who belong to a tiny community, called the “Carpet People.” This community consists of many tribes and creatures that live together in the carpet’s intricate landscape. However, the peace and harmony of this community is shattered by a mysterious force called the Fray, which destroys the villages of the tribes and pits tribe against tribe in a fight for survival. The brothers, together with their friends, undertake a dangerous quest to destroy this mysterious force, in which they encounter hostile tribes, dangerous carpet-creatures and, most dangerous of all, the world above the carpet, inhabited by giants—humans. The story also examines the relationship between brothers, with its own complexities and quirks, while making some profound observations about society, human nature, family ties and the dangers of conflict. This is a magical, adventure-filled novel with a strong element of fantasy and the deepest insights into the nature of community, survival and the savage instincts of man. The “Carpet People” must be united to defeat the Fray, and through their unity they discover the power of courage, unity and the spirit of the age.
