The purpose of this paper is to develop a novel approach to large-scale image segmentation by distributed clustering. It addresses the challenges of processing high-resolution images that are consuming significant computational resources and time. The proposed method exploits the power of distributed computing to produce high-quality image segmentation. This paper introduces a new clustering method that is capable of handling large datasets and high-dimensional feature spaces. It is evaluated on several large-scale image datasets and compared to the existing methods. The results show that the use of clustering methods in large-scale image segmentation can significantly improve the performance and efficiency of image segmentation. This work has important applications in the fields of image processing, machine learning, and big data.
