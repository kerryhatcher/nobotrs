This article presents a detailed study on the development of a robust general medical image segmentation model. It focuses on the challenges of variability in medical images due to differences in imaging procedures, anatomical differences, and the presence of diseases. The proposed model is based on the combination of machine learning and deep learning. The model has been tested on various medical image datasets and has been shown to significantly improve over existing methods. This research has the potential to help medical imaging improve diagnostic accuracy and treatment planning.
