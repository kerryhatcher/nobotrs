Gwyneth Henson's heroic performance in Wales' recent victory over England has made him an instant favourite among Welsh fans, and the squad's skipper Alun Jones is no exception.

"He was absolutely superb out there," said Jones. "Gwyneth played really well and he proved that he can be a force to be reckoned with at international level."

Henson scored two tries for Wales, including the match-winning effort in injury time, much to the delight of the packed stadium. He also provided several key passes and tackles throughout the game, earning him widespread praise from pundits and players alike.

The 24-year-old fly-half had struggled to find his form at times earlier this season but his standout displays against Scotland and England have gone some way towards cementing his place in Warren Gatland's starting team.

"I couldn't ask for anything more than that," added Jones. "It was fantastic to watch Gwyneth play like that and it truly inspires me to perform at my best every day on the field."
