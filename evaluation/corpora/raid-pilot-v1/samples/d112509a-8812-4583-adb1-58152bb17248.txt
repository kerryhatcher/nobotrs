Former Liverpool coach, Rafal Bentek, defended Jerzy Dudek and warned the media against the indictment of the Polish goalkeeper for the recent series of blunders. Dudek, whose blunders in the last matches are on the rise, found a supporter in Bentek. The Spanish coach, who led Liverpool to the Champions League title in 2005 with Dudek as the first goalkeeper, insists that the fault cannot be only with the goalkeeper. Bentek said at a press conference: - In football it is very easy to point at one player, especially a goalkeeper. But we win as a team and we lose as a team. Jerzy is a great goalkeeper, he has already proven it many times. Bentek added: - We must not point the finger of blame only at Jerzy Dudek. He made some mistakes, but so did the rest of the team. At this time, you have to support him and not defame him. - Bentek referred to the pressures of goalkeepers, saying: - We forget that many times in a match they save us. - The support of the Spanish coach is appreciated by Dudek, who does not respond to the criticism. It is uncertain whether Bentek's words will have any effect on the goalkeeper's game, but they certainly remind that football is a team game and that the guilt cannot be one player.
