As long as you are happy,
My heart will sing with glee,
For in your joy, I find my own,
And all worries set me free.

Whether it's sunshine or rain,
In every moment by your side,
I am content to bask in your smile,
And let life's journey take its stride.

Through thick and thin, through highs and lows,
Our bond remains unbroken strong,
For in your happiness, I find my peace,
And that's where my heart belongs.

So go forth and live each day,
With love and laughter in your soul,
And know that as long as you are happy,
You make this world a little more whole.
