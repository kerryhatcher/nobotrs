In recent weeks Liverpool captain Steven Gerrard has publicly declared that he’s not for one minute considering leaving Anfield. He has won the Champions League, the FA Cup and the League Cup. Despite his age, he remains an important part of the Liverpool squad and his performances this season have been an important factor in the club maintaining their place in the top flight.
