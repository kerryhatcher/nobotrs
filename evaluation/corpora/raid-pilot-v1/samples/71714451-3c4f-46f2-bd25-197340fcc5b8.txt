Barcelona is reportedly looking to sign former England forward Ashley Young from Manchester United, according to sources in Spain.

The 30-year-old winger is said to have had talks with new Barcelona coach Ronald Koeman over his future at the club. It was also revealed that Young's agent spoke with Manchester United to see if there were any potential moves for him.

Young has been playing well this season and he has scored four goals already for the Red Devils. However, it still uncertain whether United will be able to hold on to him given their financial situation.
