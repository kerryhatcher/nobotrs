It's possible that the roughness in your mouth could be a side effect of the treatment for thrush or it could be a sign of a different oral health issue. The most common treatment for thrush is antifungal medication, which can be taken orally or applied topically.

Geographic tongue is a benign (non-cancerous) condition that causes patches of redness and smoothness on the top and sides of the tongue. These patches can have a rough or sandpaper-like texture. The cause of geographic tongue is unknown, but it is thought to be related to changes in the way the tongue grows and sheds its outer layer of skin.
