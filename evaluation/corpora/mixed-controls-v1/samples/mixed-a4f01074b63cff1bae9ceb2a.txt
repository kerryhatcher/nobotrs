It allows employees to contribute a portion of their salary on a tax-deferred basis, meaning that the contributions are made with pre-tax dollars and are not subject to income tax until they are withdrawn in retirement. Employers may also contribute to their employees' 401(k) accounts, typically in the form of matching contributions.A SEP-IRA (Simplified Employee Pension Individual Retirement Account) is a type of retirement savings account that is available to self-employed individuals and small business owners. It works similarly to a traditional IRA, in that contributions are made on a tax-deferred basis and can potentially be deducted from your income tax return. However, unlike a traditional IRA, a SEP-IRA allows for much higher contribution limits, making it a good option for self-employed individuals who want to save a significant amount for retirement.It's important to carefully consider the terms of each retirement savings account, including the contribution limits, investment options, and fees, in order to choose the options that best meet your needs.
